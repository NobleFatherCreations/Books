## Relevant record the volume does not mention
- **June 2018 youth-interview guidelines** (F10). The page argues the interview structure at length and never records the change the reform campaign won. A hostile reader will cite it first.
- **Rebaptisms of Gileadi (1996) and Hanks (2012)** (F9). The page's "no standing was ever restored" line is contradicted by the record.
- **SEC settlement terms**: the Church and Ensign Peak settled without admitting or denying the findings (F1). §3 calls the SEC order proof of "deliberate concealment"; the SEC's own words ("obscure") support concealment, but the page does not mention the no-admission basis.
