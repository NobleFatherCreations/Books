# Fact-check — Islam (checked 2026-09-30)

Scope of this pass: the time-sensitive and load-bearing claims named in the brief (office-holders, "as of" figures, membership and money in §1/§7/§9), the §25 help lines, and the claims in §1/§3/§14/§20/§23 most exposed to hostile review. The other claims keep the sourcing recorded in §26 and were not re-read this pass. This volume is the umbrella for Sunni Islam, Shia Islam, Ahmadiyya and Dawoodi Bohra; cross-volume differences found along the way are listed at the end.

Classes: VERIFIED · VERIFIED-UPDATED · DISCREPANT · OVERSTATED · UNVERIFIABLE · NOT CHECKED. Nothing classed DISCREPANT or OVERSTATED has been changed in the text; each waits for a decision in `docs/sacred-divide-v5/discrepancies/islam.md`.

## Cross-volume differences noticed (not edited here)
- **X1 · NU.** Sunni Islam §4, §6 and §21 carry the same "contested elections" claim (F16).
- **X2 · MWN helpline.** Ahmadiyya §25 and its source list carry the same wrong number (F17). The number lives in `content/sacred-divide/additions/islam.md`, `content/sacred-divide/sources/islam.md`, `content/sacred-divide/additions/ahmadiyya.md` and `content/sacred-divide/new-traditions/ahmadiyya.md`.
- **X3 · Diyanet.** Sunni Islam gives 2023 as ₺35.9bn; this volume's "up 151%" follows Duvar's ₺36.47bn base (F11).
- **X4 · al-Azhar.** Sunni Islam says el-Tayeb was "appointed by the president in 2010"; this volume's chosen-by row describes only the post-2012 procedure (F24).
- **X5 · Khums.** Loop 1 names khums, which appears nowhere else in this volume; it belongs to the Shia Islam volume. It is glossed here and left in place.

## Relevant record the volume does not mention
- Morocco's pending family-code revision (Dec 2024 royal session: exemption floor raised to 17; bill still before Parliament in March 2026). §8, §20 and the Morocco card all describe the 2004 code as current, which is true, but a reader will expect the pending change.
- NU's 2025–26 leadership crisis and the switch to AHWA selection (F16).
