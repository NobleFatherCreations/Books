## Internal consistency

- **§1 grade tally equals the §12 chips.** Cultural 19 (1, 2, 8–13, 16–25, 29), Taught 5 (3–7), Codified 4 (14, 15, 26, 27), Documented 2 (28, 30). "6 of 30 sourced" matches the six *(sourced)* marks (15, 21, 22, 26, 28, 30). Consistent.
- **Repeated figures.** 25 million lari, 890,000 lari, ~10,000 clergy, 1,200+ signatories, 35,000 roubles, 2009, 1991, 5 January 2019 and 1924 are identical wherever they recur. "~220–260 million" (§1, §4, §7) and "about 260 million" (§6) are consistent with each other but see F2.
- **§19 `tactics:` numbers** all exist in §12. Fit: Burdin (17, 28, 30) fits. Uminsky (28, 29, 21): 28 fits; 29 Replacement and 21 Learned Helplessness describe lay converts and do not fit a priest's defrocking. Chapnin (17, 28) fits. Kačavenda (30, 18) fits. Vatopedi (26, 30): 26 Financial Control describes extraction from members through spiritual obligation; a land exchange with the state does not fit it. Listed in DISCREPANCIES.
- **§20 vs §1 scorecard.** §20 says the removal "mechanism exists on paper"; the scorecard grades Removal **N** ("not established from any public source"). Listed.
- **§13 Loop 6 summary** ("a transfer to a monastery") has no supporting record anywhere in the volume. Listed.
