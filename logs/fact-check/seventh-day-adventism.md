# Fact-check — Seventh-day Adventism (checked 2026-09-30)

Scope of this pass: the time-sensitive and load-bearing claims named in the brief (office-holders, membership, money), the §25 help lines, and the claims in §1, §3, §5, §7, §14, §20 and §22 most exposed to hostile review. The other claims keep the sourcing recorded in §26 and were not re-read this pass.

Classes: VERIFIED · VERIFIED-UPDATED · DISCREPANT · OVERSTATED · UNVERIFIABLE · NOT CHECKED. Nothing classed DISCREPANT or OVERSTATED has been changed in the text; each waits for a decision in `docs/sacred-divide-v5/discrepancies/seventh-day-adventism.md`.
