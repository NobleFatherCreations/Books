# Fact-check — New Age / Spiritual-but-Organized Movements (checked 2026-10-03)

Scope of this pass: the time-sensitive and load-bearing claims in sections 1, 3, 5, 7, 8, 9, 11, 14, 16, 19, 20, 21 and 22, every §25 help line, and the claims a hostile reader would test first. Source entries in §26 were opened where the publisher allowed it; Britannica, the Department of Justice page and RAINN's own site refused automated access, so those were checked through search summaries and are marked. Claims classed UNVERIFIABLE have no source in §26 and none was found for the claim as worded.

Classes: VERIFIED · VERIFIED-UPDATED · DISCREPANT · OVERSTATED · UNVERIFIABLE · NOT CHECKED. Nothing classed DISCREPANT, OVERSTATED or UNVERIFIABLE has been softened or removed in the text; each waits for a decision in `docs/sacred-divide-v5/discrepancies/new-age.md`.

New Age is a diffuse milieu, not an organization. Claims that treat it as one body with owners, membership, doctrine or finances are flagged below (F13, F14, F21).
