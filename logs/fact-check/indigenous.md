## Internal consistency
- Repeated figures: 973 / 417 / 74 / $23.3bn / $25m / under $4m / $30m / 3,200 agree across sections, except "more than 3,200" (§5) against "about 3,200" (§8).
- §1 grade tally equals the §12 chips (F20).
- "Six of the 30 sourced" equals the six "(sourced)" markers.
- The word "genocide" appears in two forms: "cultural genocide" (TRC, §5, §8) and "people who survived genocide" (§3 and §15 cost tables). The MMIWG inquiry uses "genocide" (retrieved); other bodies' use of the term was not retrieved; the TRC's finding is "cultural genocide". Only the first form is tied to a source on the page.
