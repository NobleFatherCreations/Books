# Fact-check — Scientology (checked 2026-10-03)

Scope of this pass: the time-sensitive and load-bearing claims named in the brief (current leadership, the Masterson case, the Rinder and Remini litigation, the Haney and Rathbun cases, membership counts, tax and charity status in the United States, the United Kingdom, Germany, France and Belgium), every §25 help line, and the contested claims a hostile reader would attack first. This is a litigation-sensitive volume, so every contested claim was also checked for whether it is phrased as an allegation, a finding or a court ruling, and for whether it is attributed to the right source. The other claims keep the sourcing recorded in §26 and were not re-read this pass.

Classes: VERIFIED · VERIFIED-UPDATED · DISCREPANT · OVERSTATED · UNVERIFIABLE · NOT CHECKED. Nothing classed DISCREPANT or OVERSTATED has been changed in the text; each waits for a decision in `docs/sacred-divide-v5/discrepancies/scientology.md`.

## Internal consistency
- §1 grade tallies (Codified 27, Documented 3) match the §12 chips: Documented are techniques 11, 17 and 18; the other 27 are Codified. "12 of 30 sourced" matches the twelve *(sourced)* marks (techniques 3, 5, 11, 12, 14, 17, 18, 19, 21, 24, 26, 28).
- Repeated figures agree throughout: 1,854 / about 1,700 / 315; $12.5 million; €600,000; about 2,200 suits; eleven convictions; 1987; 30 years to life.
- The §19 `tactics:` numbers all exist (11, 17 / 30, 16, 17 / 26, 1 / 30, 18). Fit: 11 (Projection) for Snow White, and 30 (Plausible Deniability) for Masterson and for *Bixler*, are poor matches; see the discrepancies list.
- "Thirty-eight years" (§5) and "forty years" (§14) are both approximate and neither matches the 39 years since 1987.
- The §1 scorecard grades Removal **N**, and §20 says the page would change if governance were published. These agree.

## Relevant record the volume does not mention
- *Remini v. CSI* (Court of Appeal ruling, 31 July 2026), *Haney* (religious arbitration, ongoing), *Baxter / Jane Doe 1* (trafficking suit, stayed pending arbitration), *Rathbun* (2015 Texas appellate ruling on harassment of a non-member): none appears. They are listed in `docs/sacred-divide-v5/proposals/scientology.md`.
- Belgium (2016 dismissal), the United Kingdom (1999 refusal and 2013 *Hodkin* ruling) and the Netherlands (2022 public-benefit status) are all absent. Leaving out the cases that went the church's way invites a charge of selection.
- The church's own position on contested points (that fair game was cancelled in 1968; that disconnection is described as voluntary; that charges are described as donations; that refunds are offered on request; these positions were not fetched this pass and should be sourced to the church's own statements before use) is not stated beside the claims it answers, apart from the "official denial" column in §15.
