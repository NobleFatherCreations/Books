**After**

> ::: card
> #### 1 · Money to Doctrine to Money
>
> Growth funds broadcast capacity, and broadcast capacity produces more growth. In Brazil, where the Universal Church's founder owns a national television network, the loop is visible.
>
> **How it runs.**
>
> 1. The ministry teaches that money given to it is a seed that produces a harvest (sections 1 and 9).
> 2. The seed offering is taken during the sermon that teaches seed offerings, and the teaching is recorded and broadcast (section 9; technique 3).
> 3. Offerings, first fruits, building funds and partnership tiers flow to a ministry that publishes no accounts (section 9; technique 26).
> 4. The money buys media. The largest ministries own networks, publishing houses and streaming platforms, and in Brazil the Universal Church's founder, Edir Macedo, owns Record TV (sections 7 and 11).
> 5. The broadcasts carry the appeal to a larger audience, and the giving grows (sections 9 and 11).
>
> **Techniques that feed it.** [3 · Future Faking](#t-3), [10 · Strategic Ambiguity](#t-10), [26 · Financial Control](#t-26), [27 · Manufactured Crisis](#t-27).
>
> **Why it closes.** The doctrine and the collection are the same act. The sermon that teaches seed faith ends in the seed offering, and the offering pays for the platform that preaches the next sermon. A member's information about the church is frequently produced by the church (section 11), so the loop also controls what the giver hears about it.
>
> **Where it could be broken, and by whom.** It weakens wherever the money becomes visible. Churches registered as charities in the United Kingdom file public accounts, as Hillsong Church London does (section 9), and section 20 names audited accounts, including founder compensation and title deeds, as the change that would revise this page's central financial findings. A member can ask for the accounts before giving (section 23). This paragraph is analysis, not a documented finding.
>
> **An example from this page.** Section 14 records that the offering appeal, the partnership tiers and the seed-faith teaching are recorded, broadcast and archived by the ministries themselves.

>
> Junior pastors under covering cannot criticise senior ones without losing their platform, so the people best placed to reform the movement are the people least able to speak.
