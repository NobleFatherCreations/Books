# THE REPAIR
### For the person on the other side of it
*48 chapters · 7 movements · Noble Father Creations*

---

## THE BRIEF

**Audience.** Someone who has read a tactic list — here, or elsewhere — and recognised themselves. They are not court-mandated. Nobody sent them. They arrived on their own, which is rare and which is the only thing currently in their favour.

**Objective.** That the people around this reader have a different year than the last one. Nothing else. Not the reader's peace, not their self-image, not their forgiveness.

**The success condition.** Behaviour change, measured over months, by the people affected. Not insight. Not remorse. Not a good conversation.

**The failure condition — and it is the likely one.** The book produces *relief*. A reader finishes a chapter, feels understood, feels better, and does nothing. Relief is the enemy of this entire work and must be treated as a symptom throughout.

**The structural risk.** This book can be used as a shield: *I'm reading a book about it, I'm doing the work.* Chapters 2, 5, and 47 exist for this and must not be softened.

**The two exits the book must block.**
- **"I'm a monster."** An identity. Static. Nothing to do. Self-focused, and secretly comfortable.
- **"I'm actually fine."** An explanation. Complete. Nothing to do. Also secretly comfortable.
Both end the work while feeling like doing it. The book's position is neither: *you are a person who did specific things, repeatedly, that harmed specific people.* That is a record, not an identity. Records can change forward and not backward.

**Voice.** The house voice, further restrained. No warmth that could be mistaken for absolution. No severity that produces defensiveness. Closest register: a very good doctor delivering a serious diagnosis — factual, unhurried, not cruel, not consoling. Second person throughout. No exclamation marks. No performed sympathy in either direction.

**Standing rule for every chapter.** If a paragraph would make the reader feel better about themselves without changing what they will do on Thursday, cut it.
