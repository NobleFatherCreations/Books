Sector blueprint

This version comes out of five rounds of audit and steelman: data checks, phone screenshots of today's build, an independent critic on v1, a second critic on v2, and a verdict on every idea. 2 · The model

Two sites, three kinds of page, one ID

Pattern Decoder stays its own site and doesn't change. The sector site links out to its existing general entries, the same way a book cites a dictionary.

Tactic

335 per sector · one move

What does this move look like here, at every size? The definition, the author's three examples (one person, an organization, the whole industry), and every pattern it appears in.

/fractal2/<sector>/tactic/<tactic>

chain step · "step 4 of 6 in The Debt Prison". Both pages link to each other.

Pattern

153 per sector · moves used together, in order

How do the moves combine, and how do I break the chain? The author's full script, with a 5-minute quick read.

/fractal2/<sector>/<pattern>

situation and category · where it happens, what kind of harm

Ways in

home, 12 situations, 10 categories, 8 stages

Where do I start? Pages that order the tactics and patterns by what a reader already knows.

/fractal2/<sector>/in/<situation> · /patterns/<category> · /tactics/<stage>

Pattern Decoder

separate site · unchanged

The general entry for each tactic (signs, questions to ask yourself, what you can do, words you could use) and the umbrella entries (Gaslighting, FOG, DARVO). Reached by one plainly labeled link out, by standard ID. Its pages don't link back, and that's fine: the browser's back button returns.

4 · The frame

Where am I, and how do I get out

Today's phone frame works, so v2 keeps it and fixes what overlaps.

Phone

  • Top: Fractal² (to the hub) · Search · Leave. Leave is at least 44 px, always top right, never in a menu, and uses location.replace (kept). Switching sector moves to the sector home and the footer: it's rare, and it took a third "home" button off the bar.
  • Bottom tab bar: Home · Now (in-it-now) · Patterns · Tactics · House. The most urgent path is always one tap away; situations live on Home. THE HOUSE slot only opens the shared drawer, whose markup stays unchanged (decision D13), so it never floats over text again. Without JavaScript the slot is a link to the House list in the footer. On desktop the same five sit in the top bar. At 200% text the labels drop to icons with accessible names.
  • Breadcrumb: one link to the parent ("‹ Hook"). The full trail shows from 768 px up.
  • Where am I: the current tab is lit, and a section label sits above every title ("Tactic · Hook", "Pattern · Exit Prevention").

Every page stands alone

  • One orientation line under every title: "One of 335 moves people use to control others in music and entertainment." There's a quiet link to "If it's happening now".
  • Title tag: "Obligation Contracts: how it's used in the music industry". Breadcrumb and term markup.
  • The safety line in the footer of every page, verbatim (kept).
  • Filters use replaceState, so taps leave no history trail on a watched phone (kept). The in-it-now page explains private browsing and clearing history.

5 · Templates

Six templates, in order of importance

Sketches show order and weight at phone width, not the visual design. The number rule for every page: a badged numeral always means a chain step. Every other number is a count with its noun ("57 patterns", "5-minute read"). Stages are never numbered.

/fractal2/<sector>/tactic/<tactic> · 335

Tactic page

  1. Section label "Tactic · Hook" (stage by name and color), name, the orientation line, then one safety line with a link to "If it's happening now". Then the definition and also called. Search visitors land here more than anywhere, so the safety line goes here as well as on pattern pages.
  2. At every size. All three examples stacked, always visible, under plain labels: One person, An organization, The whole industry. The author's terms (Individual, Institutional, Civilizational) sit small beside them. A three-step scale mark is the visual signature. Each has an anchor (#institutional), and each label links to its scale page. On desktop they sit side by side.
  3. The general guide, right after the examples. One card: "Signs, questions to ask yourself, what you can do and words you could use are in Pattern Decoder, a separate site. Your back button returns here."
  4. Used in these patterns. Each as "step 4 of 6 in The Debt Prison", grouped by category. If none: "Not part of a mapped pattern in this sector."
  5. Near it. Previous and next in its stage, and moves that share its patterns. Later: "Also in: Corporate".

Cut from today's page: the full-screen stage ring. Moved up: the Pattern Decoder link, from the last block to the second.

/fractal2/<sector>/<pattern> · 153

Pattern page

  1. Safety note first, in every mode (kept).
  2. Section label "Pattern · Exit Prevention", with "‹ previous" and "next ›" in the category beside it, each at least 44 px. They follow the category page's own order (ten to read first, then shortest). Each category becomes a line you can walk.
  3. The moves, in order. A numbered list is the main control. Each step shows its number, name, stage name and color, the one-line definition, and a link. Umbrella names say "general pattern · Pattern Decoder, separate site". Any link still unmapped is plain text, labeled.
  4. The ring with the path drawn on it, as an illustration: below the list on phones, beside it on desktop. Screen readers skip it, and it has no tap targets of its own.
  5. Quick read · The sequence · Full (kept).
  6. After the text: next in this category, patterns that share its moves, and the method's label meanings.

Back to the pattern: when a reader arrives at a tactic page from a pattern on this site, a "← back to The Debt Prison" chip appears. It reads the referrer, so shared links stay clean. Without JavaScript the browser's back button does the same.

8 · Visual rules

What "visually optimized" means here, so it can be checked

The look is chosen in step 6 by comps. These rules hold whatever look wins, and each one is a gate.

Reading first

A 3,000-word pattern is the main event. Body text 18–19 px on phones, about 62–68 characters per line, contrast of 4.5:1 or more measured on rendered pixels. Two typefaces at most.

Color is data, never decoration

The eight stage colors from your artwork appear only beside a stage name, so color-blind readers lose nothing. There's one accent, for actions.

One number system

A badged numeral is always a chain step. Any other number carries its noun. Stages are never numbered. The gate reads markup classes, not digits.

No specks

No graphic with marks smaller than 6 px or labels under 12 px. Tactic marks on the ring appear only on the map. Elsewhere the ring is its eight stage segments.

Nothing floats over text

Fixed bars reserve their space. THE HOUSE moves into the tab bar. A gate fails any overlap between fixed elements and text at 375, 390 and 1440 px.

Page length budgets

At 100% text, any page over 25 phone screens fails, except full patterns and scale pages, which have jump lists. The A–Z lists (153 patterns, 335 tactics) sit behind letter jump links. Today's library is 49 screens.

Large text holds

At 200% text size on 390 px, nothing clips or overlaps and chips wrap cleanly.

First screen per template

At 390 × 664 (after the bars): home shows the safety line and all four doors; tactic shows the label, name, safety line, definition and the start of the first example; pattern shows the safety note, title and step 1.

Type, grid and targets

A type scale of six steps at most, the 8 px grid from the house rules, visible focus everywhere, and 44 px targets for every control, not only Leave.

Both grounds, every mode

Stage colors at 3:1 or better as marks and 4.5:1 where used as text, on both the light and dark grounds. Follows the system color scheme; forced colors keep every label.

Weight and print

Fonts load once from the shared folder (decision D9), not inlined into all 530 pages. A budget per page type. Patterns print as the quick read plus safety note and steps, with addresses shown.

Calm motion

Motion only to show change (mode switch, filter results). Nothing autoplays. Reduced motion shows the same page without movement.
