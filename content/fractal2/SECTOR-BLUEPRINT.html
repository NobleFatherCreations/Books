Fractal² · sector sites · plan only, nothing built

Sector blueprint

This is how every sector site gets rebuilt from the ground up. It covers how people read the 335 tactics at three scales, how each tactic hands off to Pattern Decoder for the general breakdown, how they move through the patterns by category, and the order I would build it in, with the skills, servers and agents for each step. It is drawn from what exists in Entertainment & Music today and written so the same build works for all thirty sectors.

4 · Templates

Seven page types, in reading order

The sketches are layout only, at phone width, to show order and weight. The look comes in step 5 of the build.

/fractal2/<sector>/tactic/<tactic> · 335

Tactic page: the zoom

The most important template, because it's where the three scales live. One tactic, shown at three sizes, with the general guide one tap away.

  1. Stage chip (number, name, color), name, definition, "also called".
  2. The zoom. A three-step control (Individual · Institutional · Civilizational) over the author's three examples. It works like a map's scale ruler: the stage ring in the header grows with each step. On desktop all three show side by side, and each scale has its own link (#institutional).
  3. Recognize it and respond. One clear card leading to the Pattern Decoder guide. It names what the reader will find there (signs, questions to ask yourself, what you can do, words you could use) without copying the text.
  4. In these patterns. Each pattern as a small chain on the ring, this tactic lit, "step 3 of 6", grouped by category.
  5. Near it. Previous and next in the stage, tactics that share its patterns, and the same tactic in other sectors once they exist.

In: stage chapter, scale pages, every pattern chain, atlas, search, Pattern Decoder "in the sectors".
Out: Pattern Decoder, its patterns, its stage, its scale column.

/fractal2/<sector>/scale/<scale> · 3

Scale pages: read the sector at one size

All 335 examples at one scale, grouped by stage, each linking to its tactic. Read the Individual page and you see the sector as one person lives it. Read Civilizational and you see the whole industry's unwritten rules. This is the fractal argument as a reading experience, and it's new.

  1. A one-line explanation of the scale and a ruler showing where it sits in the zoom.
  2. Eight stage bands. Each opens to that stage's examples at this scale (name, example, link).
  3. A switch to the same stage at the next scale up or down.

In: atlas, tactic zoom control, sector home. Out: 335 tactic pages, the other two scales.

/fractal2/<sector>/tactics · 1, plus 8 stage chapters

Tactic atlas

The way into all 335. The 8×3 grid from section 2 works as a control: tap a stage for its chapter, a scale for its page, or a cell to see that stage at that scale. Below it is the full list, with type-to-narrow and a filter for "in a pattern" or "not yet in a pattern".

Stage chapters keep every tactic in full (definition and all three examples) for people who read straight through. Each entry links to its tactic page.

In: top bar, sector home ring. Out: stages, scales, tactics.

/fractal2/<sector>/patterns · 1

Pattern library

All 153, filtered by what a reader actually knows. Each card shows the chain drawn on a small ring, so the shape is visible before reading.

  1. Browse by: Category (10) · Situation (12) · Starts at stage · Contains a tactic · Length (4–11 steps) · Has a safety note (41) · Native or adapted to this sector.
  2. A live count ("47 of 153"). The filter state goes in the address so a view can be shared, and nothing is stored.
  3. Sort: the ten that matter first, then A–Z, shortest first, or longest.

In: top bar, sector home, category and situation pages. Out: 153 patterns, 10 categories.

/fractal2/<sector>/patterns/<category> · 10

Category page

Today it's a list. It becomes a page that explains what the patterns in it have in common, worked out from the data rather than written new.

  1. The author's category introduction, as written.
  2. Where they live on the ring: the stages this category's patterns use most, drawn as one stacked ring.
  3. The tactics they share: the tactics that recur across this category, linked.
  4. The patterns, ordered by the ten-that-matter rank, then shortest first, so the easiest way in comes first.
  5. Previous and next category.

In: library filter, pattern header, sector home. Out: its patterns, shared tactics.

/fractal2/<sector>/<pattern> · 153

Pattern page

The reading modes, the safety-first rule and the author's text all stay. What changes is the chain at the top.

  1. Safety note first, in every mode, always.
  2. The chain on the ring. Steps numbered in order, each bead a stage color with the stage number as text. Tap a bead to see that tactic's definition and its example at the scale you're reading; tap again to open the tactic page.
  3. Umbrella links (Gaslighting, FOG, DARVO) drawn as hollow beads labeled "general pattern, Pattern Decoder", so leaving the sector is never a surprise.
  4. Quick · Sequence · Full, as today.
  5. After the text: more in this category, patterns that share its tactics, this pattern in other sectors.

In: library, category, tactic pages, map, search, the ten. Out: every tactic in the chain, Pattern Decoder umbrellas, category.

/fractal2/<sector> · 1

Sector home

It opens on the ring of 335: eight stage arcs with every tactic as a mark. Tapping an arc opens that stage. Below the ring come "Where are you right now?" (kept), the ten that matter (kept), and three large doors: Tactics, Patterns, Map. Each door gives its count and one sentence on what's inside.

In: the hub's Enter button, the sector switcher, search engines. Out: every section.

5 · The Pattern Decoder bridge

What lives where, and how people cross

The rule is simple: the sector shows the tactic in this world, and Pattern Decoder tells you what to do about it anywhere. Neither copies the other.

Question a reader hasAnswered byWhy there
What is this tactic, in general?Pattern Decoder /playbook/<tactic>The same answer in every sector. Written once, kept once.
Is this what's happening to me? What can I do? What can I say?Pattern DecoderSigns, look-alikes, questions to ask yourself, steps and words already exist there, reviewed for safety.
What does it look like in music, at a label, across the industry?Sector tactic pageThe author's three examples are specific to the sector.
How does it combine with other tactics here?Pattern pagesChains are specific to the sector.
What is Gaslighting, FOG or DARVO?Pattern Decoder umbrella pagesThey're bundles of tactics rather than single ones, so they aren't sector tactics.
Where else does this tactic show up?Pattern Decoder, new "In the sectors" blockPattern Decoder becomes the cross-sector center: one tactic, every sector that has it.

Sector to Pattern Decoder

  • Every tactic page: the "Recognize it and respond" card, second section, not a footnote.
  • Every umbrella bead in a chain, marked as a general pattern.
  • Links resolve by standard ID only, and the build fails if a target page is missing.
  • The link says where it goes: "Pattern Decoder, the general guide", so nobody is surprised by the change of site.

Pattern Decoder to the sectors

  • New block on each Pattern Decoder tactic page: "In the sectors", listing each built sector with its tactic page link (Entertainment today, Corporate next).
  • Built from the sector manifests, so it grows on its own as sectors open.
  • This changes your live /playbook. It's a separate, small release that needs your yes.
