# STORY-DECISION: the v8 intro script (2026-11-11)

## 1. Brief (owner's words, condensed, 2026-11-11)
Combinations are not narrated as an example: they are the **antagonist, the danger**, described by how they destroy lives, jobs and sense of self; the words are a **warning**. Told like a **storyteller**; the owner's own experiences are removed. Talk about the fractal and the **scope of the new list**; introduce the combinations as **the most important map**: it sheds light on the worst we have experienced and is **the key to healing and breaking free**. **Cinematic, epic-fantasy prose**; the **spiritual and psychological weaving**: the cycle as **inversions of divine principles**, with the **religious words for it**. Use the earlier audit for guardrails; this is **not a list of facts**, it sets up the work that leads to the facts and shows its **scope, size and importance**. Camera arc: black circle -> slow zoom to the full circle fitting the screen -> pause -> zoom out to the larger map with words appearing -> at the descent, zoom back in through the words to black -> a spark of light: "your original frequency".

## 5. Choice: D as the spine, with three deliberate borrowings
- **Spine D (cartographer's tale):** the narrator is the one who looked closer; scope, importance and the key come from the map itself. Matches the owner's camera arc.
- **From A:** the combinations are introduced as the enemy's *method* ("the soldiers" and "the general"), and described by what they do to a person's life, work and mind; but the enemy is a *structure*, never people (guards against the audit's accusation problem).
- **From C:** the inversion list (grace becomes flattery, covenant becomes a debt, correction becomes shame, mystery becomes confusion, sanctuary becomes isolation, offering becomes extraction, release becomes the discard, rebirth becomes replacement) and the religious words (the snare, the veil, the false god, the captivity, the counterfeit). Offered as "one person's sight", with "to my eye", and an explicit "I do not ask you to share my faith".
- **From B:** the *order* idea only ("each step removes the defense you would have used against the next"), not the vignette. Rejected: the musician scene, the owner's own account, the second-person descent.
- **Rejected whole:** E (contested claims, off brief).

## 6. Pacing rules adopted for the page (from the owner's design notes)
One or two sentences per scene; each scene is a runway of scroll with the words appearing one by one, held, then dissolving; several scenes have no words at all (the picture speaks). Type is large. The script carries the camera: each scene states where the camera is and where it goes.
