## Fractal²

**Cut the strings.**

The Fractal showed one thing: the manipulation you met in one person is the same machine running in your workplace, your church, your school, and the culture around them. Thirty techniques. Thirty sectors. One cycle, repeating in every building. [Watch The Fractal →](/fractal)

Fractal² goes inside the machine.

Every sector gets its own field guide. Three hundred and thirty-five tactics, each shown at three altitudes: in one person's life, inside an institution, and across a whole culture. Then the part almost nobody maps: how tactics combine. Every pattern is told as a full story, then taken apart. The tactics in the order they work. Why that order matters. How to tell whether you are inside it. What breaks the chain.

Every string someone holds over another person is made of something: a debt, a secret, a fear, a promise, a door they control. This work names what each string is made of, so it can be cut.

### What it is for

Manipulation survives on one thing: the person inside it can't name what is happening. Once you can name it, the machine loses the dark it needs.

My aim is simple and enormous: to take the machine of manipulation apart, piece by piece, in public, sector by sector, until there is nowhere left for it to hide. For the people still inside it. For the people who got out and never had words for what happened. For the people who will be targeted next, so they see it coming.

This is recognition and safe routing, never a playbook. Patterns describe situations, not people. Anyone in danger is pointed to real help first.
