# Fractal² intro: the script to speak (v15, final)

Read it exactly as written. Blank lines are breaths. Lines in CAPS-free bold are the four anchor words for the music.

## 1. The question

It started with a question.

I was not looking for a pattern.

I was looking for a way out.

Why do I keep living this same cycle of narcissistic abuse, wherever I go in life?

A different room. A different face. The same ending.

I have experienced manipulation techniques at the highest level, and they have cost me much, several times in my life. The most recent, in the recent past, attempted to take everything from me and from someone I loved.

For years I gave the only answer I had.

It must be me.

Then one day I asked it the other way.

What if it isn't me?

## 2. One map

So I did the one thing I knew how to do. I wrote it down.

I took one sector of life and mapped the cycle inside it.

It came in eight stages. Always in the same order.

Idealize. Hook. Devalue. Confuse. Isolate. Extract. Discard. Replace.

Thirty techniques carried a person from each stage to the next.

I thought I had found the thing that hurt me.

I had found one room in a very large house.

## 3. Thirty

I looked at a second sector. It was there.

A third. There.

Religion. Politics. Media. Education. Medicine. Money.

Thirty sectors of human life. From the moment you are born to the moment you die.

The same eight stages. The same thirty techniques. The same order.

Two thousand seven hundred examples.

I looked for one sector where the cycle does not run.

I am still looking.

## 4. The mirror

Then the map turned and looked at me.

I found it in myself. In the way I spoke to myself when no one else was in the room.

I found it in my body.

I found it in my family.

In the generations behind them. Handed down, not out of malice. Out of inheritance.

I believe I was carrying things that began in people I never met.

## 5. The architecture

That is when I understood the size of it.

It was never only people.

A person can run this cycle. So can a contract. So can a business. So can an economy.

No one has to mean it.

The pattern wearing a human face.

The pattern wearing a logo.

The pattern wearing the sky.

The person is inside the institution. The institution is inside the civilization. The civilization is inside the pattern.

And the pattern is inside the person.

That is the fractal.

## 6. The inversion

I will tell you what I saw next. I offer it as one man's sight.

The cycle creates nothing. It copies.

Every stage is a divine principle, run backward.

Election becomes Idealize.

Covenant becomes Hook.

Inherent worth becomes Devalue.

Truth becomes Confuse.

Community becomes Isolate.

Abundance becomes Extract.

"I will never leave you" becomes Discard.

The one who cannot be replaced becomes Replace.

And every technique is a sacred faculty, inverted.


"You are loved." And then it extracts.

"You are safe." And then it watches.

"You belong." And then it isolates.

"You are whole." And then it sells you the missing piece.

"Come home." And then it charges rent.

People have called it many things.

The false god.

The devil.

The adversary.

The matrix.

The machine.

The system.

Use the word that fits your own mouth. They all point at the same thing.

Not a being. A pattern, sitting in a chair that was never its own.

A counterfeit always comes second.

Something real was here first.

## 7. The descent

So I stopped trying to climb out.

I went down.

Down through the sectors.

Down through the techniques.

Down through the tactics.

Down through the mirror.

Down through the body.

Down through the chain.

Down through the generations.

All the way down.

I shed it one inversion at a time.

And at the bottom, beneath every lie that was installed as truth, I found it.

Not above me. Not beyond me.

In me. Where it had always been.

## 8. What the traditions call it

Every tradition I know has tried to describe this walk.

The Christian calls it the narrow gate. The kingdom within.

The Buddhist calls it waking up. The end of not-seeing.

The Muslim calls it the lifting of the veils.

The Hindu says, Thou art that.

The Jew speaks of gathering the sparks from the broken vessels.

Many Indigenous peoples call it the remembering. The original instruction.

The Taoist says that on the Way, every day something is dropped.

The mystic calls it the dark night of the soul, and the dawn after it.

And if none of those words are yours, here is what it also is.

A pattern, observed. Traced to where it began. Found in my own body. Followed down my own bloodline. Until the signal showed beneath the noise.

It does not need your belief. Only your attention.

## 9. You're right

If you call it awakening, you're right.

If you call it enlightenment, you're right.

If you call it the end of illusion, you're right.

If you call it healing, you're right.

If you call it deprogramming, you're right.

If you call it liberation, you're right.

If you call it finally seeing clearly for the first time, you're right.

The words are different. The destination is the same.

## 10. The window

Notice what I did not do.

I did not rise above the earth. I did not ascend. I did not leave my body behind.

I descended. Down through everything, into my own core.

The sun did not appear.

It was never gone.

I only moved what had been stacked against the window, one block at a time, and let it through.

My original frequency.

Who I am, underneath all of it.

Pulsing. Quietly. Waiting.

## 11. The map

I came back up with a map.

The first Fractal helped people see.

This one completes the map.

Every technique, opened to show the tactics inside it. Three hundred thirty-five in a single sector.

And the thing I most needed someone to show me. The combinations.

No one is undone by a single move.

The moves come in order, each one taking away the defense against the next.

Two hundred and three of them have names now. The Debt Prison. The Golden Cage. The Trauma Bond Engine.

They are the map of how this cycle has marked every life it has touched.

And read in order, they are the outline for seeing a chain, and breaking it, wherever you meet it.


My healing journey involved forgiving the people who tried to destroy my mind and my heart for money.

Justice comes from breaking every tool they have, so that none of them ever works on anyone again by the time I am done. And from letting the truth of who they are be seen by everyone they care about. Not by exposing them myself. By exposing their tactics to the entire world, so they can never again hide in the shadows and think they are safe.

It will take me a lifetime to finish.

I am giving it away.

To everyone. Wherever this cycle runs.

It started with a question.

Now the map is yours.
