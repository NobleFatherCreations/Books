## The shape

<!--scene 140 z=2.2:2.1-->
A fractal is a pattern that keeps its shape at every scale. Look into a fern, and you find a smaller fern. Look into a river delta, and you find the veins of a leaf.

<!--scene 120 z=2.1:2.0-->
The thing that wounds people is a fractal. It arrives in eight stages, and always in order.

<!--scene 100 z=2.00:1.90 hl=0-->

<!--scene 100 z=1.90:1.80 hl=1-->

<!--scene 100 z=1.80:1.70 hl=2-->

<!--scene 100 z=1.70:1.60 hl=3-->

<!--scene 100 z=1.60:1.50 hl=4-->

<!--scene 100 z=1.50:1.40 hl=5-->

<!--scene 100 z=1.40:1.30 hl=6-->

<!--scene 100 z=1.30:1.20 hl=7-->
