4 · The combo page

The main event: a 22–32 screen read

Your text in your order with your headings. The design's job is to make it easy to start, easy to find your place, and easy to reach the parts you need fast.

Top to bottom

  1. Section label: "Entertainment & Music · Capture & Attachment".
  2. Title: the combo name from your front matter. The C009: and sector suffix in the file's heading move into the label.
  3. Safety Note (read first), when present, directly under the title in a calm, clearly bounded panel. Nothing comes between them.
  4. One meta line: "6 tactics · 14-minute read". Counts only.
  5. Jump row, one line that scrolls sideways, in your headings: Recognition Cue Cards · Chain Breakers · Escalation Triggers · Safer Response Language. A frightened reader reaches the practical sections in one tap, inside the full text.
  6. All sections in your order. Each opens with your heading and generous space above it.
  7. At the end: previous and next in this category; one line to the same combo in the other sector (where it exists); the category's other combos; the Sector Disclaimer, verbatim.

Contents: on desktop, a quiet left rail of your headings with the current one lit. On phones, the Contents button in the top bar opens a sheet of the same headings (native <details>, so it works without JavaScript). A thin reading-progress line sits under the bar.

How each section is set

Three sections get a special treatment. Everything else is careful typography of exactly what you wrote.

SectionTreatment
Recognition Cue CardsSpecial. One card per "SPOT IT — The … Test", with its Ask line and its Concern line kept as two separate lines.
Escalation Triggers — SpecificSpecial. Your three lines (LOW → MODERATE, MODERATE → HIGH, HIGH → URGENT) as three bounded rows with the labels verbatim. "Tier definitions…" opens the Standards' tiers in place.
Safer Response LanguageSpecial. Each line as a quote, with a copy button when JavaScript is on.

Cold OpenBody size, no box, a generous measure. Italic text messages keep their italic.
The Name · DefinitionThe Name set large, as the reveal the story builds to. Definition as body text.

Why This Order (and Why the System Produces This Order)Each bold-led paragraph, then its blockquote indented beneath with no added label. The closing paragraph after the last quote stands alone at full width.
On PurposeBody text, as written.
Individual Level · Institutional Echo · Civilizational EchoBody text under your headings.
Observable IndicatorsYour numbered list, set tight with a narrow number column.
Evidence To Preserve — AdditionsPlain bullets, nothing tickable. The fixed line "Plus the Universal Evidence list…" opens that Standards section in place.

Chain BreakersBullets, each opening with its bold instruction.
Where Each Tactic HappenedA table on desktop. On phones, one stacked row per step with the step number kept visible.

5 · Finding a combo

Home, categories, all combos, search

/fractal2/<sector>

Sector home

  1. Sector title and one approved line on what's here.
  2. The ten categories as doors, in your CAT01–CAT10 order. Each shows the short name, the count, and two or three of its combo titles, so the door is described in your own words.
  3. All combos, Search, and the switch to the other sector.

/fractal2/<sector>/combos/<category> · 10

Category page

  1. Your full category name.
  2. The first paragraph of your introduction, then "Read the full introduction", which expands to the rest of your text.
  3. The combos in your ID order, as cards: title · "6 tactics · 14-minute read" · safety mark.
  4. Previous and next category.

/fractal2/<sector>/combos

All combos and search

Every combo, A–Z with "The" ignored for sorting, and letter jump links. Three filters: Category, Has a safety note, Length (under 15 minutes / longer). Filter taps leave no history trail.

Search looks at plain words a reader might use, not only names: the title, Definition, Observable Indicators, the cue cards' Ask lines, tactic names and tags. So "studio time", "credit" or "Gaslighting" all find the right combos. A hit shows why it matched ("uses Gaslighting", or the matching line).

Without JavaScript: the full A–Z list and the category pages. Search falls back to the A–Z list.

7 · Reading the files

Parser rules, each with a test

The page can only be as true as the parser, so each pattern found in the 291 files gets a test that fails the build.

PatternRule
Front matterRead the title, category, tactics in order, tags, version and status. Never print the raw block.
SectionsTaken from the file, in the file's order. Never from a fixed list: Entertainment's C113 has all four Corporate sections.
Why This OrderPair each bold-led paragraph with the blockquote that follows it. The last paragraph after the final quote is its own block (present in 291 of 291).
Cue cards"SPOT IT — …:", "Ask:", "Concern rises if:" are separate lines joined by single line breaks. Parse them explicitly so they don't run together.
EscalationThree bold-labeled lines plus the closing "Tier definitions…" line.
Corporate tableA four-column table: header row, separator, one row per step.
InlineBold, italic (text messages), bold run-in heads, and the [X]/[Y] fill-in blanks in two combos (shown as visible blanks).
Word countThe words on each page equal the words in its file, excluding front matter. Exact, every combo, every build.

8 · Visual rules

What makes a 20-minute read a pleasure

The look is chosen by comps of one real Entertainment combo and one real Corporate combo. These rules hold whichever look wins, and each one is a check.

Measure and size

Body 18–19 px on phones, 62–68 characters per line, line height about 1.6. Contrast 4.5:1 or better on rendered pixels, light and dark.

Section rhythm

Every section opens the same way: your heading, with 48 px or more of space above it. The Name and Reflection get a larger setting. No boxes around story text.

First screen

At 390 × 664 after the bar: the label, the title, the safety note when present, the meta line and the jump row are all visible.

One fixed layer while reading

Nothing covers text. Checked at 375, 390 and 1440 px and at 200% text size.

Type and color

Two typefaces at most, one accent for actions, the 8 px grid, 44 px targets, visible focus. Safety panels use a calm warm tint, never alarm red.

Both modes, and print

Light and dark both designed, following the system setting. Print gives the full text and the disclaimer, with no bars or buttons.

Weight

Fonts load once from the shared folder. A combo page stays small enough to open quickly on a weak phone signal.

Calm motion

Only to show a change (a contents sheet opening, results updating). Nothing autoplays. Reduced motion means none.
