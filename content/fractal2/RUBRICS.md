# Fractal² — quality rubrics (v1, 2026-11-10)

Three rubrics: the writing, the experience, the navigation. Each criterion is scored 0 to 4 (0 absent, 1 weak, 2 adequate, 3 strong, 4 as good as it can be made). **Pass** = every criterion at 3 or above and no hard fail. The directing session scores first; an independent reviewer who has not seen those scores scores second; work iterates until both pass. Scores and notes are appended at the foot of this file.

## A. The writing (`content/fractal2/intro-final.md`)

| # | Criterion | What a 4 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Carries a newcomer** | Someone who has seen none of The Fractal understands the eight stages, three nested levels, thirty sectors as captured relationships, and 2,700 examples, without a glossary. |
| A2 | **The Origin is exact** | Three conditions (a real need, a difference in power, a gatekeeper); runs without a villain; parasite-like; and the people who drive it on purpose are clearly separated from those who carry it. No conspiracy grammar. |
| A3 | **Mirror and Chain are present** | The inward form and the inherited form both appear, with "not your fault" and "you are the current end of the chain". Inherited-stress claims are worded as belief. |
| A4 | **The true map lands as scale** | 30 becomes 335; one tactic shown at three levels; more than 1,000 per sector; the reader feels the size without a table. |
| A5 | **The combinations are the strongest section** | Foreboding and precise: why combining multiplies harm; why the order is the mechanism; one real sequence walked in order; then why knowing it heals (inside it now, lived it before, never lived it). Every harm is paired with its way out. |
| A6 | **The lens is whole** | Counterfeit and original; the eight inversions; the original frequency; the traditions as translation, never conversion; down, not up; the kingdom within. A secular reader is never shut out. |
| A7 | **The owner's account is exact** | Only what the owner wrote in message 4. Nothing added, nothing dramatized. |
| A8 | **Accountability without accusation** | Methods, never people. Grace for carriers, no hiding place for drivers. No named or identifiable villain. |
| A9 | **Register** | Powerful, organized prose. Not fiction, not a report. The lens is signposted once. No hedging that drains it; no claim stated as proven that is belief. |
| A10 | **The future and the lifetime** | A world without the cycle is described as buildable; seeing what is in the way comes first; the work is named as a lifetime's. |
| A11 | **Sentence craft** | Varied rhythm; concrete nouns; no filler; no stock AI phrasing (reveal formulas, triads on autopilot, trailing clauses); reads aloud well. |
| A12 | **The ending hands off** | The closing image resolves the opening one, and the reader is led into the sector site in one natural step. |

Hard fails: an added fact about the owner's life; a named person or company as a villain; a wrong public number (2,700; 335; more than 1,000 per sector; 203 and 153); a scene not labelled composite; no safety line.

## B. The experience (`/fractal2`)

| # | Criterion | What a 4 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | **Every effect carries meaning** | Each figure encodes something true from the work (each point is one mapped example; the ring is the eight stages; the chain is the real tactic order). Nothing moves for decoration. |
| B2 | **Prose and picture arrive together** | A figure appears as the sentence that needs it is read, on both phone and desktop. |
| B3 | **Motion is physical and interruptible** | Scrubbing back and forth never breaks a figure; nothing plays "at" the reader; reversing mid-transition is smooth. |
| B4 | **Performance** | Smooth scrolling on a mid-range phone: median frame under 17 ms and 95th percentile under 34 ms in the measured run; no long tasks over 100 ms after load. |
| B5 | **The phone is its own design** | Text is comfortably readable over the stage; step cards sit clear of the figure; nothing important is under a thumb-zone control; no horizontal overflow at 375px. |
| B6 | **Typography and space** | Two typefaces, a dramatic scale, a 60 to 70 character measure, an 8px rhythm, one accent. It looks like a published book, not a web app. |
| B7 | **Reduced motion is complete** | With motion reduced, every figure is shown as a still with its description in words; nothing is lost. |
| B8 | **Accessible** | WCAG 2.2 AA: contrast, keyboard path, focus visible, landmarks and headings, every figure has a text equivalent, axe reports no serious or critical issues in dark and light. |
| B9 | **Resilient** | No JavaScript, no WebGL, or a slow device: the full text is readable and nothing is stuck invisible. |
| B10 | **Narration** | Started only by the reader; follows the voice; pauses, speed and mute work; any scroll hands control back; captions come from the prose. |
| B11 | **House rules** | Self-contained page, no third-party requests except the sanctioned beacon, self-hosted fonts, absolute links, THE HOUSE drawer present, version note present. |
| B12 | **It is memorable** | A person shown it for one minute can describe one image afterwards. |

Hard fails: autoplay sound; text hidden until script runs; a horizontal scrollbar at 375px; an external script other than the beacon; a console error.

## C. The navigation (`/fractal2/<sector>`)

| # | Criterion | What a 4 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Seven readers, two taps** | From the sector home, each of the seven readers in NAV-SPEC §1 reaches what they need within two taps. Walk each one and record the path. |
| C2 | **Every page stands alone** | A searcher landing on any page knows what it is, where it sits, and where to go next. |
| C3 | **Safety first** | Safety notes lead every pattern that has one, in every reading mode; the short safety line and the quick exit are on every page; "in it now" never tells anyone to confront. |
| C4 | **Find a pattern three ways** | By where you are (paths), by what you notice, and by browsing, filtering or searching all of them. |
| C5 | **Reading modes** | Two-minute, sequence and full are one document; switching loses no words; deep links work; without JavaScript the full text shows. |
| C6 | **Tactic pages answer four questions** | What it is, what it looks like at each level, which patterns use it, where else to read about it. |
| C7 | **The map tells the truth** | The graph is built from real ids; selecting a pattern draws its sequence in order; the same information is available as text and by keyboard. |
| C8 | **Links are real** | Every link resolves from an id or an existing file; none are guessed; unresolved names are plain text and listed. |
| C9 | **Recognition, never diagnosis** | "What are you noticing?" stores nothing, sends nothing, scores nothing, and says what a match does not mean. |
| C10 | **Author's words intact** | Exact word-count match on all combo pages (plus logged ledger changes only); no internal ids or production cues shown. |
| C11 | **Phone and access** | As B5 and B8, for every page type. |
| C12 | **Inherits** | Everything is produced by the generator from data; a second sector would need a package, a settings file and one command. |

Hard fails: any original verify check weakened; a guessed link; a page that tells a person in danger to confront someone; any stored or transmitted reader input.
