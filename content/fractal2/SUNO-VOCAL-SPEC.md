# Suno and vocal recording spec for the Fractal² intro film (2026-11-15)

Targets, not rules. The film re-times itself from your recorded voice (word-level timestamps), so the picture follows you. Only the four anchors in section 3 have to land on the beat grid.

## 1. Tempo, key, length
- 150 BPM, half-time feel (it will feel like 75). One bar = 1.6 s. Eight bars = 12.8 s.
- One key throughout. Suggested: D minor, lifting to F major at the Window (9:48.8).
- Total 448 bars = 11:56.8. Script is 1,130 words at about two words a second, plus the visual-only stretches.
- Suno limits the length of one generation, so plan five movements in the same tempo and key and join them (or extend). Instrumental only.

## 4. Recording the voice
- Record dry: headphones on, no music in the room. Mono, 48 kHz, 24-bit WAV. Same mic distance throughout. Ten seconds of room tone at the start.
- Record section by section if that is easier; leave real pauses between lines. Do not chase the timestamps while speaking. Place the takes against the music afterwards so the four anchor words land.
- Read the script exactly as approved. Any changed word needs to come back to me, because captions are matched to the script word by word.

## 5. What to send
1. Final mix: 48 kHz, MP3 320 kbps or AAC 256 kbps. About -16 LUFS integrated, true peak under -1 dB.
2. Voice only, same length and start point as the mix (WAV).
3. Music only, same length and start point (WAV).
The separate files let the light follow your voice alone and the rings follow the drums alone, and they make the word alignment more accurate.

## 6. Mix notes that help the visuals
- Keep the music thin between 300 Hz and 3 kHz while you speak.
- Keep the sub (20-120 Hz) clean, mono and tied to the drums; the rings read it.
- Real silence at 2:59.2-3:02.4 and 7:08.8-7:15.2: no reverb tails across them.
