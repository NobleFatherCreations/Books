# The Counterfeit — three Suno songs

Written to play under the slide videos. Diction first: every verse is built to be understood on one listen while the slides are on screen. Each song teaches one layer of the map, in the same order as the videos:

1. **The Copy** (the pattern itself): the original vs the copy, the eight steps in order, and the thirty techniques by name.
2. **Thirty-Four Doors** (religion): the same eight steps found inside 34 traditions, said with respect for the faith and none for the copy.
3. **Everywhere Else** (the fractal): the same cycle in every sector of life, at three levels of scale, then inside you (The Mirror), in the body (The Body), and finally in Mother Earth.

Hooks repeat on purpose: "every copy adds a condition," "one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight," "same eight steps." By the third song the listener can finish the line before it's sung.

Suno tips: paste the **Style** into "Style of Music" and the lyrics (with the [bracket] tags) into "Lyrics" in Custom mode. If a version rushes the words, add "slow tempo, clear enunciation, words up front in the mix" to the style. For the videos, set the slide hold time to the song's length or loop the song.
