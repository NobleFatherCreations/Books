## The test used for every entry

1. **Same tactic?** Two entries are one tactic if the person on the receiving end could not tell them apart by what is done to them. A different setting (home, work, school, clinic, online), medium (in person, digital, AI), timing (before or after leaving), target or actor (a person or an institution) does not make a new tactic. It becomes an alias.
2. **Exception:** variants stay separate where the **safe response is different**: Emotional Blackmail via Self-Harm Threats (crisis services), Swatting (emergency), Debt Bondage, Document Confiscation and Status Threats (anti-trafficking or legal help), Harm to Pets or Property (a danger sign).
3. **Single move?** An entry is a **built-in combo** if it names two or more moves in sequence, an **umbrella** if it names a family of moves, and an **effect** if it names what happens to the person or group rather than something done.
4. Names: a merged name becomes an alias. Where combos already use both names, the survivor carries both (rule N4), so no finished script changes visibly.

## Checks run after the audit (all pass)

- The build stops on any error: no two entries share a name, either exactly or loosely (brackets dropped, plurals folded); no name from any source points to two entries; every entry has a definition and three Education examples; every combo step resolves.
- A word-overlap scan of all pairs of the 335 singles was run and every flagged pair was read: none is a duplicate.
- Every merged entry's name is kept as an alias (952 names from all sources still resolve).
- When two of your definitions now belong to one tactic, the survivor keeps its own; the build was fixed to guarantee this. Definitions were rewritten only where an earlier merge had put the wrong text on a tactic (Divide and Conquer, Plausible Deniability, Blame-Shifting, Intimate Image Abuse, Institutional Capture) or a survivor now covers its merged partner (Intimidation, Account Control, Gish Gallop, Astroturfing, Legal Abuse, Choice Restriction, Labor Extraction).
- Combos, proposed combos, strengthening suggestions and tiers were rebuilt on the merged list; every tactic no combo uses still appears in a proposed combo and has a strengthening suggestion.

Every decision is one line in `decisions/hard_audit.txt` with its reason. Delete or change a line and rebuild to undo it.
