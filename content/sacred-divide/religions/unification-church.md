## 5. History {#history}

The movement was founded in Seoul in 1954 as the Holy Spirit Association for the Unification of World Christianity [1]. Moon's *Divine Principle* (the movement's central doctrinal text) teaches that the Fall (in Christian theology, humanity's first sin) corrupted humanity's lineage, and that the True Parents restore it through the Blessing (arranged marriage) [1]. The movement reached Japan in 1958 or 1959, depending on the source [12][19], and the United States in the 1960s–70s. It built businesses and anti-communist political networks, which a US congressional subcommittee investigated in 1976–78 [3]. It took the name Family Federation for World Peace and Unification in the 1990s [1]. After Moon's death, sons led rival organizations.

## 10. Genealogy {#genealogy}

1. **Ancestral-karma giving**
   - *Origin:* The doctrine holds that the lineage is restored through "indemnity" (restoration through sacrifice) [1]. From 1971 the church also taught that Japan owed Korea for its colonial sins [18].
   - *Then:* Sacrifice carried theological meaning.
   - *Expired?* When courts treat the solicitation as unlawful, it is a sales method [4][11].
   - *Benefits now:* Headquarters benefits [18].
2. **Matched marriage**
   - *Origin:* The Blessing is understood as the restoration of God's lineage [1].
   - *Then:* It was the central sacrament.
   - *Expired?* International matches move spouses across borders: by February 2023 some 13,000 Japanese women had married Korean men through mass weddings, and more than half of them moved to Korea [19]. Former members' testimony describes dependency; this page cites no named account.
   - *Benefits now:* The institution controls family formation.
3. **Political cultivation**
   - *Origin:* It began as anti-communist networking in the 1960s–70s [3].
   - *Then:* It provided protection and influence.
   - *Expired?* In 2022 it became a national scandal in Japan [10], and in 2026 a first-instance criminal conviction in Korea [14].
   - *Benefits now:* Politicians and the movement benefit jointly.
