## neutral
A new religious movement founded in South Korea in 1954 by Sun Myung Moon, now led by his widow Hak Ja Han Moon. Members number perhaps hundreds of thousands worldwide *(verify; the movement's own claims are far higher)*. It is known for its "Blessing" mass wedding ceremonies, its teaching of Moon and Han as the "True Parents", and a large business and media network.

## origin
Founded in Seoul in 1954 as the Holy Spirit Association for the Unification of World Christianity. Moon's *Divine Principle* teaches that the Fall broke God's family lineage and that the True Parents restore it through the Blessing (arranged marriage). The movement expanded to Japan (1959) and the US (1960s–70s), built businesses and political anti-communist networks, and was renamed the Family Federation for World Peace and Unification in 1994 *(verify)*. After Moon's death (2012), leadership passed to Hak Ja Han, and sons split off into rival organizations.
