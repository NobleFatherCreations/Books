## branches
A single worldwide organization, with no denominations inside it. What varies is how close a person stands to the centre.

| Layer | Size | What it means on this page's questions |
|---|---|---|

| **Publishers** | A peak of 9,205,326 in 2025 [6] | Members who report their preaching activity [6] |

| **People removed from the congregation** | Not published | Formerly "disfellowshipped"; since 2024 members may greet them at meetings [8] |

## law
| Country | What the law does | What happened |
|---|---|---|
| **Australia** | A Royal Commission with powers to compel documents [1] | The organization's own files named 1,006 alleged perpetrators since 1950, none reported by it to police [1]; it joined the National Redress Scheme in 2021 [2] |
| **England & Wales** | Charity law [14] | A nine-year Charity Commission inquiry (2014–2023) found the trustees "not as straightforward or transparent as they should have been" about who held safeguarding responsibility [14] |
| **United States** | Civil liability for abuse [15][16] | A 2012 jury awarded Candace Conti $28 million; on appeal the court held there was no duty to warn the congregation and cut the award to $2.8 million [15][16] |
| **Norway** | State registration and grants, conditioned on law [3] | The state withdrew both in 2022; the Supreme Court held in 2026 that the withdrawal was invalid [3] |
| **Russia** | The Supreme Court declared the organization "extremist" in 2017 [5] | 395 local organizations liquidated [5] |
| **Japan** | Child-welfare law | A 2023 report by lawyers supporting former members found 81% of respondents had been made to carry a blood-refusal card [17] |
| **United States (1943)** | *West Virginia v. Barnette*: schoolchildren cannot be forced to salute the flag [4] | A Witness family won a landmark free-speech case [4] |

## cases
### The inquiry that took nine years (England & Wales, 2014–2023)
- **when:** 2014–2023
- **what:** The Charity Commission opened an inquiry in May 2014 into how the Watch Tower Bible and Tract Society of Britain handled safeguarding and advised congregations. Witness organizations told the national abuse inquiry of 67 allegations against 67 individuals between 2009 and 2019 [14].
- **record:** Charity Commission press release, 4 August 2023 (withdrawn from GOV.UK in September 2026) [14]
- **outcome:** The Commission criticized the trustees' transparency and concluded the charity was no longer the body responsible for safeguarding in congregations [14].
- **tactics:** 30, 18, 10
- **grade:** Documented

### No duty to warn (United States, 2012–2015)
- **when:** 2012–2015
- **what:** Candace Conti was abused as a child by a fellow congregant during door-to-door ministry. A jury awarded $7 million in compensatory and $21 million in punitive damages [15].
- **record:** Courthouse News, 14 April 2015 [15]; Reveal [16]
- **outcome:** The appeal court held the elders had no duty to warn members about a confessed molester and struck the punitive award; Watchtower's share fell to $2.8 million [15][16].
- **tactics:** 30, 18
- **grade:** Documented

### Banned as "extremist" (Russia, 2017)
- **when:** 2017
- **what:** Russia's Supreme Court declared the organization extremist and ordered its 395 local organizations liquidated [5].
- **record:** Human Rights Watch, 20 April 2017 [5]
- **outcome:** The harm here is done *to* Witnesses, by a state [5].
- **tactics:** —
- **grade:** Documented

## leaving
Practical guidance, not legal advice.

1. **Know the two exits.** Being removed (formerly disfellowshipped) or formally disassociating both lead to shunning by members, including family [1][8]; some people "fade" quietly instead.
2. **Since 2024** members may greet removed people at meetings [8]; family contact outside that is still usually cut.
3. **Update your medical directive.** If you carry a blood-refusal card, replace it with your current wishes and tell your doctor [17].
4. **If you were abused,** go to the police first; elders' records do not replace a police report [1].
5. **Find others who have left** [18][19][20].
