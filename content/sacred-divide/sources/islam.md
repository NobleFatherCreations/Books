5. Library of Congress Global Legal Monitor, "Saudi Arabia: Law on Passports Amended to Allow Women to Travel Abroad Without a Male Guardian" (23 Aug 2019; Royal Decree M/134). https://www.loc.gov/item/global-legal-monitor/2019-08-23/saudi-arabia-law-on-passports-amended-to-allow-women-to-travel-abroad-without-a-male-guardian/

7. Tunisia: repeal in September 2017 of the 1973 circular barring Muslim women from marrying non-Muslim men — Euronews report. https://www.euronews.com/2017/09/15/tunisia-overturns-decades-old-ban-on-women-marrying-non-muslims

10. Charity Commission for England and Wales, statutory inquiry report, *Brighton Mosque and Muslim Community Centre* (trustee disqualified 2024). https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/charity-inquiry-brighton-mosque-muslim-community-centre/charity-inquiry-brighton-mosque-muslim-community-centre
