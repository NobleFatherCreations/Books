# Sources — Hare Krishna / ISKCON

**Status: fact-check pass 1 complete — 2026-09-27, whole page.** Inventory: `_inventory/hare-krishna.md`.
Accurate. Two precision fixes:
1. The New Vrindaban "murder conspiracy convictions" were murder convictions of a devotee. The leader's
   1991 conviction, which included conspiracy to murder, was overturned; he pleaded guilty in 1996 to one
   count of racketeering.
2. The women-gurus question moved again. The GBC approved women as initiating gurus in 2019 and 2021,
   then paused it in November 2022.
