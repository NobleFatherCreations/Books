# Sources — Cross-cutting volumes and reference pages

**Status: fact-check pass 1 complete — 2026-09-27.**
Scope: everything outside the 27 tradition pages:
- Follow the Who (`D.who`), the legal notice, the change log and the pipelines;
- the Public-Records Toolkit, Sourcing Queue and About (V3);
- the Children's Codex and regional findings (V6);
- the Women's, Medical and LGBTQ codices, Promise Register, Succession Watch, What Sourced Counts Measure and Error Ledger (V7);

Extracted from the v4 candidate: 254 cross-cutting sentences. Claims about a single tradition are
checked on that tradition's page; this page covers the rest, plus the book's **counts of itself**,
which were checked against its own data.

**Findings**
1. **Two reader-visible "verify" notes remain outside the tradition pages.** Both are now resolved.
   - Kirchensteuer: the 2025 total was **€12.84bn**, made up of Catholic €6.75bn and Protestant €6.09bn.
   - CCLI: no public revenue total exists, so the claim is reworded to what is documented.
   - The Köhler "(verify)" note and the stale V2 apex note are also resolved.
2. **Stale self-counts.** These count the book itself, and each one was checked against the data.
   - The Sourcing Queue lede says "167 of 750"; the data says 194 of 810.
   - The Guide says "Instruments (11)"; there are 12.
   - The Guide says "Your Track (4 paths)"; there are 6. (Its four track slides already cover
     Protect and Leaving Safely inside Tracks 01 and 02, so only the count is wrong.)
   - Follow the Who cites "section 17–19"; the acts are numbered 00–17.
   - The "worst-sourced" list in What Sourced Counts Measure omits four traditions tied at the same count.
3. **Understated or imprecise facts.**
   - US religious exemptions to child-neglect law: the book says "a number of states"; it is
     34 states and DC.
   - US child marriage: the book says "tens of thousands"; it was nearly 300,000 minors from
     2000 to 2018.
   - The Grassley inquiry: two of the six ministries cooperated and reformed.
   - *Crimen sollicitationis* imposed the secret of the Holy Office. That secret was renamed the
     pontifical secret in 1974.
   - Yoder: Douglas dissented *in part*.
   - CIASE: the bishops' president contested a core *recommendation*, the confessional seal,
     not a finding.
4. **Cross-page carries.**
   - Ram Rahim's murder convictions were overturned.
   - Becciu is being retried in part.
   - JW redress date: September 2021.
   - JW Governing Body: eleven members.
   - SBC database: status Abandoned.
   - Hillsong: status Partial.

Legend: ✅ confirmed · ✏️ corrected · 🔹 interpretive · ⏳ open.
