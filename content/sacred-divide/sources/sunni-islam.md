## Sources (reader-facing)

### Law, courts and official records
1. Pakistan, Criminal Law (Amendment) (Offences in the Name or Pretext of Honour) Act 2016 (Act XLIII of 2016) — a pardon by the victim's heirs no longer spares the killer a life sentence. https://www.na.gov.pk/uploads/documents/1475762285_283.pdf
2. Oxford Human Rights Hub, "Muhammad Wasim v The State: Loopholes in the Criminal Law (Amendment) Act 2016" — the protection applies only when a court classifies the killing as honour-based. https://ohrh.law.ox.ac.uk/muhammad-wasim-v-the-state-loopholes-in-the-criminal-law-amendment-act-2016/
3. CNN, "Pakistan court frees brother who confessed to killing social media star" (Lahore High Court, 14 Feb 2022, after the parents' pardon; he was sentenced to life in 2019). https://www.cnn.com/2022/02/15/asia/pakistan-qandeel-baloch-brother-acquittal-intl-hnk
4. Indonesia, Law No. 16 of 2019 (marriage age 19), following the Constitutional Court's December 2018 ruling on a petition by three women married as children. https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Home/Details/122740/
5. Komnas Perempuan figures from the Supreme Court's Directorate-General of Religious Courts (Badilag): marriage dispensations granted rose from 23,126 (2019) to 64,211 (2020) — reported by SultraKini. https://sultrakini.com/selain-pelajar-smp-buton-selatan-64-ribu-perempuan-di-bawah-umur-menikah-pada-masa-pandemi/ ; background: Girls Not Brides, Indonesia. https://www.girlsnotbrides.org/en/learning-resources/child-marriage-atlas/regions-and-countries/indonesia/
6. Majelis Ulama Indonesia, fatwa 11/MUNAS VII/MUI/15/2005 (Ahmadiyya outside Islam) and fatwa 7/MUNAS VII/MUI/11/2005 (against pluralism, liberalism and secularism) — overview. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesian_Ulama_Council
7. Indonesia, Law 33/2014 on halal product assurance — the state agency BPJPH took over certification on 17 Oct 2019; MUI still issues the halal fatwa. https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/28038/UU%20Nomor%2033%20Tahun%202014.pdf
8. Egypt, Constitution of 2014 (rev. 2019), Article 7 — the Grand Imam of al-Azhar "may not be dismissed", chosen from the Council of Senior Scholars. https://www.constituteproject.org/constitution/Egypt_2019
9. Washington Institute, "Al-Azhar and the President: Breaking Down the Relationship" — the January 2012 law restored the Council of Senior Scholars and moved selection of the Grand Imam from presidential appointment to election by that council. https://www.washingtoninstitute.org/policy-analysis/al-azhar-and-president-breaking-down-relationship
10. Saudi Press Agency — royal order appointing Saleh al-Fawzan Grand Mufti and chair of the Council of Senior Scholars (22 Oct 2025). https://www.spa.gov.sa/en/N2426644
11. Daily Sabah, "Istanbul mufti Arpaguş appointed as head of Türkiye's Diyanet" (Official Gazette, 17 Sept 2025; took office 18 Sept 2025). https://www.dailysabah.com/turkiye/istanbul-mufti-arpagus-appointed-as-head-of-turkiyes-diyanet/news
12. Nordic Monitor, "Turkish government plans to boost Diyanet budget as its foreign network expands" (Sept 2026) — about 93,000 mosques in the domestic network per a parliamentary record. https://nordicmonitor.com/2026/09/turkish-government-plans-to-boost-diyanet-budget-as-its-foreign-network-expands/

### Human-rights reporting and scholarship
13. Human Rights Watch, "Saudi Arabia: Prominent Clerics Arrested" (Salman al-Awda and others, September 2017). https://www.hrw.org/news/2017/09/15/saudi-arabia-prominent-clerics-arrested
14. Human Rights Watch, "Saudi Arabia: Prominent Cleric May Face Death Penalty" (2018). https://www.hrw.org/news/2018/09/12/saudi-arabia-prominent-cleric-may-face-death-penalty

16. Pew Research Center, *How the Global Religious Landscape Changed From 2010 to 2020* (2025) — 2.0 billion Muslims in 2020. https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/2025/06/09/how-the-global-religious-landscape-changed-from-2010-to-2020/
17. Tempo, NU congress elects Yahya Cholil Staquf over the incumbent (24 Dec 2021). https://en.tempo.co/read/1542799/nahdlatul-ulama-congress-elects-yahya-cholil-staquf-as-chairperson
18. CNBC Indonesia, "Data & Fakta: Organisasi Massa Islam di RI" (LSI Denny JA survey, 2023 — ~57% of Indonesians identify with NU, ~6% with Muhammadiyah). https://www.cnbcindonesia.com/research/20250329201011-128-622800/data-fakta-organisasi-massa-islam-di-ri-nu-atau-muhammadiyah
