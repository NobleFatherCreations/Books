# Sources — Jehovah's Witnesses

**Status: fact-check pass 1 complete — 2026-09-27, whole page.** Inventory: `_inventory/jehovahs-witnesses.md`.
**Findings:**
1. **Norway: the Witnesses won.** The Court of Appeal (14 Mar 2025) and the Supreme Court (30 Apr 2026,
   HR-2026-1009-A) held that withdrawing their registration and grants was unlawful. The book's
   Norway card still says "whatever the final outcome". There is now a final outcome, and it runs
   against the book's precedent.
2. **Redress scheme:** the book's date is wrong. The organization announced it would join in
   March 2021 and joined in September 2021, not 2020. It acted after the government threatened to
   remove charity status.
3. **The Governing Body is eleven men, not "roughly nine".** The 2023 departure is Anthony Morris III's
   (22 Feb 2023).
4. **Four reader-visible "verify" notes** are resolved: two on the Brooklyn totals and two on the 2024 policy.

Legend: ✅ confirmed · ✏️ corrected · 🔹 interpretive · ⏳ open.
