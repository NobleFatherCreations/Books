# Sources — Sikhism

**Status: fact-check pass 1 complete — 2026-09-27, whole page.** Inventory: `_inventory/sikhism.md`.
**Main findings:**
1. **The SGPC's general house, the body that can be "voted", has not faced an election since 2011.**
   Elections are due every five years. Only the annual presidency vote, held within the 2011 house,
   has happened since. The book's compel line calls the SGPC elections "a genuine lever, used".
2. **Dera Sacha Sauda's head** still stands convicted of rape (2017), but his journalist-murder
   conviction was overturned on appeal in March 2026, and the Supreme Court is hearing that appeal.
3. The Akal Takht "verify" note can now name the holder.
