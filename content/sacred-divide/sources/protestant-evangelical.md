# Sources — Protestant / Evangelical Christianity

**Status: fact-check pass 1 complete — 2026-09-27.** Checked against the v4 candidate. Inventory:
`_inventory/protestant-evangelical.md`. **Main finding: the book says, in three places (a case, a
victory and the Promise Ledger), that the Southern Baptist Convention created a public database of
accused ministers. It never did.** Messengers voted for "Ministry Check" in 2022. No name was ever
published, and the Executive Committee shelved it in February 2025, citing legal hurdles. In
August–September 2026 survivors launched their own national Protestant database instead.

Legend: ✅ confirmed · ✏️ corrected · 🔹 interpretive · ⏳ open.
