# Sources — Mormonism / LDS

**Status: fact-check pass 1 complete — 2026-09-27, whole page.** Inventory: `_inventory/mormonism.md`.
**Findings:**
1. **The Presiding Bishop has changed.** Gérald Caussé became an apostle on 6 Nov 2025, and
   W. Christopher Waddell has been Presiding Bishop since 14 Nov 2025. The apex row still names Caussé.
2. **Utah did not abolish the clergy-penitent exemption, even "partially".** In 2024 it gave clergy
   legal protection if they *choose* to report abuse learned in confession. The privilege stands.
3. **Membership is 17.9 million (2025 report).** Convert baptisms rose about 25% that year, so "growth
   has slowed markedly" needs to be conditioned on the US.

The SEC figures, the succession and the 1959, 1978, 1993, 2014, 2015 and 2019 facts are all confirmed.

Legend: ✅ confirmed · ✏️ corrected · 🔹 interpretive · ⏳ open.
