# Sources — Judaism (umbrella page)

**Status: fact-check pass 1 complete — 2026-09-27, whole page.** Inventory: `_inventory/judaism.md`.
**Main finding:** the book says the Rabbinate got its monopoly in 1948 and still controls marriage, divorce,
conversion and burial outright. The date is wrong (the statute is 1953), and three of the four powers have
changed. Non-Orthodox conversions performed in Israel count for citizenship (2021). Civil burial has been a
legal right since 1996, though it is hard to obtain. Online civil marriages must be registered (Supreme
Court, 2023). Divorce is still exclusively rabbinic.

Legend: ✅ confirmed · ✏️ corrected · 🔹 interpretive · ⏳ open.
