# Sources — Buddhism (umbrella page)

**Status: fact-check pass 1 complete — 2026-09-27, whole page.** Inventory: `_inventory/buddhism.md`.
**Main finding: the size figure is out of date.** The book says ~500–520 million. Pew's 2025 study puts
the figure at **324 million in 2020**, down 5% from 343 million in 2010, which makes Buddhism the only
major religion to shrink. Older counts near 500 million included much of China's folk practice. The
book should say which count it uses. Everything else checks.

Legend: ✅ confirmed · ✏️ corrected · 🔹 interpretive · ⏳ open.
