# Help organizations used across religions — vetted 2026-09-27

Copy the line you need into a religion's own Sources list (and renumber) — each religion's PDF keeps one numbering.
Re-check numbers every edition; services change.

- **Faith to Faithless** (Humanists UK) — people leaving high-control religion; helpline 020 3675 0959. https://humanists.uk/faith-to-faithless/helpline/
- **Recovering from Religion** — peer helpline and chat, (844) 368-2848 ("84-I-DOUBT-IT"); Secular Therapy Project referrals. https://www.recoveringfromreligion.org/
- **International Cultic Studies Association (ICSA)** — former members of high-control groups and their families (since 1979). https://internationalculticstudies.org/
- **SNAP — Survivors Network of those Abused by Priests** — survivors of clergy abuse (since 1989), with branches beyond Catholicism. https://www.snapnetwork.org/
- **NAPAC** — UK adult survivors of childhood abuse, 0808 801 0331. https://napac.org.uk/calling-our-support-line/
- **Childhelp National Child Abuse Hotline** — US, 1-800-422-4453, 24/7. https://childhelphotline.org/
- **RAINN National Sexual Assault Hotline** — US, 1-800-656-4673, 24/7. https://rainn.org/learn-about-rainn/contact-us/
- **Karma Nirvana** — UK honour-based abuse and forced marriage, 0800 5999 247. https://karmanirvana.org.uk/get-help/helpline/
- **NSPCC FGM Helpline** — UK, 0800 028 3550. https://www.nspcc.org.uk/keeping-children-safe/types-of-abuse/female-genital-mutilation-fgm/
- **Humanists International — Humanists at Risk** — people prosecuted for apostasy or blasphemy. https://humanists.international/what-we-do/humanists-at-risk/
- **Ex-Muslims of North America** — vetted private communities. https://exmuslims.org/community/
