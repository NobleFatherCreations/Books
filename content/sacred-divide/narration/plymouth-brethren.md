## for-you structure
This movement began as a protest against clergy standing between people and God. Today it has one worldwide leader, called the "Man of God," since 2002, succeeding his father, and the structure table records "No procedure" for removing him. In a 2025 recording, a senior figure told a member under questioning that they were there to protect "the Lord's interest and Mr Hales' interest." If you are a member, when did those two interests become one?

## for-you law
The Brethren do not vote, yet election authorities in Australia (2004) and New Zealand (2005) examined campaign material linked to members. If you are a voter in either country, a church whose members abstain has tried to shape your election. In England, a charity regulator once refused a Brethren trust registration because it had not shown public benefit. Does a group that separates from the world owe the world transparency when it seeks the world's tax status?

## for-you money
The glance-level fact most people have never heard: a network of about 3,000 member businesses centered on one services firm, with combined turnover the church's own material puts at around A$22 billion a year. The Australian Taxation Office raided businesses associated with it in 2024. If you are a customer, a supplier or an employee of a Brethren-owned business, you are part of this economy. Did you know it existed?

## for-you cost
This page's central question is simple: may a withdrawn parent eat with their children? Former members describe total separation from families who stay. One told the ABC, "I have no contact with my siblings, I have no contact with my mum." The church denies preventing contact. If you are inside and someone you love has been withdrawn from, the denial means no rule stops you from calling them. Have you?

## for-you who-gets-hurt
The table is short and stark: the withdrawn lose their families and often their jobs; children can be separated from a parent; women are silent in meetings. If you are a child of this community reading this at sixteen, know that people who leave are not alone. The help section lists organizations run by people who left before you.
