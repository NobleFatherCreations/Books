## for-you structure
ISKCON is governed by the Governing Body Commission, about thirty-plus members who fill their own seats by co-option. It approves initiating gurus and, the record shows, has suspended and removed them. That is more than many movements in this book can say. The page calls it "reform under subpoena": real, partial, and "purchased at the children's expense." If you are a devotee, do you know who sits on the GBC for your region, and how you could reach them?

## for-you law
Almost every structural change on this page came through a court. About 550 former gurukula pupils sued in 2000, and after Chapter 11 filings the case settled for $9.5 million in 2005. Criminal courts convicted a devotee of two murders at New Vrindaban. If you are a parent considering residential religious schooling anywhere, the lesson of this page is simple: ask who inspects it, and who a child can report to outside it.

## for-you money
The movement's base asset, the page says, is unpaid devotee labor, alongside book distribution, temple economies, restaurants and festivals. Book-distribution quotas historically drove aggressive fundraising. If you have eaten at a Govinda's restaurant or bought a book at an airport, you have taken part in this economy. If you are a devotee, what have you built, and who holds the title to it?

## for-you cost
Leaving can cost your whole community and years of unpaid labor with no equity, and the "bad association" doctrine can frame departure as spiritual death. A devotee who gives twenty-five years of full-time service may leave with no savings, work history or equity. If you have given years, do you know what you would leave with?

## for-you who-gets-hurt
Gurukula children come first: physical, emotional and sexual abuse, documented in litigation and admitted by the movement itself, while they were separated from their parents and given little secular education. The second generation reached adulthood without credentials or savings. If you are one of those children, the movement has put your harm in writing. Has it reached you?
