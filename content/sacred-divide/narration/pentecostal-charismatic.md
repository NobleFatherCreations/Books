## for-you structure
In most of this movement, the org chart is one person: the founder. The glance table says no public record identifies any body that can remove the founders it names. Below them, authority runs through "spiritual covering", a personal relationship between a junior pastor and a senior one that is written down nowhere. If your pastor is "covered" by someone, can you name who? And what would you do if the covering failed?

## for-you law
Your protection depends almost entirely on where your church is registered. In the UK a church that is a charity files public accounts, and the Charity Commission once appointed an interim manager to run a London Pentecostal church. In the US a church files no return at all. In Brazil, charges against one of the movement's most powerful founders expired without a verdict. Which country's rules does your church actually answer to? Do you know where it is registered?

## for-you money
This is the page where giving is openly described as an investment: a seed that produces a harvest. The money section points out the problem with that promise: it cannot be proven wrong. If the harvest comes, the seed worked. If it doesn't, the seed was too small or your faith too weak. If you are giving in the hope of a return, ask what would count as evidence that the promise had failed. If nothing could, then you are not making an investment.

## for-you cost
Leaving is legally free and socially expensive, and the cost is highest where the movement is strongest: immigrant and diaspora communities, and towns where the church is also the school, the clinic and the lender. A person who leaves after years of seed-faith giving leaves with nothing back. If you are a newcomer who found your first friends in a church like this, it is worth knowing now who else you could call.

## for-you who-gets-hurt
The table's first two rows are people at their most vulnerable. Members in financial crisis receive the appeals with the biggest promised returns and the shortest timelines. The seriously ill are taught that staying on medication is unbelief. If someone you love has stopped a treatment because of a prophecy, this section is about them. Would you know how to talk with them about it without pushing them further in?

## q1

example: The healthy section lists a congregation that owns its own building, on a title deed the members can read, as a sign of a healthy church. Its absence is what the question tests.

## q4
