# ENT: finished package (9 Nov 2026)
- `ENT_combo_book.pdf`: the combo book, 1759 pages, every pattern of the 203-combo master list that fits this sector (rated A or B), plus an "Also worth knowing" back-matter page of short notes for patterns that are rare here.
- `ENT_tactic_book_335.pdf`: the 3-scale tactic book, 117 pages, all 335 standard single tactics.
- `scripts/`: the 163 combo scripts as Markdown, plus category intros and sector standards.
Recognition and safe routing, never a playbook. Children, threats, medical and crisis matters route to professionals and emergency services.
