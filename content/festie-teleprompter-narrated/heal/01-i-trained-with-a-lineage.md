SCENARIO 1 — “I Trained With A Lineage”
🎬 TELEPROMPTER SCRIPT

Here's a specific type. Speaks with total calm certainty about where the work came from. Names a teacher, a region, a tradition, a number of years. Everything he says is structurally impossible to check, and he chose it for that reason.

Picture it. He says he trained for nine years with a lineage holder in a place you would have to fly to, whose name he gives softly, as though saying it too loudly would be disrespectful. Nobody in this tent is going to fact-check that. Nobody in this tent could. And you notice, as he says it, that the reverence in his voice is doing all the work that a certificate would otherwise have to do.

Here are the signs. Credentials that cannot be verified by design — remote, oral, or a deceased teacher. Reverence used to make questions feel rude. No insurance, no professional body, no supervisor, no complaints route. Scope that expands to whatever you need — trauma, grief, fertility, diagnosis. Vague about the parts a trained person is precise about: contraindications, referral, limits. Gets cooler toward anyone who asks a second question.

Now here's what's really happening. Every regulated healing profession is built on three things a false practitioner cannot supply: a checkable qualification, a body that can remove you, and a named limit to your scope. Unverifiable lineage is chosen precisely because it substitutes for all three at once — authority without accountability. It is the single most common structure in this scene's abuse cases, and the reason is not mysticism, it is architecture. The honest practitioners with real traditional training are usually the easiest people in the world to ask about it; they will tell you their teacher, their years, what they do not do, and who they refer to. Precision is the tell in both directions.

So here's your check. I call it the E check. Ask: “Can I name this person's training, their limits, and who they answer to?” — Three blanks is not a lineage. It is a costume.

And here's what sits underneath all of it. Unfalsifiable Is The Feature. Pay attention to what the claim is built out of: every element is unfalsifiable on purpose. Far away, long ago, orally transmitted, teacher deceased, tradition that does not certify. A person inventing a credential does not pick a claim you could check, and the ones who pick unfalsifiable claims are not being humble about the ineffable — they are engineering a position where doubting them is a spiritual failing rather than due diligence. That inversion is the actual product, because it is what makes the later stuff survivable: once questioning him reads as your resistance, the room will help him. Ask the second question. His temperature when you do is more informative than the answer.

So here's your move. Ask three plain questions in a friendly voice: who trained you, what do you not do, and who would I talk to if I had a problem with a session. Ask them in front of other people. Write down what he says.

Here are the words. “Love the sound of that — who did you train with, and what's outside your scope? And if I had a concern afterwards, who's the person I'd raise it with?”

And here's the truth underneath it. Real practitioners answer credential questions with relief. Only a costume is insulted by them.
