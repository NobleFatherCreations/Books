SCENARIO 5 — “The Plug Situation”
🎬 TELEPROMPTER SCRIPT

Let's talk about the person you'll meet here. The person who becomes your exclusive source for what you're consuming — and uses that position to manufacture proximity, obligation, and a dependency that keeps you in their orbit. Often presents as generosity.

Here's the scene. He had the best stuff and he kept finding you. By Saturday you realize your whole weekend has been organized around staying near him — not because you decided to, but because that's just where the access was. When you tried to wander he got tight. "Where you going? I thought we were hanging." Your social world has quietly contracted to one person and you're not sure exactly when that happened.

So watch for these. He is the exclusive source of what you're consuming — no other connects this weekend. His emotional temperature changes when you try to spend time elsewhere. Access to substances tied to physical proximity to him. Your friend group has contracted to his orbit without a conscious decision.

Here's the mechanism underneath it. Substance gatekeeping manufactures dependency by making one person the gatekeeper to your altered state. For men this often comes packaged as generous bro behavior — sharing, providing, being the plug. The tell is what happens when you try to exercise independence. Tightness, cold shoulder, wounded energy — these are the control mechanisms becoming visible.

So ask yourself this. I call it the A check. Ask: “Who controls my access to substances this weekend — and what is that actually costing me?”

Now here's the part that's hard to hear. When The Supply Is The Setup. In some cases the substance access IS the trap — you're being kept compliant, isolated, or in debt through the supply. Signs this is happening: you feel like you owe him beyond what any substance cost, he uses access as a bargaining chip in disagreements, leaving his orbit feels dangerous rather than just inconvenient. This is leverage, not generosity. Your consciousness is yours. Nobody should be holding the key to it.

So here's what you do. Know your own source independently of anyone who also wants something from you.

Here's what to say, out loud. "I'm good tonight — heading to find my camp. I'll catch you tomorrow."

And remember this. Your altered state should never be owned by someone who wants something from you.
