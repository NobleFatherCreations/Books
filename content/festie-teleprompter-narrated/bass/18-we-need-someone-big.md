SCENARIO 18 — “We Need Someone Big”
🎬 TELEPROMPTER SCRIPT

Here's someone you'll recognize. The camp lead, the promoter, or the guy with the arrangement, who has looked at you and seen an asset rather than a person. Flattering about it. Specific about what he needs and vague about what it involves.

Picture it. He says you have a presence, that people listen to you, that he needs someone who can stand at the gate of the camp and turn people away. Then it is someone to have a word with a bloke who has been a problem. Then it is someone to come along to a conversation at the far end of the site, and you are eleven minutes into a walk before you have properly asked what this conversation is.

Here are the signs. Recruited for your size or presence rather than for judgement or skill. The task keeps growing and was never described in full. No role, no training, no authority, and no radio. You would be the one physically present if it goes wrong. He is not coming, or is coming and standing behind you. Nobody has mentioned who is liable if somebody gets hurt.

Now here's what's really happening. Untrained physical intervention is the fastest route to a criminal charge at a festival, and men get recruited into it constantly because presence reads as competence. The person asking transfers every risk to you — legal, physical and reputational — while keeping the authority and the benefit. Real security is trained, licensed, insured and radio-connected for exactly these reasons. Being big is not a qualification, it is an appearance, and it is the thing that makes you the most useful available person to put between someone else and a problem.

So here's your check. I call it the A check. Ask: “What exactly am I being asked to do, and who is responsible if it goes wrong?” — If either answer is fuzzy, that fuzziness is the arrangement.

And here's what sits underneath all of it. You Are The One In The Footage. Picture the aftermath rather than the moment. There is phone footage, and it starts thirty seconds in, and in every frame of it the large man doing something physical is you. The person who asked is not in shot. He was not there, he did not instruct anything, and his account afterwards will be that you took it upon yourself. This is precisely how men end up arrested at festivals for other people's disputes, and it is why the answer is not a braver version of yes. Getting a trained person is not cowardice; it is the only version of this that ends with you not being the defendant.

So here's your move. Decline the physical role and supply the actual one: get security or the duty manager, be a visible witness, film from a distance, stay with the person who is at risk. Presence without contact is genuinely useful and is not a criminal offence.

Here are the words. “I'm not putting hands on anyone — that's a job for someone licensed. I'll come and stand there, and I'll get a radio, but I'm not the muscle.”

And here's the truth underneath it. Being the biggest bloke available is not a job description. It is just who they will name afterwards.
