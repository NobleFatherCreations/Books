This guide is the one called B.U.I.L.D. — the Build Crew & Installation Edition. And it exists for a specific reason. You are on site two weeks before anyone else and you leave after the lights go. This guide is for the part of the festival nobody photographs.

So let me walk you through what B.U.I.L.D. stands for, because these 5 ideas are the frame that everything else in this guide runs on.

The letter B stands for “Barter Is Still A Contract.” Wristband, food, a bunk, a credit — if you are working for it, it has terms. Get them in writing before the first shift.

The letter U stands for “Unsafe Is A Full Stop.” You are allowed to refuse a task. Fatigue, missing PPE, bad rigging, no banksman. The site's schedule is not your liability.

The letter I stands for “Isolation Is The Risk Multiplier.” Half-empty site, night shift, no medical yet, no phone signal. Everything in this book gets more dangerous when nobody is around.

The letter L stands for “Log It While It Is Fresh.” Hours, incidents, changes to the deal, who said what. Build runs on memory and goodwill, and both get rewritten at settle-up.

The letter D stands for “Danger Of The Ninety-Sixth Hour.” Most build injuries happen late in a long stretch to experienced people. Tiredness is the hazard, not inexperience.

That's the framework. Barter. Unsafe. Isolation. Log. Danger. 5 ideas, and they hold this whole thing together.

Now, coming up after this, there are 9 scenarios in this guide — and each one is its own short video. We'll move through how access and trust get established, how the situation gets shaped once you're already in it, how leverage gets held and used, the practical scripts and tools that actually work, and finally how to tell real community from the counterfeit version.
