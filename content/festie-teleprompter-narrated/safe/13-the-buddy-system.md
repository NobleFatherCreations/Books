SCENARIO 13 — “The Buddy System”
🎬 TELEPROMPTER SCRIPT

Here's the person you'll meet here. The buddy system is not childish. It is the single most effective community safety tool at a festival and the people who use it consistently have better and safer experiences than the people who do not. Here is how to actually run one.

Picture it. Friday morning before the festival opens your group sits down for ten minutes. Everyone says who their buddy is for the weekend. You set a check-in time — 2pm every day, you physically find each other or send a specific text. You choose a code word that means "come find me right now, no questions." You agree on a meeting spot for if you get separated. You agree that if anyone does not check in on time someone comes looking. Ten minutes. That is the whole investment.

Here are the signs. Name your buddy before gates open — not organically during the festival. Set a specific daily check-in time with a specific response expected, not just "stay in touch". Choose a code word that means "come find me immediately" — something you would not say normally. Agree on a physical meeting spot that does not require phone signal to find.

Now here's what's really happening. The buddy system works because it creates accountability before you need it — not after something has already gone wrong. The check-in time means that if something is wrong you know within hours, not days. The code word means you can signal for help in any situation without making a scene. The meeting spot means you can reconnect without phone signal. These are not elaborate safety measures. They are ten minutes of planning that changes the entire risk profile of your weekend.

So here's your check. I call it the E check. Ask: “Does everyone in my group have a buddy, and do we all have the code word and the meeting point, agreed before we split up?” — ten minutes spent on this before the festival is the preparation that matters most.

And here's what sits underneath all of it. The Full Buddy System Checklist. Before gates open: (1) Every person has a named buddy. (2) Daily check-in time agreed — specific time, specific expected response. (3) Code word chosen — something normal-sounding that means "come get me now." (4) Physical meeting spot chosen — a Specific landmark, not a GPS point. (5) One person not at the festival has the names and phone numbers of everyone in the group. (6) Everyone writes two emergency numbers in pen on their arm — their buddy and one person at home. (7) Offline festival map downloaded on at least two phones. Ten minutes. Do it before the music starts.

So here's your move. Do the buddy system setup with your whole group before gates open. All of it. Ten minutes.

Here are the words. “Before we go in — ten minutes to sort the buddy system. Who is with who? What is the code word? What is the meeting spot?”

And here's the truth underneath it. The ten minutes you spend on this before the festival is the preparation that matters most.
