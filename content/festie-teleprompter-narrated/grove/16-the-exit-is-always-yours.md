The more someone works to make leaving complicated, the more important it is that you leave. That is the metric.

So run this check. I call it the E check. Ask: “Exit Is Mine” — can I leave right now, freely? The answer tells you everything.

Systems built before you need them are the only ones that work when you actually need them.

So here's the play. Stand up. Say "I need to go." Walk. You can be kind AND leave. These have always been compatible.

And here's the truth. The exit is always yours. Nobody gets to make leaving complicated enough that you stay.
