# The Long After: expansion outline (second pass, 2026-11-22)

Owner's brief: roughly double the book; outline every topic and thought completely, in the order that best helps the reader; then voice audit, no-AI-slop and steelman; design only after.

How each chapter was outlined: read what it already answers, then ask what a frightened, tired reader would still need to *understand* it, *act* on it, and *feel steadier* about it. New sections are listed in reading order with the job each one does. Voice spec: BOOK-MAP-v2.md §2 (short sentences, second person, one concrete picture per section, the hard thing said once, three registers).
