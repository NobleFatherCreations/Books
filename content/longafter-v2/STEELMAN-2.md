# Steelman round 2 (2026-11-22): the finished book read by hostile readers

Round 1 (BOOK-MAP-v2.md §11) attacked the outline before writing. This round attacks the written, expanded book (about 90,000 words). Each reader asks: what's missing, what's wrong, what's unsafe? Every finding has a fix and a status.

| # | Reader | Finding | Fix | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Family lawyer (England and Wales) | Nothing warns readers that sharing family court documents or details of children's cases can be contempt of court. A frightened reader posting their story could get into serious trouble. | New section in *Family court, honestly*: what you can and can't share about the case. | done |
| 2 | Family lawyer (US) | Same gap for sealed records and gag orders. | Same section, US paragraph. | done |
| 3 | Criminal justice advocate | The book covers reporting to police but stops before a criminal trial: being a witness, special measures, victim statements, bail conditions. | New sections in *Police and reporting*. | done |
| 4 | Clinician | Medication is barely mentioned. Readers will ask about antidepressants and sleeping tablets beyond one sleep section. | New section in *When it's more than aftermath*: medication, without advice on drugs or doses. | done |
| 5 | Teenager whose parent left an abuser | The book speaks to under-18s in their own relationships, not to the teenager living through a parent's separation. | New section in *If your story doesn't look like the usual one*. | done |
| 6 | Reader whose ex has died | Death of the person you left is not covered: complicated grief, relief, the funeral, inheritance and the children. | New section in *The grief nobody will let you have*. | done |
| 7 | Reader of faith | Religious divorce (for example a get or talaq) can be a separate process, and refusing one can be part of the control. Not mentioned. | New paragraph in *Divorce and separation proceedings*. | done |
| 8 | Self-employed reader | Untangling a shared business is missing from the money chapters. | New section in *Money as the channel*. | done |
| 9 | Researcher | New sections added claims (laws, guidelines, studies) without adding them to each chapter's Sources list. | Add source lines in phase 6 (facts). | phase 6 |
| 10 | Survivor at 2am on a phone | Chapters are now long. The short answer still carries each page, but a reader may not know they can stop there. | *How to use this book* already says so; design phase to make short answers and the contents easier to jump to (in-page contents for long chapters). | design |
| 11 | The person they left, reading over their shoulder | Could anything here be used against the reader? Pages are about patterns, never naming people; the Leave button and private browsing advice exist. Risk: a reader printing pages and leaving them at home. | Add a line to *Sharing this book safely* about where printouts are kept. | done |
| 12 | Domestic abuse advocate | The book rarely says what advocates themselves can't do (they can't give legal advice, can't make the police act), which leads to disappointment. | One paragraph in *Where to find the right body*. | done |
| 13 | Parent whose children were turned against them | Covered in part (*When your child is pulled in*), but there's nothing on reconnecting with adult children years later. | New section there. | done |

| 15 | Disabled reader | Accessibility of the book itself (screen readers, large text) belongs to design. Content covers adjustments and carers. | design | design |
