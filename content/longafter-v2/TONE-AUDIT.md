# Tone audit (2026-11-22): seen, safe, in the right place

Owner's brief: the writing should make the reader feel seen, safe, and in the right place to get the information they need.

| Test | How it was checked | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Seen**: the page meets the reader in their own situation before it explains anything | Every chapter's opening paragraph read (83 chapters) | 79 already opened on the reader's moment ("Your phone buzzes. Your stomach drops."). 4 opened on an explanation (therapist, legal help, work, housing); each now starts with the reader's moment |
| **Safe**: no blame, doubt or judgment aimed at the reader | Scan for judging phrases (should have, why didn't you, overreact, dramatic, crazy, weak, just leave, get over it, your fault), every hit read in context | All hits are negations ("says nothing about whether you should have stayed"), quotes of what others say, or neutral uses. No changes needed |

| **Safe**: privacy | Leave button, private-browsing advice, printed-pages advice (added), no storage | In place |
