## The Sunrise Set + The Kandi Trade (closer)

Here's the Sunrise Set. Zero slang — just the true thing. Some people use the word family to mean the thing family is supposed to mean: the people who show up when it costs them something, who celebrate your wins without accounting for them, who can be told a hard truth without converting it into a debt.

**Some people use the word family to mean:** you owe me now.

You are allowed to notice the difference. You are allowed to need real help and also be careful about where you accept it. You are allowed to say thank you and no at the same time.

Help that makes you smaller is not help. Help that makes your world narrower is not generosity. Help that arrives at exactly the moment that creates the most dependence is worth a second look.

The scene at its best runs on real generosity — spontaneous, no receipt, no balance sheet, given because someone needed it and you had it to give. That generosity is what makes this worth showing up for.

When help comes with invisible terms, it is not scene generosity. It is leverage wearing a kind face.

You are allowed to love the magic and protect yourself at the same time. They are not opposites. They are the same move.

And here's the Kandi Trade — the vow.

- I will not confuse warmth for safety.
- I will not confuse access for friendship.
- I will not confuse help for love when the help comes with a tab.
- I will not let one person hold all six domains.
- I will not call it drama when the pattern, the intent, and the refusal to change are all present at once.
- I will document boringly and speak specifically.
- I will give real help when I have it to give, with no receipt, no balance sheet, no future invoice.
- I will remember that the person who moves first after a tragedy is usually the person who caused it.
- And I will stay in my clean T-shirt, with my actual face out, and I will know what I am holding and why.
