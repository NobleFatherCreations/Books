## The Soundcheck — The Domain Audit Drill

This is the first drill. Do not make it dramatic. Do not light incense. Do not announce to camp that you are “calling your power back” unless you want the group chat to start molting.

Open a boring note on your phone. Title it something no Wook will click. Suggestions: Packing Cubes. Sourdough Starter Notes. Mileage. Tent Repair. Receipts 2026. Hydration Plan.

List six domains. For each one: Who has access? Who has control? What is my backup?

Domain 1 — Transportation. Who has the keys? Can you leave without the person you came with? Do you have gas money, rideshare money, shuttle info, or a default-world contact who knows where you are?

Domain 2 — Shelter. Who knows where you sleep? Can someone remove you from camp? Do you have a backup plan — friend, motel, sober space, staff contact?

Domain 3 — Money. Who owes whom? Who controls the Venmo loop? Who paid for the ticket, booth, gas, room, wristband? Are terms written down?

Domain 4 — Substances/Medicine. Who is supplying? Who is pressuring? Who is testing? Who knows your medications, allergies, limits, or recovery status? Can you say no without losing belonging?

Domain 5 — Communication. Who has your phone? Who knows your passcode? Who controls the group chat narrative? Who has your passwords, socials, or artist page?

Domain 6 — Career/Access. Who controls your gigs, slots, intros, green room access, vendor placement, or stage time? Are they making you more independent or more dependent?

If any domain has one answer for access, control, and backup — one person, one path, no redundancy — that is where a Wook, if one is nearby, will find the lever.
