## The Sunrise Set + The Kandi Trade (closer)

Here's the Sunrise Set. Zero slang — just the true thing. The festival at its best is a place where a retired English teacher named Marlene can put a blanket over a stranger who fell asleep in an open tent flap, and where that stranger can wake up to tea at five a.m. in a field in California, and where the field is full of people who came to be in something together.

That is the real thing. That is worth protecting.

The beige RV is not a comment on the real thing. It is a parasite on the real thing — it exists because the real thing produces a population of people who are warm and trusting and not counting asks and not checking curtains and not requiring witnesses because the baseline assumption of the space is that everyone here showed up for the music.

Most of them did.

The drawn curtains are the tell.

The two-minute response time is the tell.

The “come inside, AC’s on” is the tell.

You can love the magic and still read the tells. You can be the kind of person who puts a blanket over a stranger in an open tent and still notice when the curtains are drawn and the warmth arrived without friction. Both at once. That is the whole skill.

The hat was taken for reasons that nobody will ever fully explain.

The hat doesn’t matter.

The geometry does.

And here's the Kandi Trade — the vow.

- I will do transactions in the open. Every time.
- I will bring a witness. “Hold on, let me grab my friend” is in my vocabulary now.
- I will map the friend-of-a-friend chain before I follow it to row 14.
- I will lock the box before the headliner. The phones. The keys. The cash.
- I will leave one person at camp.
- If something feels slightly off, I will say so out loud.
- I will document immediately and pass the information through the network.
- I will remember that drawn curtains in the heat at eleven p.m. are information, and information is free, and acting on it costs thirty seconds, and not acting on it costs everything in my pockets and also the hat.

PROTECT THE
F*CKING MAGIC
