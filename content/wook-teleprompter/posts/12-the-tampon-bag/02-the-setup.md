🎛️ THE TAPERS’ SECTION

Criminal Liability · Constructive Possession · Coercive Control at the Gate

Brief. Then directly to what you need to know.

Constructive possession is the legal concept that applies when you are found in proximity to a controlled substance even if you did not own it, purchase it, or know its exact contents. In most US jurisdictions, being the driver or a passenger in a vehicle where contraband is found creates presumptive constructive possession of that contraband, rebuttable by evidence. “I didn’t know it was there” is a defense, but it is a defense you have to make in court, not a conversation stopper at the gate.

Coercive control applied to the mule situation: the architecture of the Borrowed Mule is a coercive control pattern applied to a logistical situation. The primary relationship is used to activate loyalty and social compliance. The ask arrives through the trusted person. The person who will bear the consequence is the person least able to refuse. The risk is distributed downward through trust. This is not always intentional — some of the time the person asking genuinely believes “they don’t search girls’ stuff” and is not consciously engineering the liability. The outcome for Nadia is the same regardless.

**Your rights at the gate:** You have constitutional rights at a festival gate. You are not at the border — the fourth amendment applies. The specific application varies by whether you are entering on public or private land, whether there is a valid search warrant, and whether there is probable cause (the dog). Know your rights before you need them, not from the back seat while the dog is sitting.
