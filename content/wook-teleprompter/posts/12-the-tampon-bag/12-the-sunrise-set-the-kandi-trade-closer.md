## The Sunrise Set + The Kandi Trade (closer)

Here's the Sunrise Set. Zero slang — just the true thing. You are allowed to say no at the gas station.

You are allowed to say no in the car. You are allowed to say no in the gate line. You are allowed to say “pull over before the gate” and get out and stand ten feet from the car and not know what happens next. You are allowed to not be the person who carries this.

The people who tell you that you are the safe carry, the people who rehearse sentences in case you’re not, the people who bond out at eleven p.m. while you spend the weekend in a processing tent — they are not your people. Your people are the ones who would pull over. Who would not have asked in the first place. Who, if they needed to ask, would have asked honestly, with the full information, and let you make the real choice.

You are allowed to make the real choice. Not the choice that keeps the peace at the gas station. The real one.

The real one sometimes requires a conversation that feels harder than the yes.

The real one sometimes means you drive separately.

The real one sometimes means you find out what the relationship actually is made of.

That is not the worst thing.

The worst thing is the folding chair in the processing tent and his Instagram story at nine-forty-seven p.m.

And here's the Kandi Trade — the vow.

- I will ask what it is specifically.
- I will ask what happens if I’m caught with it.
- I will pull over before the gate.
- I will not carry what is not mine.
- I will not be in a vehicle with something I did not agree to carry.
- I will know my rights. I will say nothing except: “I don’t consent. Am I detained. I want a lawyer.”
- I will track the reciprocity. If I am the only one driving, I will look at the passenger seat.
- I will remember that the sentence someone has ready for law enforcement is the evidence that the plan existed before the event.
- And I will be the person who pulls over.
