## The Fanny Pack (chapter recap)

Chapter 27. The one that has the printed sheet.

**The 3-Tier Anchor System:** Inner (knows your state, your dose, your code word) · Outer (knows the Inner Anchor’s number and the meet-up spot) · Default-World (knows where you are and when to call for help).

**The Pre-Show Crew Briefing:** Eight beats. Fifteen minutes. Government names. ICE contacts. Physical meet-up spot. Interval. Code word. Escalation threshold. Wristband photos. Buddy assignment. Before the gates open. Non-negotiable.

**The Code-Word Protocol:** One sentence. Says one thing: “Come get me, no questions.” Not watermelon. Something specific to the person. Practiced before it is needed.

**The Anchor Backup Plan:** Designated backup. Camp lead or safety contact. Festival infrastructure. Rotation on the printed sheet. Anchor does not do four consecutive days.

One anchor is a person. Two anchors is a system. Three anchors is a culture. Sister Lou’s camp is a culture.
