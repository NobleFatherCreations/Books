## The Sunrise Set + The Kandi Trade (closer)

Here's the Sunrise Set. Zero slang — just the true thing. The magic does not protect itself.

The magic is real. The music is real. The sunrises are real. The connections formed at five in the morning over cold coffee next to strangers who have become, over the course of a weekend, something more specific than strangers — those are real. The scene produces something that nothing else produces, and it has been producing it for fifty years, across generations of people who showed up with generators and gaff tape and a willingness to sleep in the dirt because the thing that would happen when they did was worth it.

The magic does not protect itself.

It has been protected, for all of those fifty years, by people with clipboards. By Sister Lou at seven fifty-nine, clicking the pen. By Hugo taking the three a.m. watch. By Beans at the water station eating a granola bar and watching for the boots with no scuffs. By the harm-reduction volunteer who stayed past their shift. By Mara Hot Water running a conversation at four in the morning with someone who needed it. By Wingnut under the shade structure looking for the clevis pin. By the person who wrote the napkin. By the person who answered the eleven a.m. phone call from a stranger asking about the Lantern Family. By Rosa photographing the wristband and saying “I know, it’s boring, but send it to the chat.”

The magic is protected by the people who are willing to be boring about it. Who run the briefing. Who set the ICE contacts. Who carry the Narcan. Who stay sober when it is their turn to stay sober. Who say government names aloud into a circle at seven fifty-nine while the music is about to start and the dust is already rising and there is something extraordinary about to happen and the boring thing is what has to happen first.

The boring thing is the most extraordinary thing.

The boring thing is the whole chapter.

And here's the Kandi Trade — the vow.

- I will run the briefing.
- I will say the government names and I will mean them and I will hold them.
- I will set the ICE contacts before the car is unpacked.
- I will name the meet-up spot and describe it specifically enough to find in the dark.
- I will choose the code word and speak it aloud so it lives somewhere muscle-memory-adjacent.
- I will assign the anchor explicitly and both people will say yes.
- I will treat the anchor role as a job and honor the Anchor’s Bill of Rights.
- I will build the backup and name the rotation on the printed sheet.
- I will be the boring meeting.
- Because the boring meeting is what lets the magic happen.

PROTECT THE
F*CKING MAGIC
