## The Soundcheck — The Full Briefing Drill

You have run this drill before in abbreviated forms in Chapters 11, 15, 16, and 18.

Here it is in its complete version. Before your next event. With everyone in the car.

Government names. Around the group. Person on the left repeats it back.

ICE contacts. Everyone sets them. Everyone shows their lock screen. Everyone puts their phone away.

Physical meet-up spot. Named. Described. Every crew member confirms they can picture it.

Check-in interval. Named. Agreed to. Timer set on at least one person’s phone.

Code word. Selected. Spoken aloud by everyone. Once.

Escalation threshold. Named. “Four hours since contact and not at the meet-up spot: medical tent.”

Wristband photos. Taken. Sent to group chat. Chat confirms receipt.

Buddy assignment. Named. Aloud. Both people say yes.

Inner Anchor named. Aloud. That person says: “I’m on anchor duty.”

Outer Anchor named. Introduced to Inner Anchor if they don’t know each other.

Default-World Anchor contacted. Text sent. Confirmation received.

Fifteen minutes.

Every event.

This is what the village looks like when it is not asleep.
