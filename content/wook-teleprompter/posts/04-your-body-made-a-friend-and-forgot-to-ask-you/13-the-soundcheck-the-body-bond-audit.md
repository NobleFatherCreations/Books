## The Soundcheck — The Body Bond Audit

One person. The one your body is loud about.

Open a boring note. Title it Laundry. Soup. Mileage. Hydration. Receipts. Whatever you will not click on accidentally.

Answer these five questions. Actual answers. Not the answers you want to give.

1. When did the bond form? Sunrise. Crisis. Bad trip. Grief. Sexual encounter. Cuddle puddle. Hot springs. During comedown. While isolated. While altered. After they rescued you.

2. What state were you in? Altered. Exhausted. Lonely. Grieving. Scared. Euphoric. Post-peak. Touch-starved. Socially isolated. Post-conflict.

3. What did they give your body that it was hungry for? Safety. Touch. Attention. Relief. Praise. Protection. Belonging. Rescue. Intensity. Permission. Escape.

4. What did they ask for in return? Sex. Secrecy. Loyalty. Labor. Money. Silence. Access. Defense. Distance from friends. Forgiveness without repair.

5. What happens when you say no?

**Green:** They respect it, stay kind, adjust, don’t punish, don’t recruit others.

**Yellow:** They get quiet, cold, hurt in a way that makes you feel responsible, or start the slot machine cycle.

**Red:** They escalate, punish, isolate, use the Phantom against you, or make the bond itself the argument for why you can’t leave.

Look at your answers. Not with judgment. With the same attention he should have brought to you.
