## The Sunrise Set + The Kandi Trade (closer)

Here's the Sunrise Set. Zero slang — just the true thing. Your body is not wrong to want safety.

Your body is not wrong to want to be held through the hard part. To want someone to say stay with me, you’re safe, I’ve got you. These are not weaknesses. They are the things humans need. They are real needs and they deserve real meeting.

The scene, at its best, creates conditions for that meeting to happen — genuine, mutual, with care and without claim. The cuddle puddle that asks first. The harm-reduction volunteer who helps and then hands you back to yourself. The friend who stayed until the peak passed and then walked you back to camp and that was the whole thing. No invoice. No mythology. Just: I was here, you are okay, you can go now.

That version of care is real. It exists. Protect it by knowing the difference.

Your body made a bond under specific conditions. That bond is data about those conditions, and data about your real needs, and data about what you are hungry for. It is not a verdict on whether the person is safe. It is not a prophecy about where this goes. It is not a debt you owe.

You are the one who has to live in your body for the rest of your life.

Give it evidence. Give it time. Give it new safe experiences and people who show up in the gray hours and the Tuesday hours, not just the sunrise hours.

The sunrise hours are real. They are not the whole day.
