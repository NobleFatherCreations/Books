## The Sunrise Set + The Kandi Trade (closer)

Here's the Sunrise Set. Zero slang — just the true thing. Jess woke up in Marlene’s tent at 5:14 in the morning and a retired English teacher made her chamomile and they talked for an hour about showing up for the things you love.

That is a good story.

That is the story this chapter is trying to give you — the one where Jess wakes up fine and the chamomile is good and the worst thing that happens is four of your friends don’t sleep, which is bad but survivable.

The preparation gives you Jess’s story.

The lack of preparation gives you the other story — the one the chapter leaves in the space between the four hours and the five a.m., the one this book does not write in full because it does not need to be written in full for you to understand what it costs.

Fifteen minutes.

Government name. ICE contact. Wristband photo. Physical meet-up spot. Code word. Threshold.

That is all it is.

That is how you love your people here.

Not by trusting that it will be fine.

By doing the fifteen minutes so that “fine” has a backup plan.

And here's the Kandi Trade — the vow.

- I will say my government name out loud in the car.
- I will enter the ICE contacts and set the lock screen before we get out of the car.
- I will name the physical meet-up spot and describe it well enough to find without a phone.
- I will charge the portable battery before I charge anything else.
- I will take the wristband photo and send it to the group chat before I take any other photo.
- I will name the threshold before I need it.
- I will run the briefing because the briefing is love in the form of fifteen minutes.
- I will be the Noor who says “should we do the thing” and I will keep saying it until someone says yes.
- I will not let the enthusiasm of the drive suppress the instinct that protects everyone.
