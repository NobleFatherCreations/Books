## The Soundcheck — The Media Literacy Drill

Pick any piece of content you have consumed in the last forty-eight hours: a post, a podcast, a book chapter, a speech, a pitch, a workshop.

Run it through the sixteen.

Not to find a Wook. To find the architecture.

Which of the sixteen is present? Which is not?

Does the architecture change your evaluation of the content?

Or does the content remain useful regardless of the architecture?

Both answers are valid.

The question is the practice.
