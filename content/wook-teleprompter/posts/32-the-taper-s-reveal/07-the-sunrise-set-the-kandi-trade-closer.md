## The Sunrise Set + The Kandi Trade (closer)

Here's the Sunrise Set. Zero slang — just the true thing. You were worked by this book.

You were also told the truth by this book.

Both are true.

The disclosure does not undo the working. The working happened. The disclosure makes it visible, which is a different thing.

You are allowed to trust a source that admits its methods. You are also allowed to be wary of sources that use transparency as a final persuasion move. Both are appropriate responses to this chapter.

The scene you have been reading about for thirty-one chapters is also like this. You know.

You have known since Chapter 2.

You know it about the scene.

You know it about this book.

The knowing is the gift.

The knowing does not protect you automatically. It is not a shield. It is a flashlight.

The flashlight is what you came for.

You have it.

Use it well.

- I know I was worked.
- I know which moves were used.
- I hold the content and the architecture separately.
- I carry the flashlight into the next conversation, the next book, the next campfire, the next cold open.
- I ask: was I informed? Was I free to say no? Did the benefit flow both ways?
- I remember that persuasion is a tool and tools have functions and I get to decide what I do with the knowing.
- I am leaving this book more informed than I arrived.
