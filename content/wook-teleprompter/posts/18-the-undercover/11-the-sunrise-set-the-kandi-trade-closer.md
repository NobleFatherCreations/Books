## The Sunrise Set + The Kandi Trade (closer)

Here's the Sunrise Set. Zero slang — just the true thing. The community’s warmth is the thing that makes this worth everything.

The warmth at the water station. The cold brew from the nineteen-year-old assigned here by his auntie. The DanceSafe volunteer who knows your name by the third day. The harm-reduction tent where the guards drop because everyone there assumes everyone else is one of them.

That warmth is real.

That warmth is also a vulnerability that someone with a training manual and a body wire and a close rate has specifically targeted.

The chapter is not asking you to become cold.

The chapter is asking you to become specific.

Warm to the people you know and have verified. Warm to the camp member you have worked with. Warm to the harm-reduction volunteer who was there last year. Warm to the first-timer who is scared and needs an anchor. Warm to the person who asked once and accepted the answer.

Cold to the third ask.

Cold to the unverifiable mutual friend.

Cold to “at cost” from a stranger in no-scuff boots on Sunday.

The warmth is for the community. The cold is for the play.

Both protect the same thing.

And here's the Kandi Trade — the vow.

- I will count the asks. Two is the ceiling.
- I will verify the mutual friend before their vouching changes my answer.
- I will remember that “at cost” is the body wire moment and that the phrase is not protection.
- I will check for the scuffs. I will ask who they know. I will notice warmth that arrived without friction.
- I will hold the policy before the festival so the policy holds me during it.
- I will text the camp lead when someone asks three times.
- I will be warm to the community and specific with the warmth.
- I will remember that the community’s trust is the thing worth protecting and that protecting it is not paranoia — it is maintenance.
