## The Fanny Pack (chapter recap)

Chapter 18. The one you check before you talk to strangers.

**The festie code is four words:** We don’t sell to strangers. Not at cost. Not for the girlfriend. Not because Tyler vouched. Not to strangers.

The Three-Asks Pattern (persistence past no): Count asks. Two is the ceiling. After two the answer is no and the conversation is over regardless of the framing.

The Mutual Friend Lie (manufactured social proof): Verify or decline. “Can you text Tyler right now?” If the phone doesn’t come out, the friend doesn’t exist.

The “At Cost” Trap (colloquial-to-statutory gap): “At cost” is the body-wire moment. In legal terms, any compensation for a transfer is value. The phrase is not protection.

The Friendly Stranger Tell (manufactured rapport): Scuffs test. Music-before-logistics test. Network test. “Who else here knows you?”

Possession is paperwork. Distribution is a press release. Know which one you are one sentence away from.

The community’s warmth is worth protecting. Protecting it means not counting on it to save you from a standing policy you should have had.

🔰 SAFETY APPENDIX — CHAPTER 18
- The legal content that belongs in your pocket.
- YOUR RIGHTS — IF APPROACHED OR DETAINED
- These rights apply regardless of whether the person approaching you is undercover or not:
- You do not have to answer questions about where you are camped, what you have with you, or what you know about other people’s activities.
- “Am I being detained?” — If no: “I’m free to go.” Walk away.
- “Am I being detained?” — If yes: “I want a lawyer.” Then nothing.
- You do not have to consent to a search. “I don’t consent to a search” is a complete sentence.
- Lying to a federal agent is a crime. Remaining silent is a right. Remain silent.
- IF AN UNDERCOVER OPERATION PRODUCES AN ARREST
- Do not speak to law enforcement without an attorney present.
- Do not attempt to explain the context, the community, the gift economy, or the meaning of “at cost.”
- Contact a lawyer immediately. NORML, the ACLU, and several harm-reduction legal organizations maintain festival-specific legal hotlines.
- The entrapment defense has a very narrow application and requires evidence of inducement and lack of predisposition. Document everything you can remember about the approach: timing, locations, exact words, number of asks.
- THE FESTIE CODE
- Post this on your internal firmware before the event:
- We do not sell, trade, give, or transfer controlled substances to people we have not met before this event, regardless of claimed mutual connections, expressed distress, or offered price.
- This is a standing policy. It does not require case-by-case evaluation. It applies to every stranger. It applies even if Tyler is real. It applies even if the girlfriend is real. It applies because the cost of the false positive is an awkward conversation, and the cost of the false negative is a press release.
- THE VERIFY-THE-MUTUAL-FRIEND RULE
- Before any mutual-friend claim changes your behavior:
- 1. “Can you text them right now?”
- 2. Wait for the text to be sent in your presence.
- 3. Wait for the reply.
- 4. Read the reply.
- If the phone doesn’t come out, the friend doesn’t exist in any operational sense. If the phone comes out but the reply never arrives, the friend has been texted at a profile that takes forty-five minutes to respond because it’s monitored by a second officer in a Hampton Inn in Redding.
- Real friends reply fast.
