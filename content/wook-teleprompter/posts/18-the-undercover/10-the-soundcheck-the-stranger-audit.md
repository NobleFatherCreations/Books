## The Soundcheck — The Stranger Audit

Before the event. Set this policy with your crew. Out loud. Everybody hears it.

“At this event, we have a standing policy: no transfers to people we don’t know. Mutual friend vouching requires the mutual friend to text us directly. If anyone asks three times, we walk away on the second ask and log the encounter.”

Drill:

**Ask one arrives:** Answer as you see fit. Note the ask.

**Ask two arrives with new framing:** “I already answered this. My answer doesn’t change.” Log the second ask. Text one crew member.

**Ask three:** Does not get a third answer. Walk away. Text the camp lead. Text harm-reduction. The information is the response.

That is the drill.

The policy is the protection.

The policy does not require you to be paranoid. It requires you to have made the decision sober, before the festival, so that the sigh cannot close the sale.
