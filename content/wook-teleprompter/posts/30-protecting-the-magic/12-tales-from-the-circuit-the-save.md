## Tales From The Circuit — The Save

Montage POV. Same as the cold open. Different outcomes. The immune system landing.

One — The Signal Thread, Fourteen Months Later

Cara is in Portland. She has the briefing from Sister Lou and she has been running it for four months and she has the Signal thread with the six women and last week she got a text from someone she doesn’t know who was on the bus for one season four years ago and who found the thread through the same path the thread started on — one Instagram, one phrase that was specific to the vocabulary, one DM asking “are you okay.”

The thread is seven women now.

The seventh woman had a conversation with a harm-reduction legal clinic this week.

The harm-reduction legal clinic had three other cases.

The four cases are now in a coordinated consultation with an attorney who specializes in this.

The attorney is not promising outcomes. The attorney is promising that the four cases will be examined as a pattern rather than as isolated instances, which is what they are, and which is what was always required for the accountability that the individual cases could not produce.

The napkin is now four cases in a legal consultation.

The steadiness was always building toward this.

Two — The Subreddit Thread, Eleven Months Later

The person whose name was in the post has not been invited to perform at three festivals this year.

The person does not know why. The festivals have not explained. The conversations that produced the non-invitations were private and undocumented and the forty-seven responses on the subreddit are not, legally, evidence of anything.

They are evidence of forty-seven people who were alone becoming forty-seven people who know they are not alone.

They are also evidence of three festival programmers who read the thread and made quiet decisions that changed the lineup.

The thread is not justice.

The thread changed three lineups.

Three lineups changing is not nothing.

Three — The Missing Stair, Named

The new camp lead held the Walk-Through meeting.

It took four hours.

At two hours and twelve minutes, the meeting arrived at the specific conversation about the specific stair. The specific stair had been there since 2017. The routing had been running for seven years. The cost of naming it landed on everyone in the room simultaneously, which was: seven years of routing labor, and an unknown number of people who didn’t know the stair was missing.

**The meeting produced:** a documented concern, a direct conversation with the person, a behavioral agreement, and a one-year probationary status with specific checkpoints.

The stair is named.

The naming is not the repair.

But in seven years, this is the first time anything other than routing has happened.

Routing addressed nobody. The naming addressed one specific person in one specific camp in one specific circuit.

The naming is the repair beginning.

Four — The Medical Tent Volunteer’s Note, Forty-Six Thousand People Later

He received an email last month from the harm-reduction coordinator.

**The email said:** “The phone number you sent us nine months ago has now been connected to five adverse events across four festivals in three states. The public safety advisory we issued has been received by forty-six thousand people across our network. We do not know exactly how many situations the advisory prevented. We know it was received before the next festival season began and was distributed to harm-reduction teams at fourteen events. You should know that the note you kept changed what was possible.”

He printed the email.

He printed it and taped it next to the note labeled “work misc” in his phone, which he has now relabeled “keep.”

The note labeled “keep.”

The immune system does its work in the things you keep.
