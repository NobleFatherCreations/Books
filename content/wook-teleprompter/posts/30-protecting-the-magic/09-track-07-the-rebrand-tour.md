### TRACK 07 — THE REBRAND TOUR
*(reputation laundering / scandal migration)*

Here's the behavior. He is at a festival in a different state now.

They know him there too — but they know him as the new name, the new camp, the new aesthetic, the reinvented version of the person whose name was in the screenshot. The reinvention is not subtle because it does not need to be: the new circuit does not know the old circuit’s receipts, and the old circuit’s whisper network has not reached the new circuit because the whisper networks are not connected, and the subreddit thread is on a subreddit the new circuit does not read, and the eleventh response happened after he had already moved.

The Rebrand Tour is the Wook’s exploitation of the scene’s geographic and social fragmentation. Different regional circuits have different networks. Different festival types have different communities. The bass circuit and the transformational circuit have significant overlap in some cities and almost none in others. The Rebrand Tour moves through the gaps: from the circuit where the information has accumulated to the circuit where it has not.

The Tour does not require a dramatic incident to prompt the move. Some Wooks move proactively — before the information reaches critical mass, when the calculus indicates that the new circuit offers a better return on the operation. The move is sometimes described, to the new circuit, as “needing a change of scene” or “feeling called to this community” or, in the most sophisticated version, as an explicitly spiritual journey of reinvention.

Here's the read on it. When someone new arrives in your circuit claiming to have left another circuit: ask. Not accusatorially. As part of the normal onboarding of anyone new. “What was your previous community like? Who do we know in common? What brought you here?”

If the answers are vague in specific ways — if they are enthusiastic about the new circuit and unwilling to be specific about the old one — that vagueness is information.

Here's your counter-drop — run this. &rarr; The Cross-Circuit Check. Step 1 — When a new person arrives in your circuit: identify their previous circuit if one exists.

Step 2 — Reach out to one person from that circuit you trust. Not through the new person — independently. “Do you know [name]? I’m trying to get a sense of their community experience.”

Step 3 — The cross-circuit check takes one text and twenty-four hours. It does not require a full investigation. A pattern that has been documented in the previous circuit will usually surface in one or two conversations.

Step 4 — The absence of information is not clearance. Some circuits have no documentation infrastructure. The absence of a negative report does not confirm a positive history.

Step 5 — The check is proportionate to the role being offered. The new camp member requires less due diligence than the new practitioner with patient access, the new booker with artist access, or the new camp lead with authority.

Quick vibe check. People move between circuits for entirely legitimate reasons: geographic relocation, community growth, aesthetic evolution, the natural diversification of someone who has been in the scene long enough to have multiple homes. The cross-circuit check is not a presumption of guilt. It is the same due diligence that any community applies to new members with roles.

Turn it around — the mirror set. Have you ever facilitated a Rebrand Tour by providing a warm introduction to a new circuit for someone whose behavior in the old circuit you knew about? Have you ever withheld the information that would have prevented the tour from working? The tour requires a receiving community and it often requires someone to make the introduction.

Refractions — this lands differently depending on who you are. **Festival Bookers and Promoters:** The Rebrand Tour runs specifically in artist booking — the promoter who was removed from one circuit appearing as a “new to this scene” presence in another. The cross-circuit check for booking decisions is the same protocol. One conversation with someone from the previous circuit before the contract is signed.

This runs in every direction. The Rebrand Tour is available to any Wook regardless of identity — the guy run out of one circuit who resurfaces three states over, the vendor blacklisted from one row who sets up on another, the camp lead pushed out of one crew who founds a new one. The circuits that are most geographically or culturally isolated are the most attractive targets. Build the cross-circuit connections before you need them.

And Sober Tuesday — the morning-after truth. The executive who left one company after a misconduct investigation and appeared six months later at a competitor with a new title and the same behavioral pattern. The reference check that went to the people the executive chose rather than the people who witnessed the misconduct. The cross-circuit check is the reference check done independently.
