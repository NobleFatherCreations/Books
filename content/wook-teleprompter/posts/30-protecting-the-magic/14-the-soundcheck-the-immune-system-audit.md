## The Soundcheck — The Immune System Audit

Four questions. Run them annually. With your community.

1. The Routing Audit.

Name everyone in your community about whom there is an implicit routing. When did the routing start? What behavior produced it? What formal process has addressed it?

2. The Roster Audit.

Name anyone in your social network who operates as a Wook in your current understanding. What role are you playing in their operation? Flying Monkey, Validator, Bystander OG, or member of the Camp That Knew?

3. The Pathway Audit.

Test your festival’s or organization’s formal reporting pathway. Is it functional? Who reviews reports? What was the last outcome?

4. The Cross-Circuit Audit.

Name anyone who has recently joined your circuit from another. Have you run the cross-circuit check? If not: do so before the role being offered requires the vetting.

Four audits. Run them before each festival season.

The immune system strengthens through regular activation.
