## The Fanny Pack (chapter recap)

Chapter 8. Under the cooler.

Camps that say “we don’t have a hierarchy” have a hierarchy. You can tell because the people at the top say it the most.

The Camp Mom Power Grab (informal authority capture): Power accumulated through care-role competence. Diagnose by asking who inherits the keys.

**The Wristband Hierarchy (credential gating):** Access distribution is a loyalty metric when the criteria aren’t written down. Ask for the criteria.

The “We Don’t Do That Here” Soft Exile (norms enforcement as ostracism): The cooling is the governance. Name the temperature shift early.

The Group Chat Flying Monkey Squad (proxy harassment): When three people deliver the same message independently, ask where it originated.

The First-Season Funnel (newcomer lovebombing): Receive the welcome. Decline the “family” frame for one full season. Observe the labor distribution before contributing at intensity.

The PLUR Mask, Toxic Camp (façade management): Plain language is the mask detector. If vocabulary escalates in response to plain language, the vocabulary is the wall.

The Gift Economy Skim (sacred labor extraction): Follow the labor. Follow the credit. If the names are different, the skim is running. Set the labor cap in writing before the event.

The cartel survives by being the only camp in your life. The remedy is more camps. Better ones. Honest ones.
