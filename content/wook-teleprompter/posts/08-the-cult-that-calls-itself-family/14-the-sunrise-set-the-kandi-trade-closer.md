## The Sunrise Set + The Kandi Trade (closer)

Here's the Sunrise Set. Zero slang — just the true thing. Camps are one of the most beautiful things this scene has ever made.

They are the chosen family some of us never had in the families we were born into. They are the structure that makes a festival possible. They are the coffee at six a.m. that someone made for a stranger. They are the truck that arrived in the rain. They are the person who was just there at four a.m. on the worst night of your year and was not trying to get anything from you.

That is the real camp. That is what we are protecting.

This chapter is about the version that puts on the costume of the real camp and runs something else underneath. Where care becomes a monopoly. Where inner ring becomes a ladder. Where chat becomes governance. Where welcome becomes harvest. Where gift economy becomes a one-directional extraction that gets called mutual.

You are not asked to become suspicious of every camp.

You are asked to look at your camp and notice whether plain language about behavior produces real change. If it does, your camp is messy and alive and worth your labor and your loyalty and your Sunday mornings on your knees.

If it does not, the most loving thing you can do — for yourself, for the people next to you in the labor, for the next first-season arrival who is about to be told they are family — is to leave honestly and build something better twelve feet away.

The cartel survives by being the only camp in your life.

Build more camps.

And here's the Kandi Trade — the vow.

- I will name the hierarchy when I see it, including in the camps I love.
- I will ask who has the keys, who has the budget, who has the password.
- I will follow the labor and the credit and name it when they go to different people.
- I will set my labor cap in writing before the event.
- I will decline the “family” frame in season one and earn it over time.
- I will use plain language and watch what happens.
- I will walk twelve feet when someone needs water.
- I will be the call the next Pippa makes.
- And I will build something with a four-sentence manifesto that actually means all four sentences.

PROTECT THE
