## The Fanny Pack (chapter recap)

Chapter 3. Bottom compartment. Where the emergency cash is.

Day Three Brain is a feral raccoon operating a forklift. Do not let it sign things.

**The Glitter Filter (confirmation bias):** One good thing bedazzles twenty weird things. Ask what evidence would change your mind.

**The First Price Is The Spell (anchoring):** Who set that number? Why first? Reset from your own reference class.

**The Halo In The Pashmina (halo effect):** Does excellence at that thing actually predict safety in this thing? Require evidence on the specific thing.

**The Now Goblin (present bias):** The Twenty-Four Hour Lockbox. No major decisions until you have slept, eaten, and had natural light. Real opportunities survive your breakfast.

**The Sunk Cost Swamp (sunk cost fallacy):** Run the Forward-Only Ledger. Past investment is not a reason to continue. Close that tab.

The Attorney In The Hammock (motivated reasoning): Write the opposing brief. Find the evidence that would break your case. Do that first.

The Identity Force Field (identity-protective cognition): Your identity is not defined by whether this camp is safe. Ask what you would want if it were someone else’s camp.

**The Reverse Halo Mudslide (reverse halo):** Separate the person, the claim, the independent evidence. Evaluate three separately. Messy does not mean false.

The Source Amnesia Fog Machine (source amnesia): Build the receipt chain. Who specifically? When specifically? What did they witness directly? Fog cannot drive the van.

Knowing the levers does not save you from the levers. Only structure saves you from the levers.
