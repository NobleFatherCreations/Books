## The Soundcheck — The Day Three Decision Gate

Use this before any decision involving money, sex, substances, transport, housing, career, public accusation, public defense, secrecy, or signing anything with a pen found near a tea altar.

Score yourself across four checks:

Body Check

Slept six hours in the last twenty-four? Y/N

Eaten real food? Y/N

Had water and electrolytes? Y/N

Sober or clear enough to understand consequences? Y/N

Too hot, cold, spun, lonely, activated, or horny to be trusted with a legal pad? Y/N

**If two or more Body Checks fail:** no major decisions. You are in goblin mode. Goblin mode can dance. Goblin mode cannot consent to a business structure.

Pressure Check

**Is anyone using:** urgency, secrecy, group pressure, spiritual language, guilt, scarcity, flattery, manufactured authority, debt leverage, crisis framing, or altered-state bonding to move this decision?

**If yes:** slow down. Pressure does not automatically mean no. It automatically means: verify before proceeding.

Exit Check

Can you leave physically, socially, financially, and digitally?

*— Ride. Phone with battery. Friend who knows where you are. Cash. Staff contact. Password control over your own accounts.*

**No exit:** no yes. That is the whole rule.

Tuesday Check

Would you say yes to this at noon on Tuesday — rested, fed, sober, unflattered, wearing normal clothes, with rent due, after one conversation with a friend who is not inside the scene?

**If no:** wait. The magic can handle your breakfast. If the magic cannot handle your breakfast, the magic was a pressure tactic.
