## The Soundcheck — The Tuesday Before

Two days before they leave. Twenty minutes total, most of which is you doing something by yourself.

**Step 1 — Answer Bev’s question honestly, on paper, alone.** Would they call you? Write yes, no, or I don’t know. Nobody is going to see it. If it is not a clean yes, write one sentence about why, and be specific — the subject that produces a reaction, the tone, the time you changed the subject. Most parents know the answer immediately and have never let themselves write it down.

**Step 2 — Find your Bev.** Name the person you are going to take the fear to this weekend. Not your kid, and ideally not your kid’s other parent if that conversation tends to escalate. One friend, warned in advance: “I might call you Saturday night about nothing.” They will say yes. People love being useful in a bounded way.

**Step 3 — Have the one conversation.** Eleven minutes. Four things. No warnings. Then thank them and change the subject and mean it.

**Step 4 — Send the money on Thursday morning, not at the door.** At the door it is an emotional event. On Thursday morning, on a phone, with four words attached, it is infrastructure.

**Step 5 — Put two things in your phone:** the one friend’s number, and a reminder for three weeks out that says “week four text.” The second one is the step almost nobody takes and it is the one that compounds.

**Step 6 — Delete the article from your drafts.** Actually do it. Open the app, find it, delete it. You will not need it and at 1 a.m. on Saturday you will absolutely send it.
