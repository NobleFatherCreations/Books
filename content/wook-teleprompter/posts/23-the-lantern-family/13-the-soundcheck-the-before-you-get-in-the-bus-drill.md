## The Soundcheck — The Before-You-Get-In-The-Bus Drill

Before joining any traveling crew, residential program, or high-intensity community:

Five questions. One hour of research. Before you commit.

1. Find two people who left the group more than a year ago. Not who the group recommends. Find them yourself. Contact them. What did they say?

2. Ask the group: who has been here the longest and has since left? How does the group describe why they left?

3. Ask yourself: if I needed to leave tomorrow, what would I need to leave without? What does that list tell me about the exit cost?

4. Identify one person completely outside this group who knows where you are going and can reach you independently.

5. Set a check-in with that person: every two weeks, you contact them. If they don’t hear from you: they follow up. Not because something is wrong — because you built the thread before you needed it.

That is the drill.

One hour before.

The bus drives without you if you’re not ready.

Real families wait.
