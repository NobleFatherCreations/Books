## Tales From The Rest Stop — The Save

The Save is the cold open.

Ines drove home.

She put her phone face-down.

She stopped at the rest stop and read the message again and noticed the gap between the first reading and the second. She did not reply.

She opened the note on Monday night and titled it “festival stuff” and wrote the question and added “Answer this Tuesday.”

She answered it on Tuesday.

She texted Damian on Tuesday.

They had Thai food with Cam and scissors on the Sunday after, and the wristband went into the box, and the box has eleven wristbands in it now, and Damian is okay, and the proportion is right, and that is the whole save.

The save is not drama. The save is a note app and forty-eight hours and the specific relief of being a Tuesday person making Tuesday decisions instead of a Sunday person at two-twelve p.m. on the highway making decisions that will need to be managed through the week.

The watermelon moment is the phone going face-down.

Not dramatic. Not in the chest. Just: the hand putting the phone face-down on the seat, and the drive continuing, and the space that opens in the absence of the reply.

That space is Tuesday arriving early.

Tuesday comes for everyone.

Let it.
