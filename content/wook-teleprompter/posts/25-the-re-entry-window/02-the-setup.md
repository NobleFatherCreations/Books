## The Setup

Here's the soundboard quote for this chapter. “Nothing real is that urgent on Sunday afternoon. Real things survive Tuesday. Urgency that evaporates by Tuesday was always just the window talking. The window is real. What the window wants you to do is almost never what Tuesday-you would choose. The Tuesday person deserves to make Tuesday decisions. Give them the forty-eight hours.” — Sister Lou Mantilla. Fifty-eight. Twenty-three years in recovery. Has been running the briefing for eleven years and has been running the Tuesday Quarantine recommendation for twelve — the year before she formalized the briefing, after a conversation with someone who made a decision on the drive home and spent three months undoing it, she started telling everyone: “Not before Tuesday. Whatever it is. Not before Tuesday.” Has never once, in the subsequent twelve years, had to walk back that advice. Recorded at a closing circle on the last morning of a burn, when the population was packing up around her and the dust was doing its specific post-event thing, which is settle into every surface you did not protect.

Here's what this chapter is actually about. The re-entry window is not about Damian.

Damian is fine. Damian might be wonderful. Damian might be the beginning of something real or the beginning of a pleasant nothing or the beginning of something complicated that needs two full-capacity adults to navigate — any of these is possible, and none of them can be properly assessed at two-twelve p.m. on the drive home with the festival still in the body.

**The chapter is about the window itself:** what it is, what it does to decision-making, and who has learned to time their approach for it.

Four Tracks. The Comedown Text, the Urgency Insert, the Festival Feeling Loan, and the Wristband Commitment. Tuesday-you is the chapter.

🎛️ THE TAPERS’ SECTION

The Peak-End Rule / Kahneman · Re-Entry Stress / Transformational Experience Research · Tuesday Quarantine

Brief.

**Kahneman’s peak-end rule:** humans evaluate experiences based primarily on how they felt at the emotional peak and how they felt at the end — not on the duration or the average of the experience. The festival’s emotional peak is stored with disproportionate weight. The ending — the drive home, the emotional rawness, the openness — is the end. The peak-end rule means that in the re-entry window, your positive evaluation of the entire four-day experience is active and amplified and available as emotional collateral that anyone who contacts you is borrowing against.

Re-entry stress in transformational experience research: return to ordinary life after an intense communal experience produces a documented adjustment period during which participants report elevated emotional sensitivity, difficulty with ordinary tasks, and a strong desire to maintain the connections and states of the experience. The desire to stay connected to the festival’s warmth makes people in this window specifically receptive to contact from anyone associated with it.

**Tuesday Quarantine:** Sister Lou’s protocol. Not invented by Sister Lou — it is the harm-reduction application of basic behavioral decision science. Significant decisions made in the re-entry window have a documented higher rate of subsequent regret and revision than the same decisions made at baseline. Forty-eight hours. Not because Tuesday is magic. Because Tuesday is baseline.
