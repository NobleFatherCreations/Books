## The Mirror + The Discog

HAVE YOU BEEN THE WOOK? Mirror Check At The Rest Stop

The re-entry window mirror is quiet.

Have you ever timed a contact for someone’s drive home? Have you ever placed a deadline in the window because you knew the window would produce a yes that Tuesday might not?

Have you ever asked someone “while you’re still in it” specifically because you needed the in-it version to say yes?

Have you ever been the thing someone was reaching for in the window — aware that you were the thing they were reaching for, aware of what the window does, and not having said: “Wait for Tuesday. If you still want this on Tuesday, I’m here.”

The window belongs to the person in it.

Real things wait for Tuesday.

If what you want from someone doesn’t survive their Tuesday: examine what you wanted.

Here's the Wook Discog for this chapter. Studio Debut — The Baby Wook learning the moves

Twenty-three. Has noticed empirically that people are different after events than they are before them. Has not fully formulated what to do with this observation. Has once sent a significant text on the drive home from a festival and received a yes that became complicated by Thursday. Filed it as good timing. The architecture is forming before it is named.

Live Album — The Seasoned Operator

Thirty-one. Has the window mapped with the specific precision of someone who has run the drive-home contact across eight years and multiple relationships and has developed a template that is warm and specific and calibrated to arrive within three hours of the event’s end. Has not examined whether the timing is the mechanism. The Live Album does not need to examine the timing because the timing has always worked.

Greatest Hits Compilation — The Institutional Scaled-Up Version

The festival that sends the early-bird purchase link for next year’s event on Sunday afternoon while the attendees are in the parking lot breaking camp. The organization that holds the membership drive at the closing ceremony. The retreat center whose closing dinner includes the enrollment conversation for the next retreat. The Greatest Hits Compilation does not need a single person timing the approach. It needs a mailing list and a Sunday afternoon send time and the understanding that the people in the parking lot are in a thirty-six-hour window and the early-bird pricing expires at midnight.
