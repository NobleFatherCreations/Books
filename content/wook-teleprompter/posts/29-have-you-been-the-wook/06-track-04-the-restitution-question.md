### TRACK 04 — THE RESTITUTION QUESTION
*(amends in the action sense, not the word sense)*

Here's the behavior. The apology is a word.

Amends is an action.

**AA’s ninth step is not:** apologize to everyone you harmed. It is: make direct amends wherever possible except when to do so would injure them or others. The amends is the action that repairs the damage where repair is possible. The words are the beginning. The action is the accountability.

In scene contexts, the Restitution Question is: “What do I need to do that is different from what I have been doing?”

Not “what do I need to say.” What do I need to do.

**Sometimes the action is:** stop attending the same events as the person you harmed so they do not have to manage your presence while they are trying to heal.

**Sometimes the action is:** distribute credit and money and recognition that was owed and withheld.

**Sometimes the action is:** tell the truth to the camp network that was told a managed version of the story.

**Sometimes the action is:** cross-train the function you have been holding hostage so the camp can operate without you.

**Sometimes the action is:** stop distributing the untested batch.

**Sometimes the action is:** don’t go to the next festival for a season.

Sometimes the action is not dramatic. Sometimes it is: update the terms of the labor distribution so that the skim stops running. Sometimes it is: stop applying the three-asks pattern to people you find in the circuit. Sometimes it is: test the batch.

The restitution is specific. The restitution is actionable. The restitution is the changed behavior made concrete.

Here's the read on it. **For each inventory item:** what is the action that constitutes genuine amends, as distinct from the words that constitute the apology?

If the action and the words are identical — if the only amends you can offer is the apology — that is acceptable when the harm was a single instance with no ongoing dimensions. For pattern harm, there is usually a behavioral dimension to the amends that goes beyond the apology.

Here's your counter-drop — run this. &rarr; The Action Inventory. Step 1 — For each item on the honest inventory: what is the action equivalent of the apology?

Step 2 — Is the action in your power? There are some amends you cannot make — you cannot undo what happened, you cannot restore what was taken, you cannot remove the Encore from someone’s head. The action inventory is about what is in your power. Those things.

Step 3 — Is the action what the harmed person needs, or what you need to give? These may not be the same. Ask if you can.

Step 4 — Do the action before announcing the action. The announcement of the action is for you. The action is for them.

Step 5 — The ongoing action is the changed behavior. Every event you attend without running the pattern is the amends. The amends is not complete in one gesture. It is the continued changed behavior over time.

Quick vibe check. Some situations have no action available except the changed behavior going forward. The harm was done, the person is gone, the direct amends is not appropriate or not possible. In those cases: the changed behavior is the restitution, and it is available, and it is the thing the chapter is asking for.

Turn it around — the mirror set. Have you ever offered an apology while continuing the behavior the apology was about? Have you ever described yourself as accountable while the pattern was still running? The apology without the changed behavior is a speech, not accountability.

Refractions — this lands differently depending on who you are. **Camp Leads with Structural Harm:** The restitution for structural harm — the labor skim, the access hierarchy, the soft exile pattern — is structural. The word-level apology does not repair the structural harm. The changed structure repairs the structural harm. The new labor distribution. The restored access. The transparent budget.

**Touring Professionals:** The restitution for vendor row harm, for the LLC Trapdoor, for the unpaid labor — is the money. The words are not the money. The check is the money. Send the check.

This runs in every direction. The Restitution Question asks the same thing of the camp lead who took credit for someone’s idea, the vendor who underpaid a helper, the veteran who let a bad pattern slide for years, and anyone facing a different kind of harm. What action, not what words. The question applies universally.

And Sober Tuesday — the morning-after truth. The colleague whose idea you presented without credit. The apology is “I should have credited you.” The amends is crediting them now, publicly, in the context where the presentation was made. The words are the apology. The public credit is the amends.
