## The Soundcheck — The Mirror Audit

Open a note. Title it something boring.

For the most significant item on your inventory:

1. Name. Write it.

2. What I did. Specific behavior. One paragraph maximum.

3. What it cost them. What I know. What I don’t know.

4. Mistake or pattern? Honest answer.

5. Have I changed the behavior? Specifically. Yes, no, working on it.

6. Is the changed behavior driven by social risk or by actual change? The closed-door audit. Run it. Honest answer.

7. What is the action equivalent of the apology? What can I do, not just what can I say?

Seven questions. One item.

When you can read the entry back and feel something: the inventory is honest.

That is the whole drill.
