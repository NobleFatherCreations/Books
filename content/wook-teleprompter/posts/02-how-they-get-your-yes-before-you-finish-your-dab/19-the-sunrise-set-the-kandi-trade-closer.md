## The Sunrise Set + The Kandi Trade (closer)

Here's the Sunrise Set. Zero slang — just the true thing. Persuasion is not the enemy. Persuasion is how anything gets built or changed or shared. You have been persuaded by music, by kindness, by a clear argument, by beauty, by a person who told you the truth about something you needed to hear. Those are not corruptions of your judgment. They are how judgment works in the presence of other people.

The levers in this chapter become harmful when they are used without your knowledge, against your real interests, to get a yes that would not survive your full understanding of the situation.

That is the line.

You are allowed to be influenced. You are allowed to be moved. You are allowed to make decisions based on imperfect information in the moment, which is how all decisions are made.

You are also allowed to name the lever when you see it. You are allowed to slow down, ask for the written terms, take forty-eight hours, and bring a friend. You are allowed to say no to this specific offer while still saying yes to the scene, the magic, the community, and the music.

Those are not opposites. Those are the same act of care, turned different directions.

Take care of yourself. The magic needs you intact.

And here's the Kandi Trade — the vow.

- I will not let a gift become a debt without my knowledge.
- I will not let my prior yeses be used as evidence that my current yes is required.
- I will not let the room’s unanimity replace my own read.
- I will ask the one specific verifiable question.
- I will say no to one small thing and clock the temperature.
- I will wait forty-eight hours and ask for written terms on anything that has a comma in the dollar amount.
- I will translate every cosmic invitation into Tuesday English before I answer it.
- I will love this scene and also protect myself from this scene.
- I will remember that the offer that cannot survive my due diligence was never a real offer.
