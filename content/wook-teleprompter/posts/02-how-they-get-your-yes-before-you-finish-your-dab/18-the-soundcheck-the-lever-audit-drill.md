## The Soundcheck — The Lever Audit Drill

Do this in the next twenty-four hours. One note. Boring title. Sourdough Log. Insurance Notes. Packing List.

Think of the last time you said yes to something you later regretted.

Write three sentences:

**Sentence 1:** What were you given before the ask? (A favor, a gift, a compliment, a credential, a social frame, an emotional disclosure. Something. What was it?)

**Sentence 2:** What lever do you think was running? Pick from the twelve. Name it.

**Sentence 3:** What would you have said if the lever had not been running — if you had been rested, not indebted, not flattered, not isolated, not afraid of looking uncool?

That third sentence is your real answer.

Write it. Keep it.

If the real answer and the answer you gave are different — that gap is what this chapter is for. Not to make you angry about the past. To make the gap smaller next time.

The drill takes seven minutes. It works.
