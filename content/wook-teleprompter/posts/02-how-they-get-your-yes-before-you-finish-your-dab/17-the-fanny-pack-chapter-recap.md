## The Fanny Pack (chapter recap)

Chapter 2. Middle pocket.

A lever is a tool, not an evil. The shovel can plant a garden. It can also bury the receipts behind the art car.

**The Trifecta for every lever:** Was I informed? Was I free to say no? Did the terms change after I said yes?

**The Kandi Debt Racket (reciprocity):** Clean gift closes the loop. Wook gift opens one.

**The Ride-Or-Die Chokehold (commitment):** “That was then. I’m deciding this one fresh.”

**Everyone’s Rolling Face, So (social proof):** Find the one person not doing the thing. If you can’t find them, the room was curated.

**The Fake-Ass Laminate Flex (authority):** Ask one specific verifiable question. The pivot is your answer.

**The Insta-Wook Bond (liking):** Say no to one small thing. Measure the warmth temperature after.

**The Secret Set Sucker Punch (scarcity):** Any offer that can’t survive forty-eight hours and a request for written terms is telling you something.

**The Cosmic Co-Sign (spiritual bypass):** Translate every cosmic claim into Tuesday language. Answer the Tuesday version.

**The Wook-To-Wook Bait (in-group):** Shared identity doesn’t mean aligned interests. Get the terms in writing regardless.

**The Trauma Tax (disclosure asymmetry):** Holding someone’s pain doesn’t obligate you to their current ask.

**The Mirror Trick (identification grooming):** Introduce one friction point. Real people disagree sometimes.

The “I’m Just Worried About You” Move (concern trolling): Ask for the specific information behind the concern. Feelings are not information.

The “If You Really Loved The Playa” Play (sacred duty): You can love the playa and also say no to this specific ask.
