## The Soundcheck — The Pre-Drive Conversation

Before any festival road trip. Out loud. With everyone in the car.

Five things. Fifteen minutes.

1. The Inventory. What is in the car. Explicitly. “The car contains [X]. Everyone in this car knows.”

2. The Driver. Who talks. One person. “If we get stopped, [name] handles it.”

3. The Four Sentences. Say them out loud together. Practice sounds silly. Practice is why the voice doesn’t shake.

4. The Consent Refusal. “We decline all search requests.” Agreed to explicitly by everyone.

5. The Escalation. “If a dog is called: I want to speak with an attorney. Nothing else.”

Fifteen minutes. Before you leave. Before the playlist starts. Before Carter has had his first rest-stop snack.

Brief Carter.
