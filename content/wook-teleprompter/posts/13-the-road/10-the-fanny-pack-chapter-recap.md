## The Fanny Pack (chapter recap)

Chapter 13. This pocket is for the road.

The Consensual Encounter Approach (Fourth Amendment bypass): Ask what you were stopped for before anything else. The thirty-second question that gives you the encounter’s structure.

**The Soft Ask (consent manufacturing):** “I don’t consent to a search.” Pre-decided. Specific. Polite. Final. Does not invite negotiation.

The Probable Cause Chain (justification escalation): Dog alert changes the rules. After the dog: “I want to speak with an attorney.” Then silence.

The Co-Passenger Information Leak (passenger override): Driver talks. One voice. Carter is briefed before the drive.

The Nervous Yes (fawn response to authority): Eighty-three percent say yes. The other seventeen percent pre-decided. Decide before the drive.

**The distributed carry decision:** four people in the car = four people in the legal situation. The inventory information belongs to everyone in the vehicle.
