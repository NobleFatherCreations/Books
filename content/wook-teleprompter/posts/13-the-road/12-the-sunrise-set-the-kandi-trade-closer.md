## The Sunrise Set + The Kandi Trade (closer)

Here's the Sunrise Set. Zero slang — just the true thing. The road is part of the journey.

Not poetically — logistically. The seven hours before the gate are as much a part of the experience as the seven hours after it, and they carry risks that the festival’s harm-reduction infrastructure cannot reach, because the harm-reduction infrastructure is inside the gate and the corridor is before it.

The people in the car with you trusted you enough to get in. They trusted you with their weekend and their presence and their name in a vehicle that has your name on the registration.

That trust comes with one obligation that costs fifteen minutes and produces the specific ease of arriving at mile marker 238 with the answer already written.

Tell them what they are in.

Give them the choice.

Give them the sentences.

Let them read the guide.

Let the warmth of the cop at the window be just a warmth that a person can have, separate from the decision you pre-made, separate from the legal encounter you walked through with your eyes open, separate from the seven-hour drive that ends at the gate and then four days of something extraordinary.

Brief Carter.

Then drive.

And here's the Kandi Trade — the vow.

- I will have the conversation before the drive, not during it.
- I will tell the people in my car what they are in.
- I will know the route and the corridor.
- I will pre-decide my answer to the soft ask.
- I will brief Carter.
- I will let the driver talk.
- I will know: I can say no.
- I will remember that the pre-decided answer is the whole protection.
- I will get there.
