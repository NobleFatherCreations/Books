## The Mirror + The Discog

HAVE YOU BEEN THE WOOK? Mirror Check At The Rest Stop

Carter is you, at some point.

Not necessarily in a car — in every situation where you have been the person who has never been told and who therefore answered the warm question completely, or the person who said yes because the no felt rude, or the person who brought people into a situation that contained information they did not have access to.

Have you ever driven people to a festival without telling them what was in the vehicle? Have you ever assumed that the conversation was unnecessary because things have always been fine? Have you ever been the person who consented to something on behalf of four people without the four people’s input?

The distributed carry decision is the ethical obligation of anyone who has information about the vehicle that the other occupants do not.

Have the conversation.

Before the drive.

Fifteen minutes.

Brief Carter.

Here's the Wook Discog for this chapter. Studio Debut — The Baby Wook learning the moves

Twenty-two. First festival drive. Has not thought about the road as a risk environment. Has a backpack and some things in it and three friends who know roughly what the weekend entails but have not been briefed. Gets stopped for a real traffic violation. The officer is warm. Carter talks. He says yes. The afternoon goes differently than planned. Files it as bad luck.

Live Album — The Seasoned Operator

Twenty-eight. Has developed what he calls being careful — specific car configuration, no visible paraphernalia, route awareness, practiced casual answer to festival questions. Has not briefed the people he drives with because the briefing is awkward and everything has always been fine. The Live Album runs clean until the night someone in the backseat answers a question he’d prepared an answer to, and the answer they give is different from his, and the inconsistency produces the next step. Files it as bad luck. The bad luck has a name.

Greatest Hits Compilation — The Institutional Scaled-Up Version

The festival-adjacent rideshare group. The Facebook group that coordinates rides from the city to the event. The shuttle service run by the promoter’s friend who has been doing this since 2017. Never once has any of these platforms mentioned what the drivers may or may not be carrying, what the passengers should or should not say if stopped, or what their rights are at the stop. The greatest hits compilation is the system that moves thousands of people without a single pre-committed conversation. It is not malicious. It is the absence of fifteen minutes of care, scaled to a network.
