### TRACK 05 — THE WHITEPAPER SQUINT
*(jargon-based authority manufacture)*

Here's the behavior. This one arrives with a document. Not a contract — a whitepaper. Or a “vision deck.” Or a “brand manifesto with embedded economic architecture.” Or, in the specific variant that has been running since approximately 2018, a blockchain-based artist platform “tokenizing creative labor on a regenerative ledger” that will “redistribute value in the conscious economy” through “a DAO structure that gives founding artists governance rights” in exchange for minting their work as NFTs on a platform the Wook controls.

The Whitepaper Squint is the deployment of technical or financial jargon to manufacture authority and suppress the question “but is this actually a good deal.” The jargon works because it makes asking for clarification feel like admitting ignorance, and admitting ignorance in front of an enthusiastic founder feels socially costly. Better to nod and squint slightly at the whitepaper as if you understand the tokenomics.

You do not understand the tokenomics.

Neither does he.

The whitepaper was generated with significant AI assistance and then printed at a FedEx in Portland at seven a.m. on Thursday before the festival.

Here's the read on it. Request the plain-English version of the deal: “Can you explain in one paragraph what I give, what I get, and when I get it?” If the paragraph takes six minutes to produce and includes the words “regenerative,” “tokenized,” “co-ownership,” or “governance rights” without specifying a dollar amount and a date — the plain-English version does not exist because the deal does not exist in plain English.

Here's your counter-drop — run this. &rarr; The One-Paragraph Test. **Step 1 — Say:** “I love the vision. Can you explain what I specifically give, what I specifically receive, and when I receive it, in one paragraph without jargon?”

Step 2 — Wait for the paragraph.

Step 3 — If the paragraph contains no numbers, no dates, and no specific deliverables: “This isn’t specific enough for me to decide. When you have the specific terms in writing, I’m happy to look at it with my advisor.”

Step 4 — Leave.

Step 5 — If you received a whitepaper, take it to someone who actually works in financial technology or blockchain and ask them to evaluate the structure. The evaluation will take twenty minutes and the results will be clear.

Quick vibe check. Real decentralized creator platforms exist. Real artist tokenization models exist. Real cooperative economics exist. The test is the same as every other Track: can the terms be stated plainly, does the compensation formula produce real numbers, and does the entity have an independently verifiable track record?

Turn it around — the mirror set. Have you ever used jargon primarily to sound authoritative rather than to communicate accurately? Have you ever described a speculative asset as “an opportunity” without disclosing that the asset’s value depends entirely on market conditions you do not control? Have you ever benefited from someone’s reluctance to admit they did not understand what you were pitching?

Refractions — this lands differently depending on who you are. **Artists New to Business:** The whitepaper is designed for you specifically. People with professional business training read it and ask the plain-English question immediately. People who have not been in business settings before are socialized to treat formal documents as authoritative. The formality is the con.

This runs in every direction. The Whitepaper Squint is run across every gender and identity, and the jargon shifts to match the audience: crypto-adjacent jargon for tech-adjacent crowds, regenerative economics language for sustainability-adjacent crowds, DAOs and governance for political-movement-adjacent crowds. Same move, different vocabulary.

And Sober Tuesday — the morning-after truth. The investment prospectus that is sixty pages long, uses fourteen technical terms in the first paragraph, and buries the fee structure in section eight. Length and formality as a substitute for transparency. The squint is the same.
