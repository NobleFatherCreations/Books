## The Soundcheck — The Vendor Economy Audit

Before your next festival or professional engagement in the scene, run this in a boring note. “Inventory. Budget. Receipts.”

1. What does this engagement cost me? Booth fee, transport, inventory cost, lodging, food, labor hours. Specific dollar amounts. No estimates.

2. What is my break-even? At what revenue number do I cover my costs for this engagement? Write the number.

3. What is the market rate for my contribution? If I were being paid fairly for this specific work at this scale, what would that look like? Compare it to what is being offered.

4. Who controls the inventory? Where is my stock during the event? Who has access? What is the retrieval process if the relationship changes?

5. Is there a document? What does it say in plain English? What do I give? What do I get? When? Have I had it reviewed?

6. Am I owed anything? From the last engagement, the last collaboration, the last shared truck, the last slow pay. Add it up. Total it. That total is information.

**If any answer is worse than you expected:** use the pocket scripts above. The scripts are awkward in the moment. They are cheaper than nine months of free labor.
