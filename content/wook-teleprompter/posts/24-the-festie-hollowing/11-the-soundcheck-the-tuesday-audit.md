## The Soundcheck — The Tuesday Audit

No festival paraphernalia required. Just Tuesday and your honest answers.

Five questions. One pen or one note.

1. The Camera Roll Question.

How many photos in your camera roll are from festivals? How many are from the rest of your life? What is the ratio? No judgment on the number. Just: what is the ratio?

2. The Contact List Question.

How many people in your first thirty contacts are people you met outside the festival scene? Name them. If the list is short: that is the information.

3. The Want Question.

Right now, on a Tuesday afternoon, name three things you would want to do that are not festival-adjacent. Three. Specific. If naming them takes more than two minutes: the inventory has been depleted. That is the information.

4. The Vocabulary Question.

Pick a word or phrase you use regularly in the scene. Now explain what it means to someone who has never been to a festival, in plain Tuesday language. If you cannot: that is a data point about how the vocabulary has replaced rather than supplemented your existing language.

5. The Translation Question.

When was the last time you talked to someone outside the scene about something that mattered to you — not what you told them about the scene, but something else? If you cannot remember: that is the information.

Five questions. Fifteen minutes. No score. Just the information.

The information is where the reconstruction starts.
