## The Sunrise Set + The Kandi Trade (closer)

Here's the Sunrise Set. Zero slang — just the true thing. You are allowed to love this.

You are allowed to love it completely and passionately and with the full weight of everything it has given you: the community, the beauty, the specific kind of knowing that comes from being with people in extraordinary circumstances, the sunrise that is different from every other sunrise because of where you were and who you were with and what you had just survived together.

You are allowed to love it and also to be someone outside of it.

The scene, at its best, does not ask you to choose. The scene, at its best, wants the full person — the one who cooks badly on Tuesdays and calls her sister and has a therapist and reads books that have nothing to do with bass music and remembers the band she liked before she discovered the thing she loves.

The full person is a better festie than the Hollowed one. The full person has more to bring to the rail. The full person has the resources to be genuinely present because there is a life to return to that makes the festival, each time, a choice rather than a default.

The room you are afraid to go back to is still there.

It does not smell like a festival.

It is yours anyway.

Go find the windows.

And here's the Kandi Trade — the vow.

- I will cook something on a Tuesday.
- I will call the person I have not called in a while.
- I will take the photo of myself in regular clothes and put it somewhere I can find it.
- I will do the Smaller Season Experiment. I will observe the actual loss, not the predicted one.
- I will translate the best thing about the scene into plain language and say it to one person who was not there.
- I will ask: am I choosing this, or is this the only remaining option?
- I will go looking for the self that was here before the scene. If it is still there, I will say hello to it. If it needs recovery, I will start the Tuesday Reconstruction.
- I will remember that loving the scene completely and being a person outside of it are not opposites.
- They are the same thing done right.
