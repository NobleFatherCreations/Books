## The Fanny Pack (chapter recap)

Chapter 24. The one that goes in the bag before you leave the apartment.

The Identity Erosion Drift (gradual self-concept replacement): What would you still be if you could not attend festivals for a year? That answer is the inventory of what remains outside the scene.

The “If You Leave, You’re Nothing” Lock (exit cost catastrophization): Test the hypothesis. The Smaller Season Experiment: two fewer events next year. Observe the actual loss versus the predicted loss.

The Belonging-As-Leverage Move (conditional acceptance): The Warmth Test. Reduce participation in one visible way. Note the temperature.

The Default-World Disgust Indoctrination (out-group contempt): Translate the best thing that ever happened to you at a festival into plain language. Try saying it to one person who wasn’t there.

**The Hollowing Spiral (identity foreclosure):** One non-scene activity, one non-scene relationship, one non-scene interest. Once a week. The floor is what remains when everything else is removed. The scene should not be the floor.

Six thousand photos of the same five festivals is not a portfolio. It is a question.

The room has windows. Go find them.
