## The Fanny Pack (chapter recap)

Chapter 33. The last one. The one that is also the first one from now on.

The five pillars, for the pocket:

**The Known Perimeter:** The full pre-event protocol, built before the lasers start. Government names. ICE contacts. Wristband photos. Physical meeting spot. Dose plan. Anchor assignment. Non-transfer rule. Lockbox. Code word. Default-world anchor. Fifteen minutes. Every event.

**The Vetted Community:** Three to six people. Trust built through time, conflict, and repair — not through warmth-without-friction. Maintained deliberately. Outside your supply chain, housing, and income.

**The Identity Floor:** Two non-scene interests, actively engaged. One non-scene peer relationship, actively maintained. One default-world context in which you are competent and known. Weekly. Consistent. Small.

**The Accountability Practice:** Post-event behavioral scan. Quarterly closed-door audit. The note in the phone titled something boring. Fifteen minutes. Every event.

**The Passed-Forward Information:** The eleventh response. The cross-circuit check. The Signal thread node. The new festie briefed at the gate. The information passed when the passing costs less than the withholding. Which is almost always.

And the closing line, which is also the opening line, which is also the whole manual in five words:

Protect the fucking magic.
