## The Mirror + The Discog

HAVE YOU BEEN THE WOOK? The Last Mirror

This mirror is shorter than the others.

Thirty-two chapters of mirrors.

This one asks only one question.

Are you more of yourself than when you opened this book?

Not more defended. Not more suspicious. Not more armored.

More yourself.

The person who reads a book about manipulation and comes out of it smaller and more closed has learned the wrong lesson. The person who comes out of it larger — with clearer eyes, a fuller floor, a vetted community, a known perimeter, a practice of accountability, and the habit of passing things forward — has learned the right one.

The magic is real.

You are the village.

Are you more of yourself than when you started?

**If yes:** put the book down and go.

**If not yet:** this chapter is still here. The pillars are still here. The construction is available any time.
