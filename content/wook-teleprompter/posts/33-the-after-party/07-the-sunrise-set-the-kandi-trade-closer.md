## The Sunrise Set + The Kandi Trade (closer)

Here's the Sunrise Set. Zero slang — just the true thing. The bus is parked somewhere.

The lab with the no-test protocol is somewhere.

Hopper is at a different festival with a different kid and a different tank.

Both things are true about the scene. They have always been true. The book has been about both of them for thirty-two chapters and this final chapter is not about resolving the contradiction — the contradiction does not resolve, it just becomes something you can hold.

You can hold both.

You can love the magic and also see what eats it.

You can be the village.

The music is about to start.
