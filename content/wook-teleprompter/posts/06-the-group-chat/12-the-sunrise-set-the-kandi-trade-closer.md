## The Sunrise Set + The Kandi Trade (closer)

Here's the Sunrise Set. Zero slang — just the true thing. The group chat is one of the best things that has happened to this culture.

That is the honest starting point and it should not be buried at the bottom of a chapter about its failure mode. Before these groups existed, showing up alone meant showing up alone — no camp, no ride, no one to eat with, and a real chance of spending four days at the most communal event of your life feeling entirely outside it. The groups fixed that. People who would never have found each other now arrive with somewhere to be. Disabled attendees coordinate access in them. Sober people find each other in them. Whole camps that have run for a decade were assembled by strangers in an April thread.

The thing that makes them work is the same thing that makes them dangerous, and there is no version of this where you get one without the other. They work because people are generous to strangers there. That is it. That is the whole mechanism. Generosity to strangers, at scale, with no vetting, because vetting would kill the thing that makes it valuable.

So the answer is not suspicion. A scene where everyone treats the helpful person as a suspect is a worse scene, and it is also not a safer one, because the arrangement that actually protects people is not distrust — it is redundancy.

One person who knows where you are can become no people very quickly. Two cannot.

One ride home is a dependency. Two is a choice.

One relationship carrying your whole weekend is a structure that a decent person can be standing inside of without ever doing a single thing wrong, and the structure is still bad, and you can dismantle it in twenty minutes in March with a post that says *anyone else coming solo*.

The eleven weeks are not the enemy. Some of the best people you will ever know will arrive in your life through eleven weeks in a group thread, and they will be exactly who they seemed to be, and you will camp with them for the next fifteen years.

Ask for one name. Keep one thing written down. Bring a second person.

Then go be generous to strangers, which is the actual point of all of it.

And here's the Kandi Trade — the vow.

- I will ask for one name of a human who has actually camped with them.
- I will ask that human the only question that works: “Anything I should know?”
- I will count hours, not weeks, before I get in anyone’s vehicle.
- I will keep one channel logged, and the plan will live in it.
- I will not arrive anywhere knowing exactly one person.
- I will build the second anchor in March, when it costs a text instead of a night.
- I will notice when someone knows a great deal about me and I know a road name.
- I will stay generous to strangers in the thread, because that is the thing worth protecting, and I will remember that generosity and verification were never actually opposites — we just built the groups before we built the habit.

PROTECT THE
F*CKING MAGIC
