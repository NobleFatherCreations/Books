## The Soundcheck — The Crew Safety Brief

Run this before your next event. It takes fifteen minutes. You can do it in the car on the way, in the parking lot, or at the camp before the first night.

Open your phone. Start a note titled something boring. “Weekend plan. Camping list. Grocery run.”

Answer these. Out loud. With your crew.

1. Real names. Everyone says their legal name right now. Not their playa name. The name their mom calls them. Say it. The person on your left repeats it back.

2. ICE contacts. Phone unlocked. ICE-FIRST. ICE-SECOND. Medical ID visible on lock screen. Do it now. I’m waiting.

3. Meeting spot. The specific physical place you go if your phone dies. Name it. Point at it on the map. Everyone acknowledges.

4. Check-in interval. Four hours or every two hours on high-dose nights. Set calendar reminders now.

5. Code word. The sentence that means “come get me, no questions.” Agree on it. Say it to each other once so you both know what it sounds like.

6. Harm reduction locations. Where is the Zendo? Medical tent? DanceSafe? Name them. At least one person in the group should have them starred on their map.

7. The four words. “We don’t know what.” Say it out loud. Twice. Both of you. So it lives somewhere muscle-memory-adjacent.

Fifteen minutes. Every event.
