## The Sunrise Set + The Kandi Trade (closer)

Here's the Sunrise Set. Zero slang — just the true thing. The cereal aisle is not the festival.

This is, at four-thirty in the morning when the Encore is running, the most important sentence in the chapter, and it is the least satisfying one, because the festival is the one that made everything feel real and the cereal aisle is the one that currently exists.

**Here is what is also true:** the cereal aisle is real too. The soup is real. The voice note from Junie is real. The first session with the therapist is real. The five-sense ground that settled the sternum in seventy seconds is real. The timeline’s trajectory is real. The fact that many people have been through this version of this chapter and have landed is real.

The magic is not only at the festival.

The magic is in the soup made with milk instead of water.

The magic is in the no-need-to-respond text that makes you respond anyway.

The magic is in the ceiling being the ceiling, reliably, every morning.

The magic is in the five-sense ground that anchors you to the cereal aisle when the festival tries to pull you back.

The magic is portable.

It came home with you.

You are going to be okay.

Not tonight, necessarily. Tonight you are at four-thirty a.m. with the cereal and the Encore and the band of muscle between the shoulder blades.

Tonight you make it to nine a.m.

Nine a.m. is different.

And here's the Kandi Trade — the vow.

- I will name the voice and not argue with it.
- I will do the somatic inventory and trust what my body holds.
- I will invest in the active base and stop tracking the provisional.
- I will make the loss inventory and let the grief be grief.
- I will do one ordinary thing per day and let the ordinary things build the floor.
- I will eat something warm before deciding anything.
- I will do the five-sense ground when the cereal aisle tries to become the festival.
- I will have the resources in my phone before I need them.
- I will make it to nine a.m. and nine a.m. is different and the timeline is longer than I want and the trajectory is known.
- And I will send the no-need-to-respond text to the person who needs it.
