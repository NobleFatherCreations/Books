## The Soundcheck — The Emotional Ledger Drill

Pick one relationship in the scene that has an emotional charge — a camp person, a recurring figure, a community elder, anyone whose name changes your breathing slightly.

Open the boring note. “Laundry. Receipts. Tent Repair. Soup.”

Three questions. Honest answers.

1. What have they shared with you? Name the disclosures. The hard stories, the vulnerabilities, the cosmic frames, the concerns about your behavior or the behaviors of others. List them without judgment.

2. What have they asked for? Name the asks. Access, housing, labor, loyalty, silence, sex, money, your social capital, your presence in a conflict, your agreement about someone else. List them.

3. What happened when you said no to an ask? Specifically. Did they accept? Did they cry? Did they use vocabulary? Did they raise a concern about you to the group chat? Did the cosmic frame recalibrate to justify a second ask?

The pattern in your answers is the ledger. The ledger is not a verdict. It is information. Use it to decide what the next conversation requires.
