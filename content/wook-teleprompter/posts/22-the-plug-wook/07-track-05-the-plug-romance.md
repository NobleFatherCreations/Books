### TRACK 05 — THE PLUG ROMANCE
*(sex-for-supply / coercive intimacy)*

Here's the behavior. The warm supply relationship becomes warmer.

The texts arrive later in the evening. The “I got you” starts to have a quality of attention that the supply transaction does not require. The free extra appears alongside a compliment that has nothing to do with the product. The access escalates: not just the supply, but the green room, the private camp, the “I’d love for you to come by later and we could—”

The Plug Romance is the supply relationship repurposed as romantic leverage, where the supply line and the intimacy line run in parallel and close to each other and in proximity to each other and occasionally through the same door. Sometimes the romance is genuine and the supply relationship simply coexists with it and neither contaminates the other, and those relationships exist and they are not this Track.

The Track is when the supply is the leverage. When the warmer nights come after the warmer access. When the colder response follows the declined invitation. When the question “is this person interested in me or in what I can provide them” has the answer “both, and the both is structural.”

The Plug Romance is the Covert Facilitator from Chapter 1 applied to a supply relationship: the help that is real and that turns into a claim, where the claim is not money but intimacy. The intimacy is a form of the supply. The supply is a form of the intimacy. The chapter needs to say clearly that access to substances is not a form of consent to a romantic relationship, and that using supply access to create the conditions for romantic pursuit is coercive regardless of how warmly it is executed.

Here's the read on it. Ask whether the romantic interest existed before the supply relationship. Ask whether the access would change if the romantic interest were declined. If the answer to the second question is “probably yes” — the access and the interest are entangled, and the entanglement is the coercion.

Here's your counter-drop — run this. &rarr; The Lane-Clear Protocol. Step 1 — If a supply relationship becomes romantically charged: separate the lanes explicitly. “I value this [supply relationship / friendship]. I want to be clear that the two are separate for me and I’d like to keep them that way.”

Step 2 — Observe whether the supply relationship remains unchanged after the lane-clear. If yes: clean. If it cools, becomes complicated, or becomes suddenly less available: the lanes were never separate.

Step 3 — If you are in a supply-dependent position when the romantic pressure arrives: build the alternative source first. The alternative source is the only thing that makes the lane-clear carry no cost.

Step 4 — If you have already declined and the access has changed: document the change. The temperature change is evidence. Evidence is what you have when the relationship eventually requires addressing through a camp network or a festival safety team.

Quick vibe check. People in supply-adjacent relationships can develop genuine romantic interest in each other, and that interest can be mutual and consensual and healthy. The test is not “did romantic interest form.” The test is “what happens to the supply relationship when the romantic interest is declined.” A clean romantic interest that is declined produces no supply change. The Plug Romance produces a supply change.

Turn it around — the mirror set. Have you ever used something you could provide as ambient leverage in a romantic context — not explicitly, but in the sense that your warmth and your access were entangled in a way that made declining the romantic interest feel like declining the access? The Plug Romance runs at every scale where access and attraction coexist.

Refractions — this lands differently depending on who you are. **BIPOC Festies:** In supply relationships in predominantly white scenes, the Plug Romance can carry additional power dynamics that compound with the racial dynamics of the space. If the person controlling your supply access also controls other forms of your belonging in the scene, the coercion is structural even when the romantic interest is presented as personal.

**Queer Scene:** The Plug Romance in small queer communities runs through the same supply-access leverage in a more socially dense environment. The small scene means the temperature change after a declined romance is visible to more people and has more social consequences. Both the leverage and the consequence are amplified.

This runs in every direction. The Plug Romance runs through the male dealer who starts calling it something else, the femme distributor whose customers become something else, the nonbinary supplier whose access and affection blur, and anyone whose supply relationship and romantic interest have been allowed to become entangled. The gender distribution of who runs it and who receives it varies. The structural architecture is gender-neutral.

And Sober Tuesday — the morning-after truth. The manager whose “management style” involves taking the high-potential employees to dinner, where the dinner’s purpose oscillates between professional mentorship and personal interest, and where declining the personal interest produces a slightly less interesting management experience going forward. The supply is career advancement. The leverage is identical.
