🔰 SAFETY APPENDIX — CHAPTER 22
- The two rules. Both are short. Both are serious.
- OWN-SOURCE DISCIPLINE
- At all times, maintain at minimum two verified, independent supply sources. This is not about distrust. This is about removing the single-point-of-failure from the supply chain.
- Verification: Each source has tested, documented, disclosed products. You have run your own independent test at least once per source per year. You have compared results.
- Independence: The two sources do not know each other in a way that creates coordination. Two sources in the same supply network are one source with extra steps.
- Maintenance: The second source does not get maintained by default. It requires occasional transactions to stay warm. Maintain it even when the first source is performing excellently. Especially when the first source is performing excellently.
- The single source test: If your first source became unavailable tomorrow, how long would it take to have supply for the next event? If the answer is “I’d have to start from scratch” — you are single-source. Build the second source this week.
- THE VAN RULE
- Your supply does not travel in anyone else’s vehicle.
- If it does:
- You have explicit, documented access to it at any time, at your discretion, without requiring their cooperation.
- It is inventoried before loading and verified against inventory at every stop.
- Your financial arrangement with the vehicle operator is entirely separate from the supply transportation arrangement.
- You can exit the vehicle at any point and take your supply with you without logistical complication.
- If any of these conditions are not met: your supply is in their van and they are driving. That is not your supply. That is access to your supply contingent on their continued cooperation.
- The Van Rule also applies to: housing, equipment, transportation, and any other resource you cannot access without the cooperation of the person who controls it.
- THE TWO-PERSON BUMP RULE
- Never use a new substance in an environment where you are the only person monitoring yourself. One other person is the minimum. That person is not under the same substance. That person knows what you have taken and when. That person has your emergency contact.
- THE PHONE CAMERA AUDIT
- After any blackout or significant memory gap: the first thing you do is open your camera roll and review the photos in order. Timestamped. You may have taken photos you don’t remember taking. The photos are data about where you were and who you were with. The data is yours before anyone else’s narrative gets to it.
- THE REDDIT DOCTRINE
- Every major festival subreddit eventually produces the thread: “anyone else have a weird experience with [name]?”
- The thread is a survivor’s first whisper network.
- Contribute to it when you have information. Check it before significant supply arrangements. The eleventh response turns a single experience into a pattern. You can be the eleventh response. You can also read the tenth before you become the twelfth customer with the cut K.
