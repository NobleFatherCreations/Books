## The Sunrise Set + The Kandi Trade (closer)

Here's the Sunrise Set. Zero slang — just the true thing. The gift economy is one of the real things.

The genuine warmth at a festival fire — the version that is not running a sequence — is one of the specific magic conditions of the scene. People give each other things. People share fire and food and music and presence. People sit with strangers at three a.m. and provide the particular care that strangers can provide: without history, without complicated relationship, without the weight of everything you know about each other. Just the present warmth.

That warmth is real.

The chapter is not against it.

The chapter is for the version of it that is free of the ledger. The version in which the gift is a gift because it was given without a receipt. The version in which the jar is what it says it is and the fire is just a fire and the warmth does not come due in the morning.

That version exists.

It is the version worth protecting.

Protecting it means naming the other version. The other version uses the same gestures and the same warmth and the same fire and the same jar and converts all of it into a tool.

You have the seal protocol.

You have the two-text rule.

You have the question.

You have Mara Hot Water at seven a.m. with the notepad.

Use them.

Receive the real warmth. Set down the ledger that was never yours.

And here's the Kandi Trade — the vow.

- I will drink from things I sealed myself.
- I will ask what is in the jar.
- I will notice the first free thing and ask what the second thing is.
- I will count the gifts and remember I do not owe a ledger I did not open.
- I will text my anchor before I drift.
- I will leave when I want to leave.
- I will make it a thing if it was a thing.
- I will go to Mara Hot Water in the morning.
- I will remember that the warmth being real does not make the ledger real.
