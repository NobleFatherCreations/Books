The Sacred Divide
# Come in.
Five screens, about two minutes. You will come out knowing what is in here and where to find it.
Nothing here is tracked, stored, or sent anywhere. There is no account, no paywall, and no way for anyone to know you were here.
You can leave at any point and nothing follows you out.
One of two things to know
# What this is.
- A map of the machinery built around faith — the offices, the treasuries, the policies, and the people who benefit from your obedience.
- Traced to documents: court judgments, government inquiries, regulatory orders, and the institutions’ own published rules.
- Written so that you can keep the faith and refuse the leash.
Twenty-seven traditions, each examined the same way, by the same standard — plus eight cross-cutting volumes and eleven accountability instruments.
Two of two
# What this is not.
- An argument that God does not exist.
- A case that your faith is false, or foolish, or beneath you.
- A campaign to get you to leave anything. No page here will ask you to.
- A claim that the people who raised you meant you harm.
There is one place this does not soften: a practice that hurts people is not made holy by the language it wears.
If a sentence anywhere in this book fails that test, it is a defect in the book — not a discovery about you.
What is in here
# Five districts.
Everything in this codex lives in one of five places. This screen is the whole map — read it once and you will know where anything is.
One thing that confuses people: the eight stages and the thirty tactics appear in both of the first two districts. That is not duplication — it is the two axes of one table. In The Lens you read a mechanism on its own, across many traditions. In a tradition you read all thirty mechanisms as they appear in that one place, with its examples, its defenders’ strongest argument, and an evidence grade for that exact intersection. Same 810 cells: read down a column, or across a row.
Every screen is tinted with its district’s colour, so you always know which part of the map you are standing in. Search reaches all of it, and a full walkthrough of every section is always available from the Board.
The last thing
# Set your own pace.
There is no correct order and no progress to lose. Every tradition is eighteen short acts, each one standing alone, each marked with how long it takes and whether it is heavy. Nothing has to be read today.
One suggestion, and it is only that: nobody sees a pattern for the first time inside the thing that raised them. The defenses fire faster than the reading does. If you are here about your own tradition, it is worth spending a few minutes somewhere unfamiliar first — then coming back holding a lens instead of a reflex.
Every one of these opens the whole codex, with search and all five districts one tap away. You can change your mind at any point and nothing is lost, because nothing is being kept.
