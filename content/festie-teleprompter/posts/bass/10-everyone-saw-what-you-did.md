# “EVERYONE SAW WHAT YOU DID”
*Reputation Threats / Social Smear Campaigns — clinically, smear campaign / reputation destruction.*

Here's who you're dealing with. The person who threatens or executes damage to your reputation on the festival circuit when you push back, question them, or simply stop complying. Common in promoter relationships, crew dynamics, and romantic situations gone sour.

Here's the scene. You stopped promoting for the promoter who wasn't paying you and the next week three festival Facebook groups had posts about you being "unreliable" and "difficult to work with." You didn't do anything wrong. But the narrative is already in circulation on the circuit and your next event inquiries have gone quiet. The people spreading it are the same people who weren't paying you.

> 🖼️ **[INSERT IMAGE HERE: `images/bass/tells-card.png`]** — hold this on screen with this scenario's tells typed in; you can shorten the next line to "Here's what to watch for" instead of narrating every tell if you want this under 3 minutes.

Watch for these signs.

- Implicit or explicit threats to "ruin you" on the circuit if you push back
- Negative characterizations of you begin circulating after you exercise independence
- Your professional contacts become suddenly distant without clear reason
- The people spreading the narrative are the same people you had a dispute with

Here's what's actually happening. Reputation threats are a control mechanism that works specifically because the festival circuit is small and relationship-driven. The goal is to make the cost of pushing back higher than the cost of compliance. It often works. The counter is documenting everything before the dispute escalates, building independent relationships that don't run through the person threatening you, and proactively communicating your version of events to people who matter to you.

> 🖼️ **[INSERT IMAGE HERE: `images/bass/check-card.png`]** — hold this on screen with the check question and letter typed in; you can shorten the line below to just naming the check instead of reading the full quote if you want this under 3 minutes.

Run the check. The S check: “Have I documented this situation in writing so I have something to point to?”

Here's the dark reality. Documentation Is Your Defense. Before any dispute escalates: screenshot every agreement, every payment confirmation, every communication. If payments are owed: send a written demand via email with terms attached — this creates a legal paper trail. If reputation damage is happening: keep records of what's being said, where, and when. Defamation (false statements presented as fact that damage your reputation) is actionable. Most predatory promoters rely on targets being too embarrassed or too tired to document. Document anyway.

Here's your move. Build independent circuit relationships. Document everything. Your reputation is built over time — one smear campaign doesn't end it.

Here's exactly what to say. “"I'm going to communicate directly with the people who matter to me about what actually happened."”

And here's the truth. Your reputation is yours. It was built by your actual work. One person's narrative doesn't own it.
