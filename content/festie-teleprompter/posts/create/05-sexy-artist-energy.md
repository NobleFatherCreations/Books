# “SEXY ARTIST ENERGY”
*Sexual Attention as Artistic Validation — clinically, objectification / sexual coercion via validation.*

Here's who you're dealing with. The specific way sexual attention gets entangled with artistic recognition for visible artists. When someone's interest in your art and interest in your body arrive in the same package, receiving one without the other becomes complicated. That confusion is the tool.

Saturday night he asks if you want to "continue the conversation" at his camp. When you decline he says "I guess I misread the creative energy between us." Suddenly the whole weekend of genuine-feeling artistic recognition has a retroactive question mark over it.

Watch for these signs.

- Artistic interest and sexual interest arrive in the same package from the same person
- Declining sexual interest causes withdrawal of artistic validation — they're linked
- Retroactive reframing of the whole interaction when you say no

Your art's validity is entirely separate from anyone's desire for you.

Screenshot. Block. Report. Document before you report.

Here's your move. Receive genuine artistic recognition with full confidence. Decline sexual interest without apology. These are completely separate.
