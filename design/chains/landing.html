A manipulation chain is a set of tactics used in a particular order

One tactic on its own can be easy to miss or explain away. Used in order, each one makes the next easier to accept, and the order is what does the work.

Each chain here is told first as a story set in corporate and consulting life. Only after the story does the page name the chain and take it apart: the tactics, why they come in that order, what it looks like for one person, for an organization and for the wider culture, and what you can notice, keep and say.

Every story is invented. The point is to recognize a pattern, not to judge a person.

In a hurry? Under each title, Jump to takes you straight to the cue cards, the chain breakers, the escalation triggers or the words you could use.

Every chain has its own shape

Each ring is one stage of the manipulation cycle, from Idealize at the centre to Replace on the outside. A chain's tactics sit on the ring of their stage, in order, running clockwise from the top. The line joining them is the path the chain takes through the cycle.

The open circle is where the chain starts and the gold dot is where it ends. A dashed dot at the centre is a tactic that spans several stages, such as gaslighting.

The shape is drawn from the tactics in each chain, so no two are the same. This one is %%SAMPLE%%. As you read a chain's tactics, its shape forms step by step.
