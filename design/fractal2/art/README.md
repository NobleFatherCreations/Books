# Artwork used by the Fractal² intro

`the-fractal.webp` is the cover image of *The Fractal* (the owner's published artwork), taken unchanged from the image
embedded in `library/fractal/index.html` and resized to 960 x 1720. It is shown at the centre of the "zoom" figure.
Rebuild it with the snippet in the 2026-11-10 session notes if the cover ever changes.

## The series logo (owner request, 2026-11-10)

`reference/owner-ref-1-expanded-rings.jpg` and `reference/owner-ref-2-completed-circle.jpg` are the two pictures the owner
attached as the look to aim for: the original filled out to a complete circle with "fractal²" at the centre, and a wider
view with more rings in the same colours. They are AI-made drafts and contain misspelled and garbled words, so they are
references for layout and colour only and are never shown on the site. The finished logo is drawn from data by
`design/fractal2/logo.py` so that every word is spelled correctly: the eight stages named once, every tactic evenly
spaced around its ring.
