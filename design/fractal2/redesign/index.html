Part I

The feeling without a name

There is a feeling almost everyone knows and almost no one can name.

It is the tightening in your stomach in a room where everyone else seems fine. It is the moment your own words come out wrong and you cannot say why. It is the apology you hear yourself making for something that was done to you.

You leave the relationship that hollowed you out, and you meet the same feeling at work. You leave the job, and it is waiting at the bank, at the clinic, in the pew, on the screen in your hand at two in the morning. It follows you from building to building. And because it follows you, you reach the only conclusion that seems to fit.

It must be me.

The Fractal began as a refusal of that conclusion. It asked one question and would not let it go.

What if it isn't you?

What if the thing that breaks a person inside one relationship is not a private wound at all, but a shape? And what if you keep meeting it in every building because it is the same shape in every building?

Part II

What The Fractal saw

A fractal is a pattern that repeats at every scale. A fern's leaf is a small fern. A river delta looks like the veins of the leaf beside it. Break off any piece, look closely, and you find the whole.

The cycle that runs inside a manipulative relationship is that kind of shape. It has eight stages, and they arrive in order.

Plate 1 · The cycle

  1. Stage 1 of 8

    Idealize. You are seen, chosen, flooded with warmth.

  2. Stage 2 of 8

    Hook. You commit, and leaving begins to cost something.

  3. Stage 3 of 8

    Devalue. The warmth cools, and your worth becomes a thing you have to earn back.

  4. Stage 4 of 8

    Confuse. What you saw, what you heard and what you felt are put in doubt.

  5. Stage 5 of 8

    Isolate. The people who could have told you what they saw are moved out of reach.

  6. Stage 6 of 8

    Extract. What you have is taken: time, money, labor, body, belief.

  7. Stage 7 of 8

    Discard. You are dropped when there is nothing left to take.

  8. Stage 8 of 8

    Replace. Someone new is in your seat before it is cold.

Thirty techniques carry a person through those eight stages, from the love bombing at the door to the plausible deniability on the way out. I mapped all thirty, in the order they run.

Then I showed every one of them at three levels of scale.

Plate 2 · The same shape at three scales

three scales

  1. Level 1 of 3

    In one life: the partner, the parent, the boss. The pattern wearing a human face.

  2. Level 2 of 3

    Inside an institution: the church, the school, the hospital, the company. The pattern wearing a logo.

  3. Level 3 of 3

    Across a civilization: the economy, the culture, the law. The pattern wearing the sky.
