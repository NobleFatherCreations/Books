## A. What this document is for (one sentence)

**To let a reader see one pattern in their own life and in the institutions around them, understand that three works were built to make it seeable, nameable and breakable, and want to help.**

Every page must serve one of four verbs. If a page serves none, move it to Reference.

## B. Architect's insights (use these as the document's spine; they are new)

1. **Three units.** The unit of harm is the *sequence*. The unit of healing is the *pause*. The unit of prevention is the *word*. Each work delivers one unit: combinations expose the sequence, the Frequency teaches the pause, Playground Protectors and the Decoder give the word. State this once, early, as a three-box figure.
2. **Three levels are three different kinds of help.** A person gets *recognition*. An institution gets a *design test*. A society gets a *shared language and something to measure*. Use these three columns on every sector card. This is how "3 levels × 30 sectors" is shown, not asserted.
3. **The thirty sectors are a lifespan, not a list.** Order the sector cards by when life meets them (birth → childhood → youth → adulthood → aging → death), not 1–30. The reader walks through their own life.
4. **Every chain breaker restores one of two things: time or a witness.** Tag every breaker in the five combinations T or W and print the count. If it holds (it should), it becomes the simplest rule in the whole work: *slow it down, and bring someone in.*
5. **The descent.** The book goes down: sectors → mirror → body → chain → frequency. "You did not arrive by going up. You went down." Shape the document the same way: outward (sectors), inward (mirror, body), backward (chain), then ground (frequency), then rise (what it could do). The ending is the ground, not a summit.
6. **Three moments, three products.** *Before* = Playground Protectors and the Decoder (the word). *During* = combinations (the turning-point question). *After* = The Fractal (the proof it wasn't you). Most resources serve only "after."
7. **Trust first for professionals.** Move a half-page version of "what is documented / my synthesis / my faith" to the front (after the index). A professional who sees the limits early believes the rest.
8. **One page grammar.** Every concept gets the same four things in the same order: one picture, one sentence, one example, one question. Predictable pages lower effort and let the content land.
9. **The work passes its own test.** Free, shows its engineering, says what else it could be, invites disagreement: the four marks of a clean system. Say it once, at the call to action.
10. **Machines feed machines.** Keep the five-machine school diagram as the centrepiece of BREAK. It is the single best argument for why adults who work with children matter here.
