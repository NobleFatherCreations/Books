# The Fractal project: 2-minute digest (read this instead of the book)

Written 2026-11-13. Read this file, not `library/fractal/` (245,383 words) or the 53-page PDFs. Details live where listed under "Where to look." Do not re-derive anything marked FACT.

## What it is
One manipulation cycle (8 stages: idealize, hook, devalue, confuse, isolate, extract, discard, replace; powered by 30 techniques) shown at 3 levels (person, institution, civilization) across 30 sectors of life (religion to immigration). Author: Shae Stovell. Free to read. Underneath it: 30 original human impulses the cycle captured and could not destroy ("the frequency"). Three works, one map:
- **The Fractal (Book 1):** 30 sectors x 30 techniques x 3 levels = 2,700 examples. Proves the pattern is everywhere. "The alphabet."
- **Pattern Decoder / playbook:** 310 plain-language entries (In short, Understand it, Is this it?, What you can do, Go deeper). Source of Fractal²'s tactics. "The dictionary."
- **Fractal²:** 300+ named tactics per sector, each at 3 scales, plus 150+ combinations per sector (ordered sequences, each with scene, why-this-order, institutional and civilizational echo, recognition cues, chain breakers). "The sentences." Finished: Entertainment (335 tactics, 153 combos) and Corporate (138 combos).
- Related: The Sacred Divide (34 traditions), Playground Protectors (children's book, 28 missions, `library/children`).
Four verbs: SEE (Fractal), NAME (Decoder, Fractal²), BREAK (combinations), RETURN (frequency).

## Reviewer's verdict (FACT, Opus/Fable review 2026-11-13)
- Strong as a teaching instrument and as writing. Weak as checkable evidence. The fix is cheap.
- Real contributions: the abuse cycle used as the bridge from personal to institutional life; every technique paired with the real thing it copies; The Mirror (the pattern in yourself, graded intent, no shame); sequences with "why this order" and chain breakers.
- Closest predecessors (cite them): Biderman, Freire, Illich, Forbes (wetiko), Dutton and Painter, Wink, Herman, Stark, Fricker.
- Evidence by layer: practices reasonable; relational techniques moderate; "same cycle in institutions" plausible, untested; civilizational level interpretive; The Body chapter over-precise (polyvagal and chemistry stated as fact); epigenetic inheritance contested; The Origin is one school of history.

## Defects in the published book (unfixed unless the owner says so)
1. One-line definitions of Spiritual Bypassing and Financial Control are swapped on all 26 sector pages.
2. Says 30 sectors / 2,700 examples; 26 sectors / 2,340 are published (missing: Technology, Dating, Surveillance, Child Welfare).
3. Zero outside sources in any chapter.
4. "This is by design" x8 in The Pattern contradicts The Origin ("no intent required").
5. The Mirror numbers techniques differently from the sector chapters.
6. Absolutes ("None of them do"; "the pattern is not a metaphor"); the mislabel table (anxiety = discernment, etc.) needs the Frequency chapter's care about medicine and professionals.

## Rules for the voice and the claims
First person, short sentences. Contested science = "my belief." Never say the map ends the cycle; it ends the method's ability to stay hidden. "Seeing is not leaving." Never invent a tactic, combination, statistic or quote. Intro Part VI (`content/fractal2/intro-final.md` lines 271-282) is the owner's account and is test-locked.
