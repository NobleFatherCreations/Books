# Pattern Decoder: a 30-minute review for a licensed psychologist

Thank you for looking at this. The Pattern Decoder is a free reading aid at noblefathercreations.com/playbook. It names manipulation and control tactics in plain words, so a person can check what is happening and decide what to do. It is not a diagnosis, a legal opinion or therapy.

What you'd be reviewing is the guide's own content, as an independent professional. You would not be endorsing it on behalf of any employer or district. With your permission, the About page (`/playbook/about-this-guide`) could name you, along with the date of the review and the statement "the reviewer's views are their own". Nothing about you is published until you say yes to the exact wording.

Each item below is one page. For each one, note **OK**, **Change** (with what to change) or **Remove**.
