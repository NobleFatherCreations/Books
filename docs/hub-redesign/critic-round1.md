**Three scores are under 4: composition, phone ergonomics and finish.**

## 5 fixes, in priority order

1. **Phone answer-panel gutter.** In the "For 'someone close to me'" panel, the heading, intro and covers sit at x=0 (phone-states.jpg frame 2). Give the panel the same 16px inline padding as the other sections, and check every answer variant.
2. **Show the answer.** After a chip tap on desktop, nothing changes in view except the book (desktop-1-answer.png). Either scroll the answer panel into view smoothly (instant scroll under reduced motion), or slide it in directly under the chips at a minimum of 18px text with 64px covers. Today the covers are about 40px (desktop-strips.jpg col 1).
3. **Replace the end-of-Workshop card row.** NFC, The Press and The Casting are three near-identical dark boxes, and two of them hold small line icons (desktop-strips.jpg col 4; phone-strips.jpg col 5). This is the "grid of identical icons at the end" the owner dislikes. Make NFC a full-width feature with its cover large. Fold Press and Casting into a single "In the making" line with a real photo of each, or leave them out.
4. **Fill the dead halves on desktop.** Put the Free tools cards in 2 equal columns spanning the full content width. Put the Listening Room heading over the track list, or make it a 5/7 split with the heading at display size. Give the "someone close" panel a 3-up layout with larger covers (desktop-strips.jpg cols 1, 3).
5. **Brighten small text and give the door real art.** Raise the song meta, footer links, licence line and card descriptions to at least 15px and at least 7:1 on the black (desktop-strips.jpg col 4). Replace the brown-slab door illustration with the 3 actual covers, tilted and half-shown behind the door, so the reveal pays off (phone-states.jpg frame 3). Use one button style for "Close the bigger picture" at every width.

## Generic or AI-made tells

- The 3-card row with line icons that ends the Workshop (desktop-strips.jpg col 4).
- The pill-outline buttons ("All 176 songs", "See every Portal", "Read the guide") are the default SaaS pill (desktop-strips.jpg cols 3, 4).
- The hero only uses about 45% of the desktop width. The headline could run larger to meet "big type that uses the screen" (desktop-0-fold.png).
- The Listening Room shows 5 dim rows that cannot be played. It reads as filler while the songs are switched off (phone-strips.jpg col 4). Consider one line plus a single featured song card.

**What is strong:** the threshold line "Worn in the light. Alive in the dark." and the daylight/dark Portals sliders (desktop-3-portal.png). They are the most distinctive moments on the page and should stay exactly as they are.
