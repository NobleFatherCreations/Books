# Legal-safeguard audit, all 17 sites (2026-11-08)

Not legal advice: a check of what each page already says and where a gap would grow once the page is findable.
Method: automated scan of every site's source for notices/disclaimers/help lines/privacy wording/copyright lines,
plus reading the notices themselves and the Fracture's sentences that pair a named person with an accusation word.
No prior legal discussion was found in either repo, any branch or any commit message; if one happened, it was in a
chat that was not saved here.
