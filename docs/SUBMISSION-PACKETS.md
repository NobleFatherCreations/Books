## 4. Zenodo (a permanent DOI, so researchers can cite the Decoder)
**Start:** https://zenodo.org. Sign in with GitHub or ORCID, then click New upload.

- **Files:** `content/decoder-v21/export/pattern-decoder-taxonomy.csv`, `pattern-decoder-taxonomy.json`, `pattern-decoder-routes.json`. I'll send these when you're ready.
- **Resource type:** Dataset
- **Title:** Pattern Decoder: a plain-language taxonomy of 335 manipulation and control tactics and 32 patterns
- **Creators:** Stovell, Shae (Noble Father Creations). Add your ORCID once you have it.
- **Description:**
  > A structured, plain-language taxonomy of interpersonal, institutional and online manipulation. It has 335 single tactics in ten groups, each with a standard number and stage (Idealize to Replace), plus 32 patterns (for example gaslighting, DARVO and trauma bonding), each broken into the tactics it is made of. It also has 109 routes showing tactics that commonly occur in sequence. Each entry gives a short definition, alternative names, its parent patterns and links to verified sources where they exist. Human-readable pages for every entry: https://noblefathercreations.com/playbook. It is a reading aid, not a diagnostic instrument.
- **Keywords:** manipulation; coercive control; psychological abuse; gaslighting; DARVO; taxonomy; domestic abuse; institutional betrayal
- **Licence:** CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 (the Decoder's current licence). You can choose a looser licence for the data if you want wider reuse.
- **Related identifier:** https://noblefathercreations.com/playbook ("is documented by")
