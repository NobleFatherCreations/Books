# Next book: The Silence (recommended), with its optimised outline plan

## Which book, and why
**Recommendation: The Silence, *For men nobody thought to ask*** (`library/silence/`, live v4).

1. **It's the owner's own ground.** He's a father of four, and his bio says the books are for "men, who almost never get asked, and almost never say", and for "the guy in your group chat who laughs it off". A deep version of this book is the clearest proof of the bio. It's also the book where "Who made this" earns the most trust.
2. **It's the thinnest book for the biggest gap.**
   - Now: 46 chapters and about 11,400 words.
   - The Long After: about 107,000 words.
   - Men in coercive relationships have almost nothing free that doesn't either dismiss them or recruit them into the manosphere. The book's own brief says so.
3. **The Long After already points at it.** The finish plan adds bridges to The Silence (fathers, children, courts). If The Silence is deepened next, those links land on a book that can carry them.
4. **The safety rails already exist.** Its brief has the load-bearing rule ("alongside the book for women, not instead of it"), plus `qa.js` AUDIT 6 (no comparative grievance), and it passes clean. A deeper rewrite keeps both.

**Runners-up, in order:**
1. **At Will** (the workplace): the widest audience and strong search demand.
2. **The Repair:** rare and valuable, but delicate.
3. **The Slow Take:** later life; families searching for an older parent.

**Target size.** About 55,000 to 65,000 words: half The Long After. Its brief asks for plain, short and direct, so depth comes from more chapters and tools, not longer prose.

## How it's built (Book Pipeline v2, 10 steps)
1. **Intake.**
   - Read `library/silence/BOOK-MAP.md`, `AUDIT.md`, BOOKS.md and the live pages.
   - Ask the owner's decisions:
     - depth goal
     - AI disclosure
     - composite tags
     - voice sample (or use `content/about.md`)
     - deploy scope
   - Keyword research with Ubersuggest and Windsor (Search Console), for seeds like:
     - "male victims of domestic abuse"
     - "signs my wife is emotionally abusive"
     - "abusive girlfriend"
     - "where can men get help for domestic abuse"
     - "false allegations domestic violence"
     - "father cut off from children"
2. **Spec.**
   - Voice spec: plain, short sentences, never "brave", never "vulnerable" as praise.
   - `book.json`, its entry in `site_manifest.json`, the gate thresholds.
   - AUDIT 6 kept as a hard gate.
3. **Outline** (below). Each chapter gets:
   - its questions
   - its shape
   - where the honest check sits
   - its cross-book bridges
   - its Decoder links

   Then steelman round 1 with the full panel, plus two Silence-specific readers:
   - a men's-services worker
   - a women's-services advocate checking AUDIT 6
4. **Writing**, part by part, through the insert gate.
5. **Audits:** voice, AI-writing, tone, AUDIT 6, steelman 2, facts, jurisdiction, weaponization.
6. **Freeze.**
7. **Build** (multi-page static, like The Long After v5) and **design** in one round. Keep its design argument: subtraction, Source Serif 4, cool blue-grey.
8. **Checks.**
9. **Release.**
10. **Retrospective note.**

- **Contested topics** (prevalence, false allegations, parental alienation) carry the tier label and sources from both sides. Never one number defended.
- **Jurisdiction** on every legal line: the US, UK, Ireland, Canada and Australia, matching The Long After.
