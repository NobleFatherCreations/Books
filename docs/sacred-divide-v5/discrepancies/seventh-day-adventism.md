## P1 · Critical (factually exposed, or breaks the codex's own rules)

1. **[§5 card "August 1980 — Glacier View", "Why it matters"; §3 "The answer"; §10 card 1 "Why that reason expired"; §12 technique 12 counter; §14 "Last time the chair ran"]** "A published finding about Ford exists; no published finding about the doctrine does. That asymmetry is the page's central exhibit." / "the institution withdrew his credentials instead of answering him" / "The process produced a decision about the man and no published decision about the doctrine" → the Sanctuary Review Committee produced two consensus statements ("Christ in the Heavenly Sanctuary"; "The Role of Ellen G. White in Doctrinal Matters") and a ten-point critique of Ford's positions, published in *Ministry*, October 1980 (F10). Proposed §5: "The committee published consensus statements reaffirming the doctrine and a list of Ford's departures from it; the only binding action it produced was against the man. That asymmetry is the page's central exhibit." Proposed §12 t12: "The process produced a reaffirmation of the doctrine and a decision about the man; it did not publish what evidence would have counted against the doctrine." Proposed §3: "...the institution reaffirmed the doctrine and withdrew his credentials; it has never said what evidence would have counted against it." → The page calls this its central exhibit. The first thing a hostile reader will produce is the October 1980 *Ministry*. The stronger, defensible claim (no stated falsification criterion) is already the page's §20 and §23 Q3.
2. **[§7 Top of the chain, White Estate row "Removable by"; §7 tell; §3 lede]** "Nobody outside the trust. The elected structure does not reach this office" / "a trust created by the prophet's own will, answerable to nobody the members elect" → since 1958 part of the board is elected for terms matching General Conference terms (now 5 life trustees of 15); under the 1933 arrangement the General Conference funds the Estate by annual budget and receives all royalty income from White's books; the GC Executive Committee fills vacancies if the trustees cannot (F9). Proposed row: "Five life trustees fill their own vacancies; the other ten serve terms tied to the General Conference, which funds the Estate. No member vote reaches it directly." Proposed tell: "Follow the prophetic claim and it terminates in a trust created by the prophet's own will, funded by the General Conference but not answerable to any member vote." → The "two structures" thesis rests on this row. It survives in narrower form; as written it is checkable and wrong.
3. **[§22 Africa card, "apex"]** "Divisions and unions here hold the overwhelming majority of world membership" → the three African divisions hold about 10 million of 23.7 million, about four in ten (F7). §7 ("Africa (the largest bloc by far)") is right. Proposed: "Divisions and unions here hold the largest share of world membership, about four in ten members." → A numeric claim that the church's own statistics refute; it is the premise of the card's "tell".
4. **[§20 row 1 "What it cost"]** "It cost careers; the researchers who surfaced them were not thanked at the time" → no source; the transcripts were found in December 1974 by the General Conference's own archivist, F. Donald Yost, and published in *Spectrum* in 1979 (F12, F13). Proposed: "Not recorded." or cite a named cost. → An unsourced cost claim in the table that benchmarks what breaking the pattern costs.

## P2 · Structural (inconsistent, incomplete, or weakens comparison)

5. **[§13 loop summaries 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 — misplaced loop text]** The cards carry the cross-volume titles, but five one-line summaries describe a different loop. Card 3 "Children to Members to Children" says pastors are paid by the conference (a money/structure point). Card 4 "Aid to Legitimacy…" describes persecution framing (loop 7's subject). Card 5 "Unpaid Labor…" describes aid and standing (loop 4's subject). Card 6 "Scandal to Removal…" describes the curriculum (loop 3's subject). Card 7 "Persecution…" describes the career cost of the parallel institutions (loop 1's subject). Under the no-move rule every summary stays on its card (completed into a sentence where needed); the rebuilt steps (SDA-L003 to L007) follow the card title, so each card's summary and steps now disagree. Proposed reassignment: card 3 ← card 6's summary; card 4 ← card 5's summary; card 7 ← card 4's summary; card 6 ← a new summary, "A critic is removed, a study is commissioned, and the teaching does not change."; card 5 ← a new summary, "The movement ran on women's and commissioned labour, and the institutions that labour built set the terms of the work."; card 3's pastor-pay line and card 7's career-cost line fold into loop 1. → The loops are the page's synthesis; a reader comparing loop 4 across volumes finds a different loop here.
6. **[§12 technique 17 grade]** **Taught**. The rewritten note (SDA-R017) states the basis: the credential actions against Rea and Ford are on the record (pointing to **Documented** or **Codified**), and the characterisation of critics and independent media is observed practice (pointing to **Cultural**). Nothing in the entry is taught doctrine. The old note repeated technique 28's basis. Decide; a change moves the §1 tally.
7. **[§12 technique 12 grade]** **Taught**, on "the reframing of Glacier View as a personnel matter is documented in the institution's own materials". 8. **[§12 technique 30 grade]** **Taught**, on a gap that "is nowhere written down". Something not written and not taught as doctrine points to **Cultural**. 9. **[§6 General Conference row]** "in 2015 they rejected women's ordination by 1,381 to 977" → the motion was whether division executive committees may make provision for ordaining women (F8); §5, §11 and §21 state it correctly. Proposed: "in 2015 they voted 1,381 to 977 not to let divisions authorize the ordination of women [3]." → Inconsistent across the page; the narrower statement is the accurate one.
10. **[Narration "for-you law"]** "Merikay Silver's equal-pay case against Pacific Press ended in a federal ruling that she had been denied allowances paid to men and fired in retaliation" → the 1982 appellate ruling concerned Lorna Tobler's claims (F16, F17); §19 has it right. Proposed: "In the 1970s two women at Pacific Press, Merikay Silver and Lorna Tobler, took the church's publishing house to federal court; in 1982 the appeals court upheld findings that Tobler had been denied allowances paid to men and fired in retaliation." (Not edited: the fix changes which named person a finding is attributed to.)
11. **[§24 item 2; §15 employment row]** "Title VII still protects most non-ministerial staff in the US" is offered to a member whose belief has changed → Title VII §702 lets religious employers prefer co-religionists, so it protects against sex discrimination and retaliation (source [13]) but offers little against dismissal for a change of belief (F22). Proposed: "It led the church to enforce conflict-of-interest declarations at every level of the denomination." → The source cited for the claim says otherwise.
