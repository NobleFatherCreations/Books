## P1 · Critical (factually exposed, or breaks the codex's own rules)

1. **[§3 "The answer", §23 "In closing"]** "Every documented failure here occurred where that code was set aside for charisma" / "Every failure documented here happened where that code was set aside in favor of charisma" → proposed: "Each of the teacher-misconduct cases documented here occurred where that code was set aside in favor of a teacher's authority." → The page's own cases include an embezzlement prosecution, a blackmail scandal and state-linked nationalism (§19), none shown to involve a set-aside Vinaya procedure. A universal claim in the thesis paragraph is the first thing a hostile reader tests (F8). Not softened in the text, per the rules of this pass.
2. **[§12 grades 16–22, 24 (Taught) and 23 (Codified)]** Nine techniques carry grades their entries do not support, now that each has its own rationale (BUD-R016 … R024). The old shared note cited merit economics and the funeral monopoly, which these entries never describe.
   - **16 Flying Monkeys, 17 Smear Campaign, 18 Stonewalling, 19 Manufactured Consent, 20 Trauma Bonding, 21 Learned Helplessness, 22 Benevolent Control**: graded **Taught**. Each entry describes what senior students, sanghas and teachers do to a complainant. Only the vocabulary (attachment, ego, non-reactivity) is taught doctrine. The behavior points to **Cultural**.
   - **24 Identity Erosion**: graded **Taught**. The basis (the doctrine of non-self) is taught, but the entry's claim is that the doctrine is applied to disable protection, which is practice. Decide between Taught and Cultural.
   - **23 Infantilization**: graded **Codified**. The entry describes teachers treating students as spiritually immature. The only codified element (the eight garudhammas) belongs to §11 and §17, not to this entry. Points to **Cultural**; the new note says so.
   - **28 Discard**: graded **Cultural**, and the note says departure is handled by quiet exclusion "which is why nothing is ever on record". The phrase is unsupported as an absolute and sits beside §19 cases that are on record. Proposed note: "Departure is handled by quiet exclusion rather than any procedure, so cases seldom reach a record." (Not applied: it weakens the claim.)
   - **27 Manufactured Crisis** and **29 Replacement** (Cultural): new notes fit.
   Any regrade changes the §1 tally (Taught 14, Cultural 12, Contested 2, Documented 1, Codified 1) and needs the §1 Evidence row edited together.
3. **[§1, §7 Who's in charge; §1 Chosen by / removable by]** The Thai Supreme Patriarch is given as the apex of Buddhism, and "The Crown / The Crown" as the answer to who chooses and removes the top office. Buddhism has no global apex, and the tibetan-buddhism and soka-gakkai volumes (same family) have their own. Proposed glance wording: "No single office leads Buddhism. In Thailand the Sangharaja (head of the sangha) is …" and, for the chosen/removable row, "No global office exists; in Thailand the Crown chooses and can remove the Supreme Patriarch." (F2: "removable by the Crown" is also not stated in the sources read.) → The scorecard's "?" (no apex) convention used in other volumes is not applied here.
