## P1 · Critical

1. **[§12 grade basis, techniques 19, 23, 26]** Once each rationale states what its own entry rests on (JAI-R019 was already apt; R023 and R026 rewritten), three grades are stronger than their entries support:
   - **23 Infantilization** (Contested): the entry describes adults corrected over food, fasting and temple attendance. The Contested basis, Digambara doctrine on women's liberation, is a different fact (technique 23 never mentions it). The entry points to **Cultural**; the doctrinal dispute belongs to §10 and §14.
   - **26 Financial Control** (Codified): the old note said the auctions have "published bidding". Bidding is public (read aloud, §2) but no document codifies it, and §9 says trusts publish little. The entry also describes donations, sponsorships and wedding costs, which run by custom. Points to **Cultural**, or **Documented** for the auction alone.
   - **19 Manufactured Consent** (Documented): the Documented basis is minor renunciation litigated as a consent problem. The entry's bullets are about dietary restraint; only bullet 3 (children) touches it. Defensible narrowly; decide.
   Also **13, 14** carry `(sourced)` with no [n] under them, and §1 says "5 of 30 techniques sourced to a named document"; none of the five has a citation marker. Any regrade changes the §1 tally (Cultural 25, Taught 2, Documented 1, Contested 1, Codified 1) and the sourced count.

Proposed: retitle "1949 — The Anuvrat movement" and cite a source such as a Terapanth or Britannica entry, or cut the card. → The card is offered as "the model" for the healthy column, with nothing behind it.

5. **[§3 objection, §15 ledger, §15 "Formal religious sanction"]** "There is no clergy with coercive power, no excommunication apparatus, and no enforcement body" sits badly with documented community boycotts of Jain families (F15).
