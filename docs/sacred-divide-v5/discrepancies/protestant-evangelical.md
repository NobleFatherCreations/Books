Proposed: "About 800 million (Pew counts 801 million Protestants, 2011), up to 1 billion on wider definitions. Pew counts 584 million Pentecostal and charismatic Christians across all traditions, Catholic charismatics included [19]." → The first row of the page double-counts across volumes (catholicism, pentecostal-charismatic) and contradicts §6.

Techniques 3 and 26 cite "recorded sermons" with no named sermon; 9 and 23 cite nothing; 18 rests on the unsourced NDA claim (item 1) (F20). Proposed: either add named sources for 3, 9, 18, 23 and 26, or change the row to "Three of the 30 techniques are sourced to a named document" and remove the five unsupported *(sourced)* markers. → The row is the reader's measure of how much of §12 has a paper trail.
