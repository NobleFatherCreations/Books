## P1 · Critical (factually exposed, or breaks the codex's own rules)

1. **[§7 top of the chain, Archbishop row; §1 scorecard Removal **N**; §1 narration "no public evidence of a written removal procedure"]** "no removal procedure has been identified" → the Clergy Discipline Measure 2003 applies to archbishops and allows removal from office, and a CDM complaint against Archbishop Mullally was brought and dismissed in March 2026 (F1). Proposed §7 cell: "The holder can resign, as in 2024 [3], or be removed from office under the Clergy Discipline Measure 2003 after a tribunal finding; no archbishop has been removed under it." Change the scorecard Removal to **P** (a written procedure exists; it has not been used against a primate), and add the Measure as a source. → The volume's thesis turns on whether anyone can correct the apex; the removal cell and the scorecard are the first thing a hostile reader will check, and the procedure is public.
2. **[§26 sources 5 and 12]** Two source entries carry editor instructions that print to the reader: source 5 "— update to the latest annual report at import.", source 12 "**cite the Anglican Communion Office** at import." Proposed source 5: drop the parenthesis and cite the 2025 results as its own entry (already source [21]). Proposed source 12 addition: "Anglican Communion, 'Member Churches'. https://www.anglicancommunion.org/structures/member-churches.aspx — 42 member churches; about 85 million people", and move the [12] citations for the 85M / 42 figure to that entry (F2). → Build instructions in live text break the deploy-hygiene rule, and the size figure in §1 currently cites a news story that does not contain it.
3. **[§23 In closing, item 1; §7 narration (for-you structure)]** Credited the church's own synods with abolishing blasphemy law; Parliament did it (Criminal Justice and Immigration Act 2008, s.79), as §5, §8 and §20 of the same page say (F18). **Changed in wording** (ANG-W133, ANG-N002) to match the page; listed here so the owner can confirm or reject the two edits. → A factual error in the page's closing argument, contradicted by its own sources.
4. **[§12 — grade basis, table format]** §12 in this volume is a table, not cards, so there is no reused grade note to rewrite. Read against each row's own content, these grades do not fit their basis (grades unchanged):
   - **17 Smear campaign**: graded **Contested**, but the cell itself cites IICSA's finding that survivors were disbelieved in documented cases [1][4]. The basis points to **Documented**.
   - **6 Gaslighting**: **Documented** on "per reviews", with no citation in the row. Name the review and cite it ([1], [3] or [4]), or the grade has no receipt.
   - **25 Spiritual bypassing**: **Documented** on "per reviews", no citation. Same fix.
   - **13 Normalization**: **Documented**, no citation in the row; IICSA [1] and Gibb [4] on deference would support it.
   - **21 Learned helplessness**: **Documented** on "survivors' repeated failed complaints". Failed complaints are documented; the psychological state the technique names is not. Consider **Contested**, or reword the row to what IICSA found.
   - **19 Manufactured consent**: **Contested**, but clerical assent to doctrine is a written requirement (the Declaration of Assent). The basis points to **Codified**.
   - **2 Weaponized generosity**: **Contested**, but worship-based admissions criteria are published (§11, §15). Consider **Codified** for the school-place half.
   - **22 Benevolent control**: **Taught**, but the row describes oversight of clergy lives exercised through licence and housing, which is Common Tenure law (**Codified**) or practice (**Cultural**), not teaching.
   - **26 Financial control**: the chip "Codified (clergy) / weak (laity)" and **4, 16, 23** "Cultural (weak)" are not standard grade values; the build colours chips by the first word only. Decide whether qualifiers belong in the chip.
   Any regrade changes the grade distribution (Documented 8, Contested 7, Cultural 5, Cultural (weak) 3, Taught 3, Codified 2, mixed 1, Ungraded 1).

## P2 · Structural (inconsistent, incomplete, or weakens comparison)

5. **[§12 format]** §12 is a six-column table (as in the other newer volumes), not the 30 cards with definitions, defenses, counters and an evidence-grade note used by Catholicism and Eastern Orthodoxy. Fragments in the cells were completed; the structure was not changed, per the brief. Consequences: no `{#t-n}` anchors exist, so the technique links in the rebuilt §13 loops (`[26 · Financial control](#t-26)` etc.) and the expanded-PDF "All thirty at a glance" grid have nothing to point to; and no row carries its own grade rationale. → The comparison the book promises ("any section beside the same section for another") fails at §12, and the new loop links are dead until anchors exist.
6. **[§5 timeline 2024 row]** Receipt "[GOVERNMENT REPORT — independent review]" on the Makin Review → the review was commissioned by the church and is independent of it, not a government report; §19 correctly tags it [INVESTIGATIVE REPORT]. Proposed: "[INVESTIGATIVE REPORT — church-commissioned, independent]". Similarly §11, §14 and §16 tag joint IICSA + Makin findings [GOVERNMENT REPORT]; IICSA alone is. → Receipt labels are the reader's evidence key; this one inflates the authority of the source.
7. **[§6 GAFCON row; §5 2025–26 row]** "GAFCON provinces … Announced in October 2025 that they would leave the Communion" → the statement was issued by the chair of the GAFCON primates' council, and a week later ENS found no other province publicly supporting it (F7). Proposed §6: "In October 2025 the chair of the GAFCON primates' council announced that its members would leave the Communion and form a rival network; few provinces had publicly confirmed it a week later [12]." → Overstates a split as done.
8. **[§23 Q4; narration q4]** "Who decides whether £100m is the right answer?" → §9 records that the Commissioners accepted a £1bn target in 2024, and F16 shows the target is "in partnership with others". Proposed: "The Commissioners traced part of the endowment to the slave trade and accepted a target of £1bn, to be raised with others. Who decides how much of it comes from the endowment, and who it reaches?" → The question as written is overtaken by the page's own §9.
9. **[§1 Money; §7; §9; narration money]** £11.1bn is the end-2024 value; the §9 chart and source [21] give £11.6bn at end-2025 (F4). Proposed §1: "a published £11.6bn endowment (end 2025) [21]" and the same in §7 and §9, keeping £11.1bn only as the 2024 chart point. → Two different endowment values stated in the present tense on one page.
10. **[§26 source 10]** Figures 1.009m / 581,000 are the final *Statistics for Mission 2024* (Oct 2025); the URL is the preliminary May 2025 release, which says 1.019m / 582,000 (F3). Proposed: point source 10 at https://www.churchofengland.org/media/press-releases/attendance-church-england-churches-rises-fourth-year-row . → A reader who follows the link finds different numbers.
11. **[§9, §24 item 4]** The £150m redress scheme was approved by Synod in July 2025 but still needs its parliamentary stages and is due to open in late 2026 (F10). §24 tells survivors to "use the redress scheme Synod approved in 2025". Proposed §24: "…and, once it opens (planned for late 2026), apply to the national redress scheme Synod approved in 2025 [9]." → A safety section should not send survivors to a scheme that may not yet take applications.
12. **[§5 Feb 2026 row; §14; §22]** "a new national safeguarding charity … approved" — its design was out to consultation until 17 May 2026 and operations are expected from late 2026 (F9). Proposed addition to §14 Chair now: "The charity, the Church of England Independent Safeguarding Authority, is expected to begin operating from the end of 2026." → Readers will otherwise assume the independent body is already handling cases.
13. **[§13 loops 4 and 7]** Two loop summaries carry claims recorded nowhere else in the volume: "chaplaincies, food banks" (loop 4) and "persecution of Anglicans elsewhere is real and is invoked in realignment disputes" (loop 7) (F26, F27). The rebuilt cards keep the summaries and flag the unsourced step. Proposed: source both, or cut to "Civic standing, earned through schools, sustains establishment privilege" and "Largely absent in England." → [PATTERN OBSERVED] loops are only valid if every step is recorded elsewhere on the page.
14. **[§17 Women clergy row]** "With minority provinces refusing ordination entirely" beside §11's "Many Communion provinces do not ordain women". Reworded in the fragment pass to "the provinces that refuse to ordain women at all"; confirm, or give the count. → "Minority" and "many" pull in different directions.
15. **[§1 Who's in charge]** The glance names the monarch as the one "in charge"; §3, §7 and §14 treat the Archbishop of Canterbury (and Synod) as the operative apex. Not changed. Consider "The monarch is Supreme Governor; the Archbishop of Canterbury leads the church and the Communion." → The narration's "top office" question then reads against the right office.
