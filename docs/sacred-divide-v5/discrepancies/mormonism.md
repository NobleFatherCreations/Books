## P1 · Critical (factually exposed, or breaks the codex's own rules)

1. **[§3 "widest gap" and §14 say-do row 1; §14 "Last time the chair ran"; §23 Q1; stage 6 text; §16 "Money out"; §9 "Where it comes from"]** "A federal regulator found twenty years of deliberate concealment of a hundred-billion-dollar portfolio" / "A $100 billion concealment" / "fined your Church for hiding a $100 billion portfolio" → the SEC found that the securities portfolio hidden behind 13 LLCs from 1997 to 2019 had grown to about **$32 billion** by 2018; the ~$100 billion figure is the whistleblower's 2019 estimate of total reserves (F1). The volume's own §9 "Money in numbers" says $32 billion, so the page contradicts itself. Proposed §3/§14: "A federal regulator found that for over twenty years the Church's investment arm hid a securities portfolio, about $32 billion by 2018, behind thirteen shell companies; a whistleblower put the whole reserve at over $100 billion." Proposed §23 Q1: "A federal regulator fined your Church for hiding its investment portfolio from disclosure for twenty years. A whistleblower put the reserve at over $100 billion. Ask why ..." → The thesis row of the page attributes a figure to a regulator that the regulator did not find. It is the first thing a hostile reader will check, and the page's own chart note refutes it.
2. **[§1 "The unanswered question", §3 question box]** "A hundred-billion-dollar fund was concealed for two decades" → same issue as item 1: what the SEC documented concealed was the equity portfolio's size in 13F filings. Proposed: "A portfolio a whistleblower valued at over a hundred billion dollars was hidden from securities filings for two decades; the penalty was five million dollars and no resignation. On what date do the members see the books?" → The thesis question should rest on what the regulator found, with the larger figure attributed to its source. (The §1 narration and §3 narration quote this question word for word; change them together.)
3. **[§7 Authority bullet 1; §10 card 2 "Why that reason expired"; §16 Who pays; §17 row 1; stage 6 text; §23 Q3]** Worthiness interviews of minors are described in the present tense as conducted alone ("an untrained adult male volunteer questions a child alone"; "Why does an untrained volunteer question a twelve-year-old alone about masturbation") → since June 2018 Church guidelines let a youth invite a parent or other adult to be present, require an adult in an adjoining room, and require the questions to be shared in advance (F10). Proposed §10: "It means an untrained adult male volunteer may question a child alone about their sexual thoughts and behavior; since 2018 the child may ask for a parent to be present, but the default remains one adult and one child." Proposed §23 Q3: "Why may an untrained volunteer still question a twelve-year-old alone about sexual matters, with a parent present only if the child asks, and what institution that works with children would call that acceptable?" Add the 2018 guidelines to §5 timeline and §20 (as a partial win of the reform campaign). → The page's sharpest child-safeguarding claim omits the reform it itself credits campaigners for pursuing. Omitting it makes the strongest section the easiest to discredit.
4. **[§5 card "September 1993" "Why it matters"; §14 Words table, "Anti-Mormon" row; §20 Promises row 1; §13 loop 7 step 5 as rebuilt]** "no standing was ever restored" / "The label was retired for the material and never for the people who were disciplined for it" → Avraham Gileadi was rebaptized in 1996 with his discipline expunged, and Maxine Hanks was rebaptized in 2012 (F9). Proposed §5: "The essays are the receipt. Two of the six later rejoined the Church, but no discipline was withdrawn on the ground that the history was true." Proposed §14: "The label was retired for the material; no discipline was ever withdrawn on the ground that the material was true." → A falsifiable absolute that the record falsifies. Loop 7 step 5 repeats the §14 sentence (by rule, loops use only facts in the volume); change it together.
