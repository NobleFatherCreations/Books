## P1 · Critical (factually exposed, or breaks the codex's own rules)

1. **[§14 say-versus-do row 2]** "the one exit is a civil marriage conducted online from abroad, which the state must register (2023)" → marriages performed abroad have been registrable since a 1962 Supreme Court ruling, and about 20,000 couples a year marry abroad (F6). The volume's own §8 ("marriages performed abroad — including online through Utah") and §24 ("marry abroad or online") say so. Proposed: "A secular or Reform Jew cannot legally marry in the country without Orthodox rabbinic authority; the exits are a civil marriage abroad, or online from Israel, and the state must register both (2023)." → The row contradicts two other sections and is the first thing a hostile reader will check.
2. **[§1 unanswered question, §3 table, §12 t7, §14 row 1, §10 card 1, §23 q1]** "Courts hold sanctions they decline to apply" / "have overwhelmingly declined" / "Name the rabbinic authority who has publicly declared her husband's conduct a violation and imposed a consequence" → Israel's Rabbinical Courts (Enforcement of Divorce Decisions) Law of 1995 gives the courts sanctions (travel ban, passport and licence, imprisonment) and they impose them in some cases (F7). Proposed: scope the claim: "Diaspora batei din have no statutory sanctions and rarely use the communal ones; Israel's rabbinical courts have held statutory sanctions since 1995 and apply them in some cases." Add a source for the rate of use. → The volume's thesis is stated without a source for "decline"; question 1 can be answered by naming an Israeli court, and the objection then reads as though the whole page is wrong.
3. **[§12 grade chips — evidence-weighting rule]** Once each technique carries its own rationale (grades unchanged; rationales corrected under JUD-R001 … R022), these grades are stronger than their entries support:
   - **7 Double Bind**: graded **Codified**. The old note justified the chip with the get and the courts' refusal to compel, which belongs to technique 30 and §10. The entry itself describes questioning that is celebrated and then punished, converts turned away, and leavers mourned. That is communal practice, pointing to **Cultural** or **Contested**.
   - **15 Triangulation**: graded **Codified**. The old note cited the Rabbinate monopoly, which the entry never mentions. The entry describes parents, ancestors, Holocaust memory and warnings against airing problems, which is custom. Points to **Cultural** or **Contested**.
   - **17–25 (nine entries)**: all graded **Contested** on a note about tuition, dues and the get. The entries rest on described family and communal pressure with no named document, so **Contested** holds, but the §1 line "4 of 30 techniques sourced to a named document" is the only evidence statement for them.
   - **2 Weaponized Generosity**, **4 Hoovering**: graded **Cultural**. Defensible.
   - No §19 case is tagged 7 or 15 (cases are tagged 14, 24 and 22), so the two **Codified** chips have no case behind them.
   Any regrade changes the §1 tally (Contested 21, Cultural 7, Codified 2) and possibly "4 of 30 sourced".
4. **[§1 scorecard; §1 narration]** Safeguarding **Y**, External first **Y**, Reply **Y** → no section of the volume names a public safeguarding policy, an allegation that went to police first, or an on-the-record reply (F20), and §14 to §16 describe the opposite. The narration builds on the scorecard ("and that deserves credit"). Proposed: cite the policies behind the three Y grades in §7 or §8, or change the grades to **P** (as Accounts and Pay are). → A credit given to Jewish institutions with no evidence on the page, beside sections that describe suppression of abuse reporting, will be read as an internal contradiction.

## P2 · Structural (inconsistent, incomplete, or weakens comparison)

5. **[§5 card 1953; §5 timeline]** "Israel's Knesset granted the Chief Rabbinate exclusive statutory jurisdiction" → the 1953 law gives jurisdiction to the rabbinical courts (F9). Proposed: "granted the rabbinical courts exclusive jurisdiction." In the timeline, "acquires state enforcement for the first time in 1,900 years" contradicts the same row ("the Mandate's religious-court system continued") and §10's millet origin (F10). Proposed: "acquires a modern state's enforcement" or drop "first time in 1,900 years". → The rows disagree with themselves on the page's central legal fact.
6. **[§5 card 2013–2015]** The kidnapping-ring conviction has no source; [1] and [11] do not cover it (F8). Proposed: add a source entry (Epstein, convicted April 2015, sentenced 15 December 2015, District of New Jersey) and cite it. → A named criminal case is stated without a receipt.

8. **[§9 pipeline cards 2, 3, 4]** "Conduct conditions attached to enrolment", "Curriculum exempted from standards / What is actually taught" and "Trust deeds, because litigation forced it" appear to be shared templates; nothing in the Judaism volume records any of them. 9. **[§11 Children bullet 4; loop 3 step 3]** "Youth movements and Israel trips … in some programs explicitly designed to shape political and marital choices" has no source (F18). 10. **[§14 row 3; §7 and §9 kashrut]** "Competing agencies generate overlapping fees on the same product without improving reliability" has a [FINANCIAL RECORD] receipt and no source (F19). 11. **[§10 card 3; §23 q2; loop 7]** "Rabbis have ruled internally that abuse reporting is not mesirah [LEADERSHIP STATEMENT]" and "families who call the police still lose their standing" have no source (F17). Proposed title: "Donations to Boards to Deference to Donations", summary: "Donor-dominated boards govern the institutions, and the staff who depend on them learn what not to say." → The title asserts a mechanism the page does not document.

14. **[§7 Adherents; §7 Regions; §1 Size]** "~15–16 million" (with a Pew attribution), "~7M" and "~6M" alongside 15.8 million, 7.3 million and 6.3 million in §6 (F1). Pew counts US Jews at a different total than DellaPergola's 6.3m because the definitions differ. Proposed: use 15.8, 7.3 and 6.3 throughout, and say that the count is of people who are Jewish by religion, ethnicity or upbringing. → Repeated figures should be identical.
15. **[§1, §7 "150-member electoral body"]** The run-off had 135 votes cast (77–58) and JNS reports 140 voters (F3). Proposed: confirm the size of the electoral body from the Chief Rabbinate law or drop the number. → A frozen number the sources do not clearly support.
16. **[§13 loop 2 summary, §12 stage 7]** "in liberal ones this loop barely runs, which is the evidence" treats absence as evidence for the loop. Proposed: "in liberal communities the institutional cost of leaving is low, and the loop barely runs." → Not a factual error; the reasoning is circular as worded.
