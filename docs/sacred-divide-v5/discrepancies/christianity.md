## P1 · Critical (factually exposed, or breaks the codex's own rules)

1. **[§14 say-do row 3; §16 "Money out" bullet 3; §8 US row; §1/§3 unanswered question; q1 narration]** "the only charitable exemption from public financial disclosure in U.S. law", "uniquely exempt", "the only charities most Americans cannot look up", "The one charitable sector legally exempt" are all wrong as stated. §6033(a)(3)(A) also excuses the religious activities of religious orders, integrated auxiliaries and conventions of churches, and very small organizations; governmental units and affiliates are excused too (F8). §27 tells readers that "uniquely among charities" was already softened, yet two sections still say it. Proposed §14: "The sector retains the broadest exemption from public financial disclosure in U.S. law: churches, their auxiliaries and conventions file no Form 990 [2]." Proposed §16: "…churches receive full charitable status while exempt from the public financial disclosure almost every other charity files." Proposed §8: "Churches are the one kind of charity most Americans cannot look up." Proposed question: "The largest charitable sector legally exempt from public accounts…". Also cite [2] for §14's "lobbied for", or cut "lobbied for": no source on the page shows lobbying. → This row sits in the section the narration calls "the most falsifiable on the page", and a hostile reader needs one counter-example to discredit it.

Add §7611 to source [2] or as a new source. → As written it tells readers that no federal regulator can reach their church, which is false and bears on what a reader can do.

An ongoing dispute must carry its current status (EDITORIAL-STYLE, Evidence).
