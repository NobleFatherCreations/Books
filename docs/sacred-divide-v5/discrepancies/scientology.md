# DISCREPANCIES — Scientology, awaiting the owner's decision

Format: **[Location]** what is wrong → what it should be (proposed wording) → why it matters. Nothing below has been changed in the text; every item touches the frozen layer (numbers, receipts, grades, source entries, case tags), moves loop text, or asserts a claim the wording pass may not soften or alter.
Evidence for each item is in `logs/fact-check/scientology.md` (F-numbers). Omissions are in `docs/sacred-divide-v5/proposals/scientology.md`.
This volume is litigation-sensitive. P1 items below are mainly cases where an allegation, a characterisation or an unattributed superlative is phrased as a finding or a court ruling.

## P1 · Critical (factually exposed, litigation-sensitive, or breaks the codex's own rules)

1. **[§3 lede; §3 "The strongest objection, answered"]** "everything in this profile comes from the organization's own written policies and from court records — not from critics' opinions" and "Every central claim here is either the founder's own policy letters … or a court record" → the page's own receipts say otherwise: FORMER MEMBER TESTIMONY and INVESTIGATIVE REPORT appear in §1, §5, §7, §9, §10, §11, §15 and §21, and §26 lists no policy letter at all (the only primary-source entries are court cases and a newspaper report). Proposed: "Most of what follows comes from court records, the organization's own written policies and published reporting; where a claim rests on former members' accounts, its receipt says so." and "Every central claim here is attributed in its receipt to a court record, a policy letter, a news report or former members' accounts." → The first sentence a hostile reader tests is false by the page's own labels, and it invites the objection that the page misdescribes its sources (F31, F16).
2. **[§7 "Who holds what" Bridge row; §9 pipeline card "Graded spiritual services" ("Disclosed. Price lists"); §12 techniques 3, 26 and others ("published sequence of levels with published prices", "Fixed published price lists"); §14 "We are a religion, not a business" row; §10 card 1 "the price list is the founding document"]** "published price", "published fee structure", "price lists are disclosed" → no cited source shows that the church publishes prices. The church describes its charges as fixed donations; the figures in circulation come from former members. Proposed: "a fee structure documented by former members and in reporting (the church calls the charges donations)" and, for the pipeline card, "Disclosed. Donation levels, to members at the point of sale." → An unsourced "published" is the easiest line to knock down, and several grade notes (techniques 3, 4 originally, 26) rest on it (F33).
3. **[§3 forefront, §5 card "Operation Snow White", §7 "Who holds what", §16 "Money out, leverage back", §19 case, §5 timeline row 1973–1979]** "the largest documented infiltration of the U.S. government by a private organization" → an unattributed superlative stated as fact in six places; the convictions are a court record, the ranking is not. Proposed: "one of the most extensive infiltrations of the US government by a private organisation on record, as described in [named source]", or cut the ranking and keep "eleven senior members were convicted of conspiring to burgle government offices, steal documents and obstruct justice" (F15, F16). → A superlative with no named author reads as the page's own opinion.
4. **[§19 Operation Snow White outcome; §20 "Operation Snow White convictions" row, "What it cost"]** "The organization obtained full tax exemption fourteen years later, following a litigation campaign against the IRS" / "None to the public; the organization obtained exemption fourteen years later" → the exemption is placed in the outcome of a criminal case it did not arise from; 1979 to 1993 is a coincidence of dates, not a consequence the sources state. Proposed §19 outcome: "Convictions and prison sentences." (end there), and §20 "What it cost": "The convicted officials served prison sentences." → Implied causation between a conviction and a later tax ruling (F17).
5. **[§7 "Who holds what" Snow White row, "Why it matters to you"]** "Proof of what the policy authorizes" → the convictions prove what eleven officials did. They are not an adjudication of what church policy authorizes; the church's position, not checked in this pass, is that the unit involved was dissolved afterwards. Proposed: "The convictions show what eleven senior officials did." → An individual conviction presented as a finding about the institution's policy.
6. **[§5 card "1967–1993"; §8 "Who can compel an answer"; §22 US card "regulator"; §5 "The 1993 agreement is a reviewable administrative decision"]** "reviewable administrative decision" / "remains a reviewable administrative act" → a closing agreement under 26 U.S.C. §7121 is final and conclusive and can be reopened only for fraud, malfeasance or misrepresentation of a material fact (F20). Proposed: "The 1993 agreement is a closing agreement with the IRS, final unless fraud or misrepresentation is shown; the IRS remains the first body able to ask for the underlying records." → The whole "compel" argument for the United States rests on the word, and it is legally wrong.
7. **[§5 card "1967–1993"]** "as established in the Church's own subsequent disclosures and court records, extensive private investigation of officials" → the supporting source [2] is a *New York Times* report that private detectives investigated agency personnel; it is not the church's disclosures or a court record. Proposed: "as reported by the *New York Times* in 1997, private detectives investigated agency personnel." → Misattributed evidence, and the receipt-level difference between a report and a record is the point of this volume (F19).
8. **[§5 timeline 1993 row; §7 "Who holds what" 1993 exemption row; §16 "Money out, leverage back"]** "Exemption obtained through litigation pressure rather than ordinary review"; "Your taxes subsidizing a fixed-price service business"; "the public subsidizes an organization that sells its services at fixed prices and discloses nothing" → the sources report the suits and the settlement. They do not say review was not ordinary, and "subsidizes" is a characterisation of every tax exemption. Proposed: "The exemption was granted in a 1993 closing agreement, after the church and its members had brought about 2,200 suits against the agency and its officials [2]." → Editorial causation stated beside a court-and-regulator fact (F21).
9. **[§5 timeline 1952–1954 row]** "Religious status brings tax and legal advantages to an existing fee structure" and the §5 paragraph "self-help psychotherapy rebranded as religion" → a statement of motive, tagged [ACADEMIC SOURCE] with no author or title in §26 for it. Proposed: "The church was founded in 1954; its religious status carries the tax and legal protections that apply to churches [9]." → Motive is a contested claim that should be attributed, not asserted.
10. **[§19 "Convicted on former members' testimony (United States, 2023)"; §26 source 5]** the outcome line says "his appeal was pending in 2026 [5]", but [5] is an NPR article of 7 Sept 2023 and cannot support a 2026 status (F4). Proposed outcome: "Sentenced to 30 years to life on 7 September 2023 [5]. His appeal was argued before the California Second District Court of Appeal on 25 June 2026 and had not been decided on 3 October 2026 [new source]." Proposed new source: Courthouse News or Tony Ortega, 17 May 2026. → Time-sensitive status carried by a source that predates it.
11. **[§19 Masterson card, `tactics: 30, 16, 17`; §21 "The women who testified"]** the case tags and the card's place on a page about the institution imply that the church's conduct was found; the criminal case was against one man, and what the accusers say the church did afterwards (harassment, discouraging police reports) is alleged in a separate civil suit with no finding. Proposed: drop tags 16 and 17, keep none, or add a line: "What the accusers allege the church did afterwards is the subject of a separate civil suit, *Bixler et al.*, in which the church denies the allegations." → Allegation versus finding versus court ruling: the only finding here is a conviction of an individual.
12. **[§3 "Widest gap" table; §3 cost table; §10 card "Disconnection"; §14 say/do; §15 ledger; narration "for-you cost"]** "Members must sever contact with those declared suppressive, including parents, spouses, and children"; "It requires severing contact …"; "The organization publicly denies disconnection is required while its own policy letters instruct it" → stated as fact with receipt [OFFICIAL POLICY], but no policy letter is cited in §26; the supporting source is a book of reporting (Wright, [10]) and former members' accounts. The church denies that disconnection is compelled. Proposed: "According to the founder's policy letters as quoted by former members and in reporting [10], members are required to cut contact with those declared suppressive; the church says disconnection is voluntary." The narration line "this comes from the founder's policy letters, not from their personal choice" tells a reader what another person's motive was and should be cut. → The page's central claim, with no primary source.
13. **[§10 card "Sea Org billion-year contracts", "Why that reason expired"]** "Monastic orders do not bill departing members … That is not a vow; it is an indenture with a cancellation charge." → the first sentence is an absolute with no source; the second is the page's legal characterisation of the Sea Org contract, which the church treats as a valid contract and disputes as indenture. Proposed: "Former staff describe recruitment as minors, decades of labor at nominal stipends and 'freeloader debt' invoiced on exit [10]; the church disputes this characterisation." → An allegation framed as a legal finding.
14. **[§5 timeline row 1995–2000s; §12 technique 17 grade note]** "The 'fair game' pattern documented in modern litigation [COURT RECORD]" and "Fair game practice and litigation against critics established in court records across decades" → no court record is named for either. The Lisa McPherson matter ended with the criminal charge dropped (2000) and a settled civil suit; neither made a finding on fair game. The one appellate ruling on harassment of a critic (*Rathbun*, Texas, 2015) is not cited (F9). Proposed: name the case or change the receipt to [INVESTIGATIVE REPORT / FORMER MEMBER TESTIMONY]; for technique 17 cite *Rathbun* if the grade is kept. Also state the church's position that the fair-game label was cancelled in 1968 (F30). → Receipt-label overreach plus a missing rebuttal a hostile reader will lead with.
15. **[§3, §14, §15 quoted denials]** "Disconnection is a personal choice about toxic relationships." / "We are a religion, not a business." / "We only defend ourselves against attacks." / "Auditing is confidential." / "Contractual obligations." / "We simply respond to attacks with our rights." / "Confessional confidentiality is absolute." → direct quotations in the church's voice with no source in §26 (F31). Proposed: cite the church's statements by document and date, or remove the quotation marks and attribute as "the church says that …". → Unsourced quotation attributed to a named party.
16. **[§6, §7, §9, §16, §18 "Front organizations"]** "Undisclosed affiliation … Programmes in your child's school with the affiliation omitted" and "without disclosing their affiliation" → no source in §26 supports it; Narconon, Applied Scholastics and Criminon describe their use of Hubbard's methods publicly, so omission in particular schools or prisons would need a named report (F32). Proposed: "…some programmes have been reported to operate in schools and prisons without naming their link to the church [named report]". → A blanket accusation against named programmes.
17. **[§11 Information, Children, Bodies bullets]** receipts such as [COURT RECORD / FORMER MEMBER TESTIMONY] sit on "Reporting sexual assault to police rather than internal Ethics has been discouraged, per multiple former members and litigation", and "Disconnection has been applied to minors' relationships with parents deemed suppressive" and "Cadet Org and Sea Org recruitment of minors … is documented" are stated as fact on testimony alone → litigation allegations are not findings, and "is documented" overstates testimony. Proposed: "…has been discouraged, according to multiple former members and the allegations in *Bixler*, which the church denies"; "…is reported by former members"; "…is described by former members and in reporting". → Allegation versus finding.
18. **[§22 Germany card "law", §8 Germany row]** "treated as a commercial enterprise" and "Federal and state monitoring reports" → (a) the 1995 Federal Labour Court decision cited behind [6] concerned a labour-law question, not the treatment of Scientology across German law; (b) on 15 May 2026 the BfV ended its nationwide observation as a separate area (Bavaria continues; Berlin lists it) (F25, F26). Proposed: "In German labour law the Federal Labour Court (1995) treated Scientology organisations as commercial; the federal constitutional-protection office ended its separate observation of Scientology in May 2026, and Bavaria and other states continue to monitor it." → Out-of-date legal status and a sweeping claim.
19. **[§15 ledger "Financial"; §17 "Members who mortgaged homes"]** "unrefunded advance payments" and "no refund route" → the church says refunds are available on request, and the *Garcia* case in §19 was a refund dispute. Stating there is no refund route as fact needs a source. Proposed: "advance payments that former members say they could not recover". → Contested practice stated as fact.
20. **[§12 grade notes, basis mismatches]** the wording pass rewrote the notes under 2, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 16, 19, 20, 22, 23, 25, 27 and 29 to state what each grade rests on. Where that basis is not the one the chip implies, the entries need a decision (grades unchanged):
   - **11 Projection · Documented** rests on the Snow White convictions, which show infiltration, not the accusation of critics for the organization's own conduct. Suggest Codified, or Documented only if a court record on the labeling of critics is cited.
   - **12 DARVO · Codified**, **6 Gaslighting · Codified**, **7 Double Bind · Codified**, **4 Hoovering · Codified**: the written part (suppressive declaration, ethics handling) is the codified element; the described conduct (calls, visits, reversal of blame) rests on reports. Suggest Taught or Documented for the conduct, Codified for the policy.
   - **8 Intermittent Reinforcement · Codified**, **9 Moving the Goalposts**, **20 Trauma Bonding**, **22 Benevolent Control**, **23 Infantilization**, **25 Spiritual Bypassing**: what is codified is the structure of levels, prices and categories; the experience claimed is from members. Suggest Documented or Taught.
   - **19 Manufactured Consent · Codified** rests partly on "former staff testimony", which is not codified.
   - **27 Manufactured Crisis**, **29 Replacement**: the codified element (suppressive declaration) is not the claimed conduct (urgency, a new recruit taking a seat). The recruit-takes-your-slot claim has no source.
   - **18 Stonewalling · Documented** says "in court records" without naming one.
   - **2 Weaponized Generosity**, **16 Flying Monkeys**: reasonable, but the entry bullets (free tests, informal pressure) are not what the written-policy basis proves.
   → The 30-technique grade is the codex's main claim to rigour; a careful reader can see the mismatch.

Not normalised, per instruction. The wording pass followed the local spelling where it edited a cell.

The loop steps (SCI-L004) use only recorded facts and cite the sections; the summary line is the volume's existing text. Per instruction, no loop text was moved between cards.

48. **[§20 "It has been broken before" row 2]** the "Who" for the Snow White row is "Federal prosecutors" and the "What it cost" cell is the exemption sentence (see item 4); the row belongs to a different question from the other three.
