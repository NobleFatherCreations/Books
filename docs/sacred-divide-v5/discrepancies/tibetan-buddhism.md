## P1 · Critical

1. **[§12 stage 8 heading line; §12 technique 30 tagline]** "...because the authority was attributed to God" and "because the authority is attributed to God — who isn't available for cross-examination" are shared-template text. Tibetan Buddhism is non-theistic, and the stage's own body names "the lineage, the deity, the guru as buddha". Proposed stage line: "...nobody is responsible for any of it because the authority was attributed to the lineage." Proposed tagline: "The institution is never responsible, because the authority is attributed to the lineage, the deity or the guru as buddha, none of whom is available for cross-examination." Why: a reader of this tradition meets an error in the page's most emphatic line. (F27)
2. **[§9 "Pipelines this tradition shares", card "Retreat and ceremony tourism"]** Shared-template card that does not fit: "Western participants seeking healing", "Operator, often not from the source community → Facilitators → Offshore or personal accounts", "Hidden: Safety record, training, and benefit-sharing". Nothing in this volume documents offshore or personal accounts for Tibetan Buddhist retreats. Proposed: delete the card, or replace with the retreat-centre flow already sourced in the money table (volunteer labor and donations build assets students never own). Why: an unsourced accusation of offshore accounts. (F29)
3. **[§1 Who's in charge; §1 Chosen by; §7 top of the chain; §3; §14; §20; §24; narration]** "The Dalai Lama has repeatedly and publicly instructed students to expose abusive teachers by name." Source [5] records the March 1993 Dharamsala meeting statement: confront the teacher, then "publicize" unethical behaviour. It does not say "by name" or "repeatedly". Proposed: "In 1993 the Dalai Lama and Western teachers agreed that students should confront an abusive teacher and, if he does not reform, make the behaviour public." Why: this is the page's central rebuttal of the silencing mechanism (§3 thesis, §23 closing) and a hostile reader will test it first. (F17)
4. **[§6 Kagyu row; §7 Karmapa row]** "Two rival Karmapas were recognized in 1992" / "Two recognized claimants since 1992". Only Ogyen Trinley Dorje was recognized in 1992; Trinley Thaye Dorje was recognized by Shamar Rinpoche in 1994. "Rival regents" also misdescribes Shamar Rinpoche, who was not a regent. Proposed: "Two rival Karmapas were recognized, in 1992 and 1994". Why: a dated fact in a frozen cell that is wrong, in the section on succession disputes. The §26 [8] parenthetical repeats 1992. (F14)
5. **[§7 "The top of the chain" lede]** "two governments preparing two rival children for one throne". The Dalai Lama's Trust is not a government, and no child is known to be prepared. The 2025 statement names a procedure only. Proposed: "two competing procedures for one throne, one set by the Dalai Lama's Trust and one claimed by the Chinese state." Why: the page's prediction is stated as fact. (F18)
6. **[§7 Who holds what, Rigpa and Shambhala rows, "Why it matters to you"]** "Proof that samaya was the enabling mechanism, established on the record" and "Proof that the structures failed for years while everyone knew". For Rigpa the report confirmed abuse and senior figures who failed to act; for Shambhala no source found names samaya, and "everyone knew" is not in the report. The same claim ("both investigations identified samaya") is in §3, §10 and §15 ("directly documented as the shield in both major investigations"). Proposed: "The Rigpa report found that senior figures were aware and failed to act" and, for Shambhala, "The Shambhala report found that the community's structures failed to respond to reports over many years." Why: hostile reviewers will read the Shambhala report. (F12)

   - **6 Gaslighting**: graded **Codified** on the samaya text; the entry describes senior students' reinterpretation of a student's experience. Points to **Taught**.
