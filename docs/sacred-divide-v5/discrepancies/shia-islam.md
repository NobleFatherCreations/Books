## P1 · Critical (factually exposed, or breaks the codex's own rules)

Proposed §19 what/outcome: "…and in May 2023 appointed an interim manager to run the charity in place of its trustees [27]." / "The mosque closed for two months over insurance and reopened; in May 2025 the Commission ordered governance reforms, and the inquiry remains open [27]." Proposed §22 tell: "The only jurisdiction on this page where a regulator took the Leader's own office out of its trustees' hands, because it was a charity." Add the gov.uk interim-manager (May 2023) and section 84 (16 May 2025) notices to source 27. → The case is one of five documented cases and the page's only example of a regulator acting; as written it reports an outcome that did not happen. The wording pass kept "removed" (SHI-W111, W114) rather than soften it.
2. **[§17 Dawoodi Bohra row]** "Australia's first FGM prosecution (convictions 2015, quashed on appeal; the High Court clarified the law in 2019; charges dropped in 2020)" → the High Court allowed the Crown's appeals on 16 Oct 2019 and reports say the convictions stand; no record of charges dropped in 2020 was found (F15). Proposed: "…and Australia's first FGM prosecution (convictions 2015, quashed on appeal in 2018, and the appeal reversed by the High Court in 2019) [n]", with sources added for both cases, after the post-2019 history is confirmed. → A criminal-case claim about a named community, unsourced and apparently wrong on its last step.
