Format: **[Location]** what is wrong → what it should be (proposed wording) → why it matters. None of the items below has been changed in the text. ## P1 · Critical (factually exposed, or breaks the codex's own rules)

1. **[§25 Muslim Women's Network Helpline; §26 source 28; §24 step 6 via [28]]** The page gives **0303 330 0288**. MWN's published numbers are **0800 999 5786** (freephone) and 0303 999 5786, text 07415 206 936, Mon–Fri 10am–4pm (F17). Proposed cell: "**0800 999 5786** (freephone; Mon–Fri 10am–4pm) [28]". Proposed source 28: "Muslim Women's Network Helpline — 0800 999 5786 (freephone) or 0303 999 5786; text 07415 206 936." → A help line that does not connect fails the reader at the worst moment, and the narration tells readers to save this contact first. **Shared data:** the same number appears in the Ahmadiyya volume (X2). Fix it in `additions/islam.md`, `sources/islam.md`, `additions/ahmadiyya.md` and `new-traditions/ahmadiyya.md`; it was not edited here.
2. **[§21 Nahdlatul Ulama; §22 Indonesia, documented and tell]** "Elects its leaders in contested votes", "leadership elections are genuinely contested" and "runs its two largest religious organisations by membership election" are out of date (F16). In Nov 2025 NU's Syuriyah declared the elected chair dismissed. At the Aug 2026 congress, delegates voted 401 to 150 to have a nine-member AHWA council choose the chair, and it chose KH Abdul Hakim Mahfudz by consensus. Proposed §21: "The world's largest Muslim organization, it replaced its chair in a contested vote in 2021 [25]. In 2026 its congress voted to have the chair chosen by a council of nine senior scholars instead [new source]." Proposed §22 tell: keep the point, but say "chooses the leaders of its two largest religious organisations through congresses of their members". Section 4 (ISL-W005) cites only the 2021 vote, which remains accurate. → The §22 tell is one of the page's structural arguments, and it rests on a present-tense fact that changed a month before the check date. **X1:** Sunni Islam carries the same claim.
3. **[§3 The answer, last sentence]** "Islamophobes have never once been the source of an internal critique." is a universal negative with no source, and it is circular, because an outsider's critique cannot be internal (F23). Proposed: cut it; the sentence before it ("the reformers cited are Muslims") already makes the point. → It sits in the section a hostile reader attacks first.
4. **[§12 grade basis; §1 Evidence tally]** Once each technique carries its own rationale (grades unchanged; ISL-R002 … R029), these grades no longer match what their entries support:
   - **22 Benevolent Control**, graded **Codified**. The basis is state morality-police dress enforcement "in specific jurisdictions", but every bullet in the entry describes family and community control. As written the entry points to **Cultural**. The alternative is to add a bullet on state enforcement (Iran, Afghanistan; §11, [15]) so the entry matches its grade.
   - **28 Discard**, graded **Codified**. The basis is statutory apostasy penalties, but the entry describes family withdrawal and exclusion. The grade is defensible only for the one bullet on "legal ... consequences". Decide.
   - **7 Double Bind** (Taught) and **23 Infantilization** (Contested). One half of each bind is statutory in some states (apostasy law; male guardianship, §10 card 2, §11, [5]). Consider **Codified** for the jurisdiction-specific half, or say so in the note.
   - **16 Flying Monkeys** and **19 Manufactured Consent**, graded **Cultural *(sourced)***. Neither note names a document, yet both are counted in "6 of 30 techniques sourced to a named document". Name the documents or drop the flag, which changes the count to 4.
   - **8 Intermittent Reinforcement** (Contested). The entry describes community practice, not a doctrinal dispute. **Cultural** may fit better.
   Any regrade changes the §1 tally (Contested 18, Cultural 5, Codified 4, Taught 3).
