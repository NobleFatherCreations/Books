## P1 · Critical

1. **[§8 "New York — schools"; §22 New York "documented"; §1 and §3 question by implication]** "The city's investigation found 18 yeshivas failing in 2023" and "its final 2023 determinations found 18 failing" (F2). The 18 is the Department of Education's determination out of 39 schools examined; for 14 of the 18 the state education department must still affirm it, and only 4 stand as final without that step. Proposed §8: "The city's Department of Education found 18 of 39 yeshivas examined not substantially equivalent in 2023; the state must still affirm 14 of those findings [4]." Proposed §22: "its 2023 determinations found 18 of 39 not substantially equivalent, 14 of them awaiting state affirmation." → "Final" is the word a hostile reader will test first, and the source the page cites says otherwise.
2. **[§5 card "A city investigates its own yeshivas"; §17 Children's Codex bullet]** "found the overwhelming majority of the schools examined were not providing substantially equivalent instruction" (F3). 18 of 39 is about 46%; 7 were found equivalent or equivalent by association. Proposed: "found 18 of the 39 schools examined not substantially equivalent." → Overstated as worded; the same sentence is in the Children's Codex import.
3. **[§8 "New York — schools", Law column]** "since 2022 local authorities must make that determination for every private school [11]" (F5). Search results show the 2022 regulations were partly struck down by a state court in Albany, that the state appealed, and that a 2025 class action alleges a rollback. Proposed: replace with a current statement after checking the appellate status, for example "In 2022 the Board of Regents required local authorities to review every private school; a state court later struck down key parts of those rules and the state has appealed [source to add]." → A present-tense legal requirement that may no longer be in force.
4. **[§12 grade basis mismatches]** After the rationales were rewritten from each entry's own content (HAS-R entries), these grades are stronger than their entries support. Grades are unchanged:
   - **7 Double Bind**: graded **Codified**. Only the third example (shalom bayit as a wife's duty) rests on halachic marriage law; the teen's doubts and the baal teshuva's treatment are social. The old note (get refusal) belongs to a different entry. Points to **Cultural**, or **Codified** only for the wife example.
   - **15 Triangulation**: graded **Codified**. The entry describes pressure through relatives, matchmakers and rabbis; no published ruling sets it out. Points to **Cultural**.
   - **27 Manufactured Crisis**: graded **Documented**. The entry describes urgency framed in speech around the internet, secular education and leaving; the court records of custody cases belong to technique 28. Points to **Cultural**.
   - **12 DARVO**: graded **Cultural**, but section 19 records retaliation against a reporting family. That case is tagged 16, 17 and 18, not 12. Consider tagging case 1 with 12 and grading **Documented**.
   - **17 Smear Campaign**: graded **Cultural**; section 19 tags the Weberman case to it. Consider **Documented**.
   - **14 Isolation** (Codified, rabbinic proclamations on internet and media) covers only part of the entry, which is mostly structural isolation (schools, marriage networks, neighborhood). Defensible narrowly; decide.
   - **28 Discard** (Documented on custody cases): the entry's bullets describe loss of warmth, school access and marriage prospects. The grade holds for the custody half.
   Any regrade changes the §1 tally (Cultural 22, Codified 4, Documented 4).
