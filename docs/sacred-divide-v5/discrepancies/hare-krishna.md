## P1 · Critical (factually exposed, stale, or breaks the codex's own rules)

1. **[§6 Branches, §11 Bodies, §21 Voices; F2]** Women gurus are presented as "approved in 2019 and 2021, then paused in 2022", as the current position. On 14 February 2026 the GBC approved a proposal that qualified Vaishnavis be recognized only as siksa-gurus, except Narayani Devi Dasi, who remains diksa-guru of her current disciples. Proposed §6/§11: "…approved in 2019 and 2021, paused in November 2022, and in February 2026 the GBC resolved that qualified women be recognized as siksa-gurus only, with the exception of Narayani Devi Dasi [6]." Source 6 needs the February 2026 statement added (https://iskconnews.org/statement-from-the-executive-committee-of-the-iskcon-gbc-on-vaisnavi-diksa-gurus/). → It is the page's own "live dispute", and the page now reports a pause that has since been replaced by a decision.
2. **[§8 India row, §19 "The temple two ISKCONs claimed", §22 India; F3, F4]** "ending a 25-year dispute; a review ended in a split decision in November 2025." The review has not ended. The two judges split, the matter went to the Chief Justice, and on 17 August 2026 the Chief Justice said the Court would consider a fresh bench. Proposed §8: "…the Supreme Court held in May 2025 that the Bangalore temple belongs to ISKCON Bangalore, not ISKCON Mumbai, after a 25-year dispute; Mumbai's review petition produced a split decision in November 2025 and remains pending before the Court [10][11]." Add the Verdictum report of 17 August 2026 as a source. → A live case is described as finished.
3. **[§8, §9, §21, narration; F7]** "About 550 former gurukula pupils sued in 2000." The 2000 Texas suit was brought by about 90 students; the larger figures are claimants in the bankruptcy. Hinduism Today (source 3) says the payout went to about 450 victims, $2,500 to $50,000 each. Proposed: "A 2000 suit by about 90 former pupils led, through ISKCON's Chapter 11 filings, to a $9.5 million settlement in 2005 covering several hundred claimants [3]." The figure "about 550" needs a source that states it. → The headline figure of the gurukula story is exposed to a one-line rebuttal from the page's own source.
4. **[§9 "Pipelines this tradition shares", four cards]** Shared-template leak. The card "Dera and ashram economy" uses "Dera", a term from other traditions (Radha Soami, Sikh-derived groups) that has no place in ISKCON, and its path ("Living guru's organization → Family-held entities → Political protection") is not supported anywhere in this volume. The other three cards assert, without a source, royalties to "serving leaders and their estates", bulk purchases, and non-disclosure agreements "that donations funded the defense". Proposed: delete the four cards, or replace them with cards built from this volume's sourced flows (the Bhaktivedanta Manor accounts; quota fundraising; the gurukula settlement funded from general funds). → These are accusations without support, in the wrong tradition's vocabulary.
