## P1 · Critical (factually exposed, or breaks the codex's own rules)

1. **[§2 composite day; §12 Stage 3 and Stage 6 text; §12 technique 8 bullets; §18 "Pioneers and regular publishers" row]** Monthly reporting of preaching hours is presented as current for every member ("Donna reports her hours at the end of the month", "Hours are counted and reported monthly", "recalculated monthly from field-service reports"). Since November 2023 publishers report only whether they took part; hours are recorded only by pioneers and others in special service (F15). Proposed §12 Stage 3: "Until November 2023 hours were counted and reported monthly, and pioneers still report them. Your service is compared to others'." Proposed §18 row: "| Pioneers | Report hours monthly …" and "| Regular publishers | Report each month whether they preached …". §2: have Donna report "her month" rather than her hours, or date the day before November 2023. → A current Witness reader will know this at once, and it is used as evidence in several grade notes.
2. **[§5 card 1989; §14 Words used here, "Two witnesses"]** "A scriptural rule about property disputes". The rule the organization cites is Deuteronomy 19:15 ("for any iniquity, or for any sin"), with Matthew 18:16 and 1 Timothy 5:19; none concerns property disputes (F17). Proposed: "A scriptural rule written for accusations of any wrongdoing, applied to a crime that has no second witness by design." → It is the first sentence a defender of the organization will check, and it sits in the entry that carries the page's central finding.
3. **[§7 lede; §14 Words used here, "The faithful and discreet slave"]** "Eight million members" and "a body of about nine self-appointing men" / "'Nine men, and you may not question them.'" The page elsewhere says ~9 million publishers (§1, §4, §6, §7) and eleven Governing Body members (§1, §6, §7, source [7]); both are current (F1–F3). Proposed: "Nine million members …"; "… in a body of eleven self-appointing men …"; "'Eleven men, and you may not question them.'" → The page contradicts itself on its two most-quoted numbers.
4. **[§9 pipeline cards "Tithe to reserve fund" and "Missionary self-funding"]** Shared template cards that do not describe this organization. "Member's income, taught as obligation" contradicts the §9 table ("no collection plate"; "genuinely low-pressure at the point of giving"), and Witness missionaries and special pioneers receive a stipend from the organization rather than being funded by their families (F28). These two cards were left unedited. Proposed: remove both cards. → Unsourced factual claims inside a Money section, contradicted by the same section.
5. **[§12 — evidence-weighting rule]** Once each technique carries its own rationale (grades unchanged; rationales corrected under JW-R002 … R029), these grades are stronger than, or different from, what their entries support:
   - **4 Hoovering**: graded **Reformed**. The entry describes retrieval through reinstatement (sit silently, meet elders, wait), and the 2024 change permits only a greeting at meetings; §24 says family contact outside that "is still usually cut". The practice described has not "materially changed". Points to **Codified** (reinstatement is published procedure).
   - **12 DARVO**: graded **Documented** on the Royal Commission's non-reporting finding. The entry describes questioners attacked as apostates and the organization cast as victim, which the finding does not establish. Decide whether the documented record fits the technique, or regrade to **Taught**.
   - **18 Silent Treatment / Stonewalling**: graded **Documented** on "repeated refusal to disclose the two-witness rule's application". The rule is published (the page grades it Codified elsewhere), so the stated basis does not hold; the entry describes questions answered with "wait on Jehovah", which points to **Taught** or **Cultural**.
   - **11 Projection** and **25 Spiritual Bypassing**: graded **Codified**. The published element is the labels and the "new light" teaching; the behavior described is how members apply them. Points to **Taught**.
   - **29 Replacement**: graded **Codified**. The entry describes a congregation filling a person's place, which is observed practice; the old note justified the chip with technique 30's basis. Points to **Cultural**.
   - **7 Double Bind** and **24 Identity Erosion**: the *(sourced)* flag was set for the old notes (a blood-transfusion bind; reported hours), which the entries do not describe. Confirm the source covers the new basis, or clear the flag.
   Any regrade changes the §1 tally (Codified 21, Taught 4, Documented 4, Reformed 1) and the "14 of 30 sourced" count.

## P2 · Structural (inconsistent, incomplete, or weakens comparison)

6. **[§23 question 3; narration q3 "why"]** "Ask what that proves about the forty years before it" / "change after forty years". The volume dates formal shunning to 1952 (§5, §10) and §20 says "Seventy years of families severed first" (F20). Proposed: "the seventy years before it". → Internal inconsistency in a question the reader is asked to put to others.
7. **[§21 Candace Conti]** "the largest single-victim verdict against a religious body in US history at the time" is her attorney's claim, and it was limited to religious child-abuse cases (F12). Proposed: "Her $28 million verdict was described by her lawyer as the largest for a single victim in a religious child-abuse case in the country, before it was cut on appeal [16]." → Unattributed superlative.
8. **[§19 Case Study 29, tactics line]** `tactics: 12, 18, 28`. 28 Discard describes removing members who become costly; the case is about allegations never reported. Suggest `12, 18, 30` (30 Plausible Deniability). → The case-to-technique links are the audit trail for the grades (see item 5, technique 12 and 18).
9. **[§9 pipeline cards "Unpaid labor to titled property" and "Legal defense and settlement"]** Fragments completed (JW-W046–W051), but the claims are template: "Nothing is disclosed about proceeds" / "Sale values … stay hidden" sit beside §9's own published Brooklyn sale prices; "Settlements and non-disclosure agreements" and "that donations funded the defense stays hidden" have no source for this organization. Proposed: "Hidden. The destination of the proceeds" and cite, or drop, the NDA path. → Unsourced claims in a Money section.
10. **[§9 "Congregations' local funds and halls are titled to the organization"; §16; technique 26 note]** No numbered source; title-holding varies by country (F29). Proposed: cite the Kingdom Hall Trust (UK) or equivalent, or narrow to "In many countries …". → The claim underpins loops 1 and 5 and the Codified grade on technique 26.
11. **[§20 "Promises on the record"; §23 closing; §24 item 4]** The page does not mention that the organization's published policy since 2019 says elders comply with mandatory-reporting laws and that victims and parents may report to the authorities (F18). The finding that the two-witness rule and internal-first structure have not been abandoned still holds. Proposed addition to the §20 note: "Since 2019 the published policy states that elders follow mandatory-reporting laws and that victims may report; the two-witness rule still governs congregational action." → Leaving it out invites a charge of omission on the central finding.
12. **[§8 "Who can compel an answer"]** "charity regulators, whose registration decisions in Europe" — Norway's decision was made by the county governor under religious-community law, not by a charity regulator. Proposed: "state registration authorities, whose decisions in Europe …". → Mislabels the one case the sentence rests on.
13. **[§12 grade notes for 4, 11, 12, 18, 25, 29]** now state a basis that does not match the chip above them (see item 5). A careful reader can see this until item 5 is decided.
