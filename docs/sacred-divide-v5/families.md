## Mutual declaration

Every volume lists its family's members. A pair is mutual when each lists the other.

All declarations are mutual. No volume claims a sibling that does not claim it back.

## Notes for the comparator

- Single-member families: Indigenous & folk. The comparator has no sibling to move to here; it must fall back to "compare with any tradition".
- Membership is single-family in the data. Plausible cross-family links (for example Hare Krishna with New movements, or Unification Church with Christianity) are not declared anywhere; adding them is an editorial decision and has not been made.
- The umbrella volumes (Christianity, Islam, Judaism, Buddhism, Hinduism) sit in the same family as their own branches. A comparison of Catholicism with "Christianity" compares a part with the whole; the comparator should say so.
