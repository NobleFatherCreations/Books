The Festie Bible

Twenty-one free field guides to psychological safety at festivals, one for each corner of the scene: women, men, first-timers, sober, LGBTQ+, BIPOC, disabled, artists, vendors, crew, harm reduction and more. 270 real-feeling scenarios with the tells, what is happening, and what to say.

By Shae Stovell · Updated November 8, 2026 · Free to read

Field guides for awareness and harm reduction, not medical, legal or mental-health advice. Scenarios are illustrative. In immediate danger, contact emergency services; in crisis, call or text 988. Full notice.
