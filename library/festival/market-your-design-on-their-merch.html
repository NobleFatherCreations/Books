Clinical: Intellectual Property Theft / Copyright Violation

Who You’re Dealing With

Your designs, your photographs, your product images appearing on someone else's booth, Etsy shop, or social media — without your permission, without credit, and often in direct competition with you on the same circuit.

The Scene

A customer at your booth holds up their phone: "I saw something almost identical to this at the booth down the way — they are selling it for less." You go look. It is not almost identical. It IS identical — your photo, your design, printed by a drop-ship service. The vendor shrugs: "I found the image online, how was I supposed to know?" Your image has a watermark. It was cropped out. This is not an accident.

Spot It — The Tells

  • Your product photos appearing on other vendors' social media without credit
  • Your designs appearing in physical or digital products from another seller
  • Drop-ship or print-on-demand versions of your work sold at lower prices on your circuit
  • Your business name or brand elements used by someone else in your market space

What’s Actually Happening

Design theft and image theft are endemic in the handmade marketplace — the internet makes copying easy and enforcement feels difficult. But copyright protection is automatic the moment you create original work and you have real options. The key is documentation: timestamp your original files, watermark everything you post publicly, maintain a creation file with original dated drafts. US Copyright registration ($65) enables enforcement with statutory damages of $750 to $30,000 per infringement.

M.A.R.K.E.T. — “R” CHECK: “Do I have timestamped documentation of my original designs that establishes my ownership before anyone else’s does?” — your designs are copyrighted the moment you create them. You get to enforce that.

Dark Reality — When Your Work Is Stolen — Your Options

Documentation first: screenshot everything, note dates, save original files. DMCA takedown: file with the platform — Google, Etsy, and Instagram all have processes and take these seriously. Etsy: file an Intellectual Property infringement report — they will remove listings. Legal: small claims for amounts under your state limit. Copyright registration ($65 at copyright.gov) opens the door to statutory damages and attorney fees — making enforcement financially viable. Volunteer Lawyers for the Arts offers free consultations in most major cities.

The Move

Watermark everything. Document your creation dates. Enforce your copyright.

Say This

“That is my image and my design. I need you to remove it from your inventory today. I have documentation of the original.”

The Truth

Your designs are copyrighted the moment you create them. You get to enforce that.
