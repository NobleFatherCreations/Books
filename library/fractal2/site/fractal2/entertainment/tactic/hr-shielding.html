HR Shielding

Using policy and process to protect the system, not the person.

Also called Dutiful Reporting, Firewall Function

What it looks like at each level

Individual

"I hear you, but let's keep this between us and the label's people" — the tour's welfare contact takes your report about the crew chief and sends it straight to the people who hired him.

Institutional

Labels and production companies whose complaint process is run by the same team that manages the headliner — the policy protects the brand and the tour dates, not the dancer who spoke up.

Civilizational

The assumption that an industry "process" exists to help — when its quieter job is to contain risk, keep the money moving and make the person who complained the one to be managed.
