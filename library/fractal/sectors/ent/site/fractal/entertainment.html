The ten that matter most

  1. 01

    The Debt Prison

    The math nobody explains. Recoupment and cross-collateralization can leave a balance that never clears, and the person owing it often cannot reproduce the numbers.

  2. 02

    The Phantom Equity

    A promised stake that is never papered. It is invisible until the money arrives, and by then the only record is a conversation.

  3. 03

    The Erased Contributor

    Credit disappears in the room, and the contributor is talked over. Almost every songwriter or session player knows someone it happened to.

  4. 04

    The Normalization Engine

    'That's standard.' The most likely line anyone hears, and the gateway that makes bigger terms feel normal.

  5. 05

    The Room

    Highest personal-safety stakes: closed late-night rooms. It ranks high for severity, and it is the one entry here that leads with safety, not strategy.

  6. 06

    The Halo Shield

    A beloved figure's public goodness is used to shield them, so reports are disbelieved. Rare to meet, very hard to act on when you do.

  7. 07

    The Economics Obfuscation Layer

    Costs and downside terms spread across affiliated entities and waterfalls so nobody can see the whole picture.

  8. 08

    The NDA Silencer

    The one no one sees: payment traded for silence means the next person never gets warned. It protects every other pattern here.

  9. 09

    The Soft Sell

    Small recurring fees and 'free' starts that quietly continue. Low stakes each time, very wide reach among independent artists.

  10. 10

    The Memory Hole

    Versions, emails and records go missing, so the original promise cannot be proven. The fix is cheap and early: keep your own copies.

Judgment scores 1-5 on likelihood, harm, hidden-ness, recognition; order by total then harm. Not statistics.
