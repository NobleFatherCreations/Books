All Your Power-Ups

The Power-Up Master Guide

Every Power-Up in one place. When something is happening and you need the right tool fast — flip here.

For kids and the grown-ups reading with them; not a substitute for professional, medical, legal or mental-health advice. If a child is in danger, call emergency services. Childhelp: 1-800-422-4453. Full notice.

⚡ TRUTH LOCKCh. 1

Keeps you connected to what you actually know when someone tries to make you doubt your memory.

"I remember it differently. My memory is that [say exactly what happened]." ⚡ FEELING PROTECTORCh. 1

Protects your feelings when someone says you're overreacting.

"My feelings are real. I don't have to prove that." ⚡ MEMORY VAULTCh. 1

Write it down so your memory stays solid.

What happened. When. Who was there. Write it and trust it. ⚡ FREE WILL FORCECh. 2

Separates your choices from someone else's feelings.

"I didn't make them do anything. Their choice is their choice, not mine." ⚡ I OWN MY PART SHIELDCh. 2

Take what is genuinely yours — nothing more, nothing less.

"I might have been a bit noisy. But how you respond is still your choice." ⚡ GUILT-FREE ZONECh. 3

Lets you care about someone without having to do everything they want.

"I care about you. And I still can't do this. Both things are true." ⚡ COMPASSION COMPASSCh. 3

Checks if you're helping because you want to — or because you're scared not to.

"Am I doing this because I want to, or because I'm scared of what happens if I don't?" ⚡ ONE DAY AT A TIMECh. 4

Keeps new friendships at a natural speed.

"I like getting to know you. Let's take it one day at a time!" ⚡ FRIENDSHIP BRIDGECh. 4

Reminds you that having other friends is always okay.

"I love hanging out with you! I also spend time with other people. That's what real friendship looks like." ⚡ NOT ABOUT ME SHIELDCh. 5

Reminds you that someone going silent is about them — not a verdict about you.

"Their silence is about them, not me. I don't have to chase them." ⚡ SIGNAL BOOST ULTRACh. 6 & 16

Goes straight to the person — skips the gossip chain entirely.

"I'd rather hear it from you directly. Can we just talk?" ⚡ TRIANGLE BREAKERCh. 6

Collapses the rumor triangle by talking directly.

"Before I believe anything secondhand — did you actually say that?" ⚡ STEADY SUNSHINECh. 7

Keeps your warmth steady even when someone else goes cold.

"I'm here if you want to talk. Otherwise I'll just keep doing my thing." ⚡ PATTERN SCANNERCh. 7 & 17

Spots when something keeps happening on purpose.

Write it down. Date it. Count it. Three times is a pattern. ⚡ NO COMPARISONS SHIELDCh. 8

Stops the game of pitting your friends against each other.

"I like you both for different reasons. There's no contest!" ⚡ SELF-WORTH ANCHORCh. 9

Keeps you connected to what YOU know about yourself — not what someone else says.

"I know what I put into this. I'm proud of it." ⚡ DRAMA-FREE ZONECh. 10

Lets you step back from a drama bomb calmly.

"If it's real and important, tell me exactly what happened. Otherwise — I'm letting it go." ⚡ CALM BUBBLECh. 10

Keeps you calm inside your own bubble when someone else explodes.

"I care about you. Let's talk when things are calmer." ⚡ INNER GPSCh. 11

Uses YOUR values — not the crowd — to decide what to do.

"This doesn't feel right to me. I'm going my own way." ⚡ REASON SHIELDCh. 11

Protects your reasons from being judged by someone else.

"It matters to me. That's enough." ⚡ HERO WALKCh. 11

Walks away from a group that charges the wrong price for belonging.

"I'd rather make choices I feel good about." Then walk. Head up. Steady. ⚡ SIGNAL SCRAMBLERCh. 12

Tests if someone is copying you on purpose to get your trust.

Change your posture. Change a gesture. Watch if they copy you within seconds. ⚡ PERSONAL FORCE FIELDCh. 12

Protects your personal space — always.

"Can you give me a little more room, please?" ⚡ DEFLECT + COLLECT ULTRACh. 13

Lets you accept a compliment without letting it become a trap.

"Thank you! That means a lot. And I still can't help with that right now." ⚡ GENUINE RADARCh. 13

Senses if a compliment has strings attached.

Did the compliment come right before a request? Was it withdrawn after a "no"? That's your radar signal. ⚡ SAFETY SCANCh. 14

Checks if a secret is safe to keep before you agree.

"I can't promise to keep something before I know what it is." ⚡ MISSION CONTROLCh. 14

Gets a grown-up involved when a secret is too heavy to carry alone.

"This is too big for me to hold alone. I need to get help." ⚡ TRUTH BEAMCh. 15

Shines a light on scary warnings to see if they're actually true.

"Is this a real fact — or is it being made to sound scarier than it is?" ⚡ COURAGE SURGECh. 15

Does the right thing even when feeling scared.

"I'm scared. And I'm doing it anyway. Because the right thing is bigger than the scared thing." ⚡ NAME IT TO TAME ITCh. 15

Gives a name to a scary thing so you can think clearly.

"What just happened is that someone threatened me to stop me from doing the right thing. That is a threat." ⚡ BROADCAST MODECh. 16 & 21

Makes your truth visible to more than one person at once.

Say the true thing clearly, once, to the people who need to hear it. ⚡ GROUND CONTROLCh. 17

Keeps your feet on the ground when someone tries to put you way up high too fast.

"I really like spending time with you. I'm still getting to know you!" ⚡ PATTERN SCANNER ELITECh. 17 & 18

Upgraded scanner — tracks the whole cycle, not just one moment.

"I disagreed with something small and the warmth disappeared. Is this the first time — or a pattern?" ⚡ APOLOGY ANALYZER ELITECh. 17 & 18

Tests if an apology is real or fake.

Real apology: says sorry and means it, no "but." Fake apology: "Sorry, BUT you made me..." ⚡ NOT MY DATACh. 18

Refuses to accept a description of yourself that is not true.

"That description is not accurate. I don't have to carry it." ⚡ UNSHAKEABLE MODECh. 18

Stays steady when someone needs your reaction as fuel for their next move.

Take one breath. Slow down. Say less. Answer steady. ⚡ EMERGENCY EJECTCh. 18 & 20

Removes you from a situation that Power-Ups alone can't fix.

Stop engaging. Limit contact. Tell a trusted adult. Save screenshots if needed. ⚡ INNER COMPASS ACTIVATEDCh. 19

Checks your own values before agreeing to a group rule.

"Does this rule actually make sense — or does it only help the person in charge?" ⚡ SOVEREIGNTY FIELDCh. 19

Protects your thoughts and choices from being owned by a group.

"What I think and what I do outside this group is mine." ⚡ CLEAN EXIT PROTOCOLCh. 19

Exits a group that doesn't feel right — without drama.

"This isn't the right fit for me right now. I'm going to step back." ⚡ IDENTITY CHECK PROTOCOLCh. 20

Verifies who someone really is before trusting them online.

"Before I share my number — can we do a quick video call? That's just how I do it." ⚡ KEEP IT PUBLIC FORCE FIELDCh. 20

Keeps new online friendships on visible platforms until you can verify.

"I'm good chatting here for now." ⚡ MUTE MISSION ALPHACh. 21

Quietly steps out of a harmful group chat.

Mute. Turn off notifications. Remove yourself from the audience. No announcement needed. ⚡ THINK TWICE FILTERCh. 21

Asks one question before sending anything online.

"Would I be okay if EVERYONE saw this?" ⚡ FRIEND MODE ACTIVATEDCh. 22

Turns your kindness toward yourself the same way you'd be kind to a friend.

"What would I say to my best friend if they were going through this? Now say that to myself." ⚡ FULL RECEIVECh. 22

Actually takes in a good moment before the Mean Voice can interrupt it.

Stop. Breathe. Feel the good thing for thirty whole seconds. No "yeah, but." ⚡ MY OWN SCALECh. 22

Measures yourself by your own growth — not by comparing to others.

"Did I try? Did I learn? Did I grow? Those are my measures." ⚡ FORGIVENESS COMPASSCh. 24

Helps you figure out if you're ready to put something down.

"Am I ready to set this down — not for them, but for me?" ⚡ BOUNDARY ARCHITECTCh. 24

Builds a clear boundary even while forgiving.

"I forgive you. And I know the pattern now, so I'm going to do things differently going forward." ⚡ FOUNDATION CHECKCh. 25

Checks if a friendship has all four building blocks.

Does this friendship have Kindness, Trust, Celebration, and Real Apologies — even in early form? ⚡ REPAIR PROTOCOLCh. 25

Gives a real apology that actually fixes things.

Name what you did. Say how it affected them. Take responsibility. Ask what they need. Then do it. ⚡ CELEBRATION LAUNCHCh. 25

Fully celebrates a friend's win with no conditions.

"That's amazing! You worked so hard for this. I'm SO proud of you!" ⚡ BRAVE VOICECh. 26

Says one sentence when something wrong is happening nearby.

"Hey — that's not cool." One sentence. Said normally. That's enough. ⚡ SIDE DOOR SUPPORTCh. 26

Privately checks in with the person who was targeted.

"I saw what happened. I want you to know I don't think it was okay. You alright?" ⚡ INNER CHAMPIONCh. 26

The inside speech for when you're scared to do the right thing.

"It doesn't matter what they think. I choose the version of me I can be proud of." ⚡ KINDNESS BOOMERANGCh. 27

Gives something kind with no receipt and no expectation of return.

Find one thing to give today — freely. Give it. Let it go. ⚡ SPECIFIC NOTICINGCh. 27

Gives a compliment so specific it actually lands.

"I noticed you stayed to help clean up even when everyone else left. That mattered."
