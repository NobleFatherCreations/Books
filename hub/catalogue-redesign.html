The through-line

Everything here has a surface — and something underneath it.

One half of this catalogue is the Library: field guides that take the same pattern of psychological manipulation and follow it up through every scale of life — a playground, a festival, a marriage, a sanctuary, an institution, an entire economy. The promise is simple. A pattern you can see is a pattern you can exit.

The other half is the Workshop: hand-poured pendants and hand-pressed wax seals with a near-field core sealed inside. They look like one thing in the open and become another the moment a phone touches them — a song, a voice, a tribute, a secret. Tap to reveal.

Two crafts, one instinct: what matters is usually the thing you can't see yet.
